# Hormozi Formulas, Thresholds & Verbatim Quotes

Formulas use the definitions as given in the cited videos. Every quote is copied exactly from a note's "Notable lines" section, and each was checked by script against `notes/<id>.md`. Where two videos give different thresholds, both are listed.

## Formulas

### Value Equation (5MHQr-Z17Hc, _qspvJAq34M)
`Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`
- Raise the top and cut the bottom toward zero. The biggest moats are on the bottom. (_qspvJAq34M)

### Two ways to grow (XwZH-lOKG9c, MwhsM7qijy8, f_ceDZqhKbw)
`Growth = more customers OR more value per customer`
- Four controllable levers: traffic, conversion, price, churn. (kloJJeiysxg)

### LTGP : CAC (QGcjweehrvU, Cr0KdqZ954c, i7bLRKwKSms, A_tx40lNpf8)
- `LTGP = (price − delivery cost) × purchases over the customer lifetime`. This is gross profit, not revenue.
- `CAC = fully loaded acquisition cost` (ads, sales comp, tools, people) ÷ new customers.
- Thresholds: at least 3:1 (Cr0KdqZ954c, QGcjweehrvU). Scale by humans in the loop: 3:1 with none, 6:1 with one, 9:1 with two, 12:1 with three. (A_tx40lNpf8, i7bLRKwKSms)
- uYds0zcAFWM adds that 3:1 only works if the business is fully leveraged.

### Client-financed acquisition (XwZH-lOKG9c, k-3PoOT4vOM, A_tx40lNpf8, nSQdjim8CsE)
- Minimum: `30-day cash collected ≥ CAC + COGS`. (A_tx40lNpf8, Cr0KdqZ954c)
- Target: `30-day gross profit > 2 × (CAC + COGS)`, so each customer funds the next two. (XwZH-lOKG9c, k-3PoOT4vOM)
- Payback goal is 30 days. Per A_tx40lNpf8, every one of his businesses that got really big recovered CAC in the first 30 days.

### Hypothetical maximum (XZEjp2mP1U0, f_ceDZqhKbw)
- `Max customers = new customers per period ÷ churn rate per period`
- `Max revenue = new sales × (price ÷ churn)`
- Worked example: 100 sales a month at $1K and 13% churn caps out at about $770K a month. At 3% churn the cap is about $3.3M. (f_ceDZqhKbw)

### Golden ratio: referral vs churn (IFpHasZ7jN4, XZEjp2mP1U0, qpQvdBFW_yI)
`Passive growth factor = referral % ÷ churn %`. Above 1 means you grow at rest. Only pour gas on it when it's above 1.

### Six LTV levers (Q2VUuUGfpNY, MwhsM7qijy8)
Raise price, cut cost of fulfillment, increase frequency, upsell, cross-sell, downsell. Price is the biggest: $20 → $30 on a $10 cost doubles gross profit per unit. (Q2VUuUGfpNY)

### Gross margin (9xAMe0QBFhU, A_tx40lNpf8, aOq1Hdz0w3k)
- Services need at least 80% gross margin.
- At a $100 cost: 70% margin means a $333 price, 80% means $500, 90% means $1,000.
- Each halving of delivery cost (80 → 90 → 95%) roughly doubles per-unit profit.

### Close-rate price ladder (A_tx40lNpf8, ZuJryiwxjDw)
- Closing ≥80% usually means you're underpriced by 3–4×.
- About 30–40% is right for a proper process.
- Low close rates mean fixing the process or the avatar before the price.

### Enterprise value (fD-sxKiB30M, HKbFUWJwEG0)
- `Value = profit × multiple`. The multiple reflects risk and reliability.
- HKbFUWJwEG0: `Enterprise value ≈ customers × lifetime gross profit ÷ risk` [paraphrase].
- Example: +$2M EBITDA at 5× is +$10M of untaxed net worth. (w5g0JiO7OdE)

### Price tiers (ZuJryiwxjDw, uWdIgftpvBI)
- The next tier is priced at 5–10× and expects roughly a 20% take rate (ZuJryiwxjDw).
- A capped premium tier with about 10% uptake can double revenue. (uWdIgftpvBI)

### Throughput (2tHlHWgDRdQ, RdAKXJlMIZM, gD0X-PLax5I)
`Output = Volume × Skill (leverage) × Time`

### Rule of 100 (A_tx40lNpf8, HsQeQM1jUeg)
100 qualifying acquisition actions a day for 100 days.

### Change cost (hfwZwPGsbIo, XwzU4RikbGs, uYds0zcAFWM)
Every change costs about 20% in performance. Only bet on ≥20% upside, and hfwZwPGsbIo says ≥40% upside at 50/50 odds.

### Wealth (wtsX7WHQMFM, Nh8Oc7ERdIU)
- `Wealth = (Build + Sell + Lead) × Risk` (wtsX7WHQMFM)
- `Leverage = output ÷ input` (Nh8Oc7ERdIU)

### Operating benchmarks
- Lead response under 60 seconds. (StVqS0jD7Ls, A_tx40lNpf8)
- Sales calendar 70–75% booked, never above 85%. (A_tx40lNpf8)
- About 70% show rate and about 40% close for a two-call mid-ticket sale. (c_6BrF7jOGk)
- Churn under 3% a month before you buy or scale. (U_s0ekwPK5g)
- B2B annual retention above 80%, measured on the original cohort. (A_tx40lNpf8)
- Paid-in-full discount of at least 10%. (A_tx40lNpf8)
- Concentration risk: one channel above 50% of leads, or one customer at or above 20% of revenue. (sjt5G3YPjmY)
- Give:ask ratio of at least 3:1. (5cOwh-8scu8)
