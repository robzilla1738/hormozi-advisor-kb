# Wealth Principle #1: Get Paid First

## Core thesis
"The Money Ladder": wealth and control track who gets paid when. Move your agreements up the ladder — get paid earlier and more in full, and be first in line when things go wrong.

## Frameworks & models
- **Money Ladder (bottom → top):**
  1. Employee — fronts time/labor, paid 2–4 weeks later.
  2. Self-employed (low) — same as employee: work first, paid later.
  3. Self-employed (better) — 50% upfront / 50% on delivery.
  4. Paid first — like a surgeon: paid before doing the work; sub-variable: partial upfront vs. paid in full (payment plans sit lower).
  5. Banks — preferred creditor in the capital stack: paid first when it goes bad; owner gets leftovers.
  6. Insurance — paid for years before (and possibly never) fulfilling; "reverse of a depreciating asset": collect capital upfront and let it appreciate. Oldest companies (~180–300 yrs).
  7. "God and franchisors" (joke) — 10% off the top for life.
- Moving up the ladder is driven by demand / how good you are.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Structure deals to collect more upfront, in full; when possible, take a preferred/first-paid position or top-line royalties.

## Examples / case details
- Hypothetical insurance business: 100–500 families paying $1–2k/month → ~$1M/month for decades.
- He says he changed how he structured agreements after adopting this view.

## Notable lines
- "Watch where the money flows, watch where it goes, and watch who gets paid when."
- Insurance "get all the money up front and then they get to appreciate the money over time."
