## Core thesis
When you don't know what to do, build capacity so you can both recognize and capitalize on opportunities. Opportunities come to everyone, and only people with capacity can act on them: "Don't be in the bleachers when the fat pitch comes." Six ways to build capacity: rest and health, money, stacked skills, an audience before a product, a waitlist, and network surface area. (Portfolio "north of $250M" last year.)

## Frameworks & models
- **Good Samaritan study** (Princeton seminary students): how they rated their own morality had zero correlation with helping a fallen person. Lateness predicted it, with roughly a 6× difference between 10-min-early and 10-min-late. No slack means you can't even see opportunities.
- **1. Capacity basics**: sleep on time, get in shape before you have dates, save before you know the investment.
- **2. Money**: "Money buys time and time buys optionality." Same cost cuts as elsewhere (no eating out, no clothes for 2 years, shared housing at $300–400/mo), plus use 5–9 AM and 5–9 PM.
- **3. Skill stacking**: spend all excess cash on skills. Skills are inflation-proof. Jay-Z: rhythm → rap → lyrics → sell → market → label → recruit artists. Finance: math → bookkeeping → accounting → taxes → insurance → M&A. Each skill multiplies the others.
- **4. Audience without a product**: attention is potential energy. With no proof yet, show "epic proof or epic effort" and document the work.
- **5. Waitlist before product**: people who pay with time now are more likely to pay with money later.
- **6. Network / luck surface area**: go out (gym, coffee shop) and move to the hub (finance = NYC, film = Hollywood, politics = DC, per Chamath).

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Learning is always additive. Even bad courses teach you what not to do.
- "The fastest way to change your life is to change the people around you."

## Playbook steps
1. Rest and get fit. 2. Cut costs and save. 3. Reclaim 8 off-hours/day. 4. Stack complementary skills. 5. Document your work publicly. 6. Build a waitlist. 7. Relocate to your field's hub.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Waiting for the opportunity before preparing, and getting lapped by the prepared.
- Doom-scrolling your off-hours.
