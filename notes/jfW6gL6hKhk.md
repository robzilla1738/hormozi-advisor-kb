# If I Wanted to Make My First $100K in 2026, I'd Do This

## Core thesis
He felt wealthiest at $100K in the bank, more than at a $46.2M exit, because that's when he stopped worrying about rent and could think long-term (Maslow). His six steps to bank the first $100K: cut all costs, reclaim time, pick a skill people already pay for, actually learn it through iterations, spend on tools/implementation/attempts, and don't inflate your lifestyle. Most self-made billionaires got there through big active income they could put at risk.

## Frameworks & models
- **Step 1, cut all costs**: no eating out; discount groceries only; no new clothes for 2 years; live with family or a saving roommate; a paid-off clunker. He split a bedroom in a 6-bedroom house at ~$300–400/mo.
- **Step 2, save time**: your 9-to-5 isn't killing your dreams. Use 5–9 AM and 5–9 PM. Focus comes from subtraction.
- **4-4-4 split** (if you don't have a day job): 4 hrs promotion first, 4 hrs delivery, 4 hrs building (curate opportunities, then prioritize the highest return).
- **Maker vs manager**: a maker's perfect day is an empty calendar; a manager's is 5-minute chunks. Block them separately, because task switching kills productivity. He keeps the first 4–6 hours of each day to himself; Mondays are his manager day.
- **Step 3, research a skill people already pay for**. B2B: the functions a business buys (content, outreach, funnels), each capable of a $1M+ business. B2C: read your own bank statement. Consumers buy time back; businesses buy money.
- **1-1-1 rule**: one product, one avatar, one channel until $1M.
- **Step 4, learning = same condition, new behavior**. It's 10,000 iterations, not 10,000 hours (feedback loops). Method: get volume → analyze the top 10% → find what differs from the other 90% → isolate the key details by testing one at a time → avoid the 90%'s mistakes → repeat in batches of 100.
- **Step 5, spend money in three buckets**: tools (CRM, landing pages, Skool at $9/mo), implementation help (courses, communities, one-on-one tutoring, the fastest way to learn), trial attempts (ad spend, editing software).
- **Step 6, don't increase your lifestyle.** "Be rich, not look rich." It's $100K in the bank, not in revenue.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Everything beyond food and shelter is profit to bank and reinvest.
- "The day that you stop spending money on learning is the day you decide that you do not want to make more."
- You're rewarded for the risk you take, and you can't take risk without time and money.

## Playbook steps
1. Cut costs. 2. Reclaim 5–9/5–9. 3. Separate maker and manager blocks. 4. Pick one paid-for skill (1-1-1). 5. Iterate: volume → top 10% → isolate → repeat. 6. Spend on tools, help and attempts. 7. Hold lifestyle flat and bank it.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Doom-scrolling your free hours.
- Consuming content without changing behavior.
- Lifestyle creep. He knows salespeople who made $40K/month for years and spent it all; friends who didn't start saving until their 40s.
- Interleaving deep work with Slack and texts.

## Examples / case details
- His first gym paid him ~$20K/mo personally, and he still paid $400 rent sharing a room to fund new locations and conferences.
- With $100K banked, he told Leila they could do nothing for 3.5 years.
- Pitch: "business backpack" of 3 donated books (~$16–17 shipping) plus a 30-day Skool trial.
