## Core thesis
This is a podcast guest spot on the Acquisitioncom channel; the host is a Welsh bodybuilder, "Branden" per the captions, and a long middle stretch is the host talking. Alex's blueprint: sell one thing to one customer on one channel to reach six figures, do it consistently to reach seven, then add a backend to raise LTV so you can afford more channels. For life and strategy decisions, define the problem, play it out, pick the problem you prefer, then find and relentlessly execute your input→output equation.

## Frameworks & models
- **Stages of growth**: 0→$100K = one product, one avatar, one channel. $100K→$1M = the same activities *consistently*. $1M→~$1M/month = add backend products to raise LTV, which funds more channels.
- **Business in 5 steps**: LLC (e.g., LegalZoom) → bank account → Stripe/PayPal → message people with your offer and get them on the phone → take the card, then deliver what you promised.
- **Three strategic-decision questions**: (1) What problem are we trying to solve? (2) Play it out ("okay, then what?"). (3) Which problem do we prefer?
- **Input→output equation**: know the primary repeatable actions that stack (e.g., 100 reach-outs/day, 5 pieces of content, 2 affiliate relationships/week).
- **Customer acquisition is finite**: warm outreach, cold outreach, ads, content, plus affiliates and referrals. "Those are the only ways."
- **Unreasonable equations**: "If I did this every single day for 10 years... would it be unreasonable that I won't be a millionaire?" If so, do that.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Don't diversify products, avatars or channels before six figures.
- Most people stall after the first sale because they stop doing the activities.
- If you don't know your input→output equation, that's the first problem to solve.
- Spend the first ~6 hours of the day on the core inputs (content, reach-outs, recruiting affiliates).
- Make room by removing time-wasters, not adding to your plate.

## Playbook steps
1. Define the problem (e.g., "make money" vs "be a bodybuilder"). 2. Play out each path. 3. Choose the preferred problem. 4. Set daily input targets on one channel. 5. Execute for years, ignoring doubters and your own doubts. 6. At ~$1M, add backend/LTV, then more channels.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Getting "cute" with many offers and channels too early.
- Choosing a path without playing it out (bodybuilding for money only pays at top-5 level; an influencer could out-earn without stepping on stage).
- Trying to "be me": "be you, not me, because you're not going to beat me at being me."
- Filling your day with low-value things. People further ahead "have no more time than you do."
