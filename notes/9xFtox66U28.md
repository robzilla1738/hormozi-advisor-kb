## Core thesis
Group coaching Q&A (Alex answering a member). Chase joy, which comes from within, rather than happiness, which reacts to circumstances. External achievement and wealth are temporary. Meaning comes from living by values: decide based on values rather than outcomes, so that belief, speech and action line up. Little direct business tactics.

## Frameworks & models
- **Happiness vs joy**: happiness is a reaction to outside circumstances; joy is how you choose to experience them. You can mourn and be joyful.
- **Casino of life**: everyone stacks chips, but you leave the table without cashing out, so it's "a fake game with fake money."
- **Shovel analogy**: achievements are shovels digging holes we fill back in. Pick the shovel you enjoy; for him that's learning through teaching.
- **Outcome-based vs value-based decisions**: values outlast you. Alignment = what you believe = what you say = what you do; misalignment = dissonance.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Cutting a small corner nobody notices (a rushed email campaign, ad or VSL) is living outside your values. Aim for "truly excellent," not "done."
- [inference] Ask "but why?" behind money goals.

## Playbook steps
1. Redefine the goal from happiness to joy. 2. Ask why you want the money. 3. Find what you inherently enjoy. 4. Make decisions based on values and close the gaps between belief, speech and action.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Rushing work just to check it off the to-do list.
- Bragging about hard work. He asks why working hard is even praised, citing China's six 12-hour days as the norm.

## Examples / case details
- His companies combined were doing "just under two million a week." He takes these calls for joy, citing members' wins (a chiropractor business, a first sale).
- Member had $1M cash while living on about $30K.

## Notable lines
