## Core thesis
Last century's fortunes were built on oil. This century's are built on attention, the new scarce resource. Alex had wanted to be "rich and unknown." Celebrity-brand outcomes (Kylie, Huda, McGregor's Proper 12, The Rock's Teremana) and Naval's four types of leverage changed his mind: his businesses had nothing that compounded. Media is a compounding form of leverage, and the audience it builds is the asset that compounds. The cost of getting attention only rises, so the best time to start is now.

## Frameworks & models
- **Attention is the new oil:** the total pie is every human's eyes and ears, 24 hours a day. Platforms own the "real estate" of that attention.
- **What holds entrepreneurs back:** skills, beliefs and character traits. His blocker was a false belief about how the world works, and you make decisions from what you believe.
- **Four types of leverage** (Naval's *Almanack*): labor, capital, code, media. Leverage is the difference between what you put in and what you get out, and compounding is itself leverage. Code and media take a one-time input and offer unlimited output.
- **Content is the input; the audience is the output,** and the audience compounds. Each short or post is worth making if it reaches one more person.
- **Monetizing attention:** (1) build for a long time before monetizing and give without asking ("the longer the runway, the bigger the plane"); (2) monetize something on brand (The Rock with Under Armour because fitness is his brand).
- **Endorsement and status transfer:** brands can't be victorious or high-status on their own, so they borrow it from people (Kim with Dolce & Gabbana, LeBron with Nike). Buyers trade money for that association.
- **Cross-pollination:** going deep on many dimensions (Rogan with UFC, comedy, fitness, UFOs) compounds an audience faster.
- **Capture, hold, multiply attention:** the three abilities of the next generation of fortunes.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Choose the leverage type that fits your skills. Alex judged he'd be better at media than code.
- Expect a very long horizon: The Rock built for about 20 years before Under Armour and Teremana. Don't complain after posting for 4 weeks.
- Media, ads and content creation get more expensive and more fragmented over time, so start now.
- If you say your mission is X (for him, making real business education available to everyone), your actions must match it, or "they're just empty words."

## Playbook steps
1. Audit your business for something that compounds. 2. Pick a leverage type (media, for most). 3. Make content steadily, give without asking, stay on brand. 4. Go deep across several interests to cross-pollinate. 5. Monetize later with an offer that fits the brand.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Ego-protective excuses about others' success (e.g., "Kris Jenner's her mom").
- Treating content as wasted time because it disappears. The audience is what lasts.
- Avoiding fame because of fear of weirdos. Dean Graziosi (captioned "graciosi"): weird letters are "the price I have to pay for the impact."
- Monetizing too early or off brand.
