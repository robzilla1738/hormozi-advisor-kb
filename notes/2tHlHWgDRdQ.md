## Core thesis
Most people fail at lead gen not because the channel doesn't work but because their volume is a fraction of what's needed. Throughput = Volume × Skill × Time, multiplicative and self-reinforcing, and it all starts with doing. Beginners (low skill, no compounding time) must compensate with extraordinary volume. "Most people dramatically underestimate how much work it takes to make money."

## Frameworks & models
- **V × S × T throughput equation**: volume, skill, time feed each other (more you do → better you get → more you do; longer you do → better → longer). Multiplicative, not additive.
- **Only four activities generate new business**: reach out to people you know; post to an audience; one-on-one outreach to strangers; advertising. (The Core Four.)
- **Community sayings**: "do the boring work"; "violence is the answer" (sheer activity beats pretended nuance); "do more than you're doing now and you'll get more than you have"; "do what matters most first."
- **Effort nonlinearity**: going from 8/10 to 10/10 effort might be ~5× the work but ~100× the outcome.
- **Quality and quantity** beats either alone.
- **Redoing work raises S**: even at same volume, the redo increases throughput; "your work works on you more than you work on it."

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Before concluding a channel "doesn't work," check test size against practitioners' volumes.
- New channel / no first customer yet: do far more volume up front to build skill and start compounding.
- Make one of the four lead activities your primary daily activity, done first.

## Playbook steps
1. Pick one of the four core activities. 2. Benchmark the volume real winners use. 3. Match or exceed it daily. 4. Let reps raise skill; keep going long enough for compounding. 5. Redo work that isn't at 10/10.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Tiny tests (300 flyers) then quitting the channel.
- Blaming the vehicle "when the reality was that you were not working."
- Settling for 7–8/10 effort and comparing yourself to 10/10 producers.
- Avoiding tedious work ($100/day prospecting, repurposing, 100 ad variations, referral asks).

## Examples / case details
- Flyers: Alex tested 300; a multi-location friend won't test under 5,000, runs 100,000/month at 0.5–1% response → ~30 walk-ins/day.
- Arms: 6 sets/week vs. suggested 12 sets × 6 days.
- Agency owner making 5–10 creatives/week vs. Alex's team testing 30 pieces every 3 days.
- Grant Cardone: "it's about 10x" — 6 Instagram posts/day, 15 tweets/day, omnipresence.
- Alex went from posting 1×/week to 1×/day (7× effort) and organic growth went ~40×.
- A top-performing video took 20 hours of slide prep plus 2 days travel.
- Leila re-recorded ~70 portal videos she judged not crisp enough.
- Book: ~2 weeks to write, ~a year to edit; book two on 5th full version.
