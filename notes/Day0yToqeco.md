# Why Looking Poor is actually Smart
id: Day0yToqeco / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Day0yToqeco / duration: 868s (~14 min) / tags: 10-money-cashflow, 12-mindset-leverage, 01-what-business-to-start
Signal: medium

## Core thesis
Early on he lived on ~$1,500/mo while taking home roughly $15–20K/mo (caption garbled: "$15$2,000"; the later "making 20 grand a month" supports ~$20K), so he could go on the offense later. A low cost of living raises your risk tolerance and makes you "dangerous." Spend the spread on education and getting around the right people, invest in relationships by over-delivering favors, and gamify savings by checking your bank balance daily.

## Frameworks & models
- **Three cost centers**: food, transport, housing.
  - **Food ("Mosy Meals")**: Chipotle twice a day (~$15–20/day ≈ $600/mo), Panda Express with extra chicken, Chick-fil-A nuggets (~$10–12). He says this beat ~$600/mo on groceries plus 8 hours of Sunday prep. Cheapest floor: bread, peanut butter, a multivitamin, (Lactaid) milk.
  - **Transport**: a 5–10-year-old used 4-door car bought with ~$10K cash. No payments, cheaper insurance.
  - **Housing**: slept on his gym floor (rent $5K/mo for the business), then in a member's place, then half a bedroom in a 7-bedroom Costa Mesa house with dogs everywhere at $400/mo.
- **Move to where things happen**: an extra $200–400/mo to be near ambitious people is worth it. Wealth shows in the time and money increments people talk in (weeks and paychecks vs years and $100K investments). "Handshakes instead of hacks": his big deals were one or two degrees from people he met.
- **Favors: blow them away**. The "free patio" problem: a half-done free favor breeds resentment. Do a full-scope job so they feel awkward. Not needing anything back is the ultimate leverage. Stack IOUs, "dig the well before you're thirsty."
- **Spending spread**: compare living on $10K and earning $10K with living on $1.5K and spending $2–5K/mo of the spread on education. The second person is "hard to be stopped."
- **Daily bank-balance check plus a Google Sheet log**: awareness drives change (like weighing yourself daily, or sales leaderboards lifting close rates). Chase personal records in your balance. "Money sticks to the person who pays it the most attention."

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Making under ~$60–70K/yr? Drive Uber (4-door car, ≤15 years old, no visible damage). His plan B was "strip at night, drive Uber during the day."
- Don't turn down the first invite. Prep 30 minutes on the person and bring value.
- Budget: put the month's cash in your wallet (Ramsey). Skip credit-card point hacking and just be disciplined.
- No fixed income-to-spend ratio: minimize spend, maximize income, save everything except food, transport and housing.

## Playbook steps
1. Cut to food, car and room. 2. Move near ambitious people or join online pockets. 3. Accept first invites; over-deliver favors. 4. Spend the spread on skills. 5. Log your bank balance every morning for at least 90 days.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Car payments, bottle service, $100–200 nights out: "You're paying for the car, but you're sacrificing your dreams."
- Avoiding looking at your money out of anxiety.
- Half-done free favors.

## Examples / case details
- Moved SoCal → Puerto Rico → Irvine → Albuquerque purely to be around people to learn from.
- Portfolio now "over $200 million a year."
