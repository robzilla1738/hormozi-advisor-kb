# The anti-routine...[what billionaires use to win]
id: lRyGIzW9d9k / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRyGIzW9d9k / duration: 669s (~11 min) / tags: 12-mindset-leverage, 08-ops-scaling
Signal: medium

## Core thesis
Success comes from what you STOP doing, not from adding a "million-dollar routine." Enter a temporary "season of no": say no to everything that isn't the one priority so all that remains is the work to be done. He credits this anti-routine for his ~18-month run from looking up bankruptcy lawyers to taking home >$1M/month at ~27.

## Frameworks & models
- **Anti-routine**: champions usually lack something you have (e.g., an off switch), rather than having something you lack.
- **Priorities rule**: two things cannot be equally important. Splitting 30 min / 30 min means both are "important", so neither is. A priority means you do nothing else.
- **Communication = implied ask**: every message carries a request to reply. 500,000-texts thought experiment: having time to reply ≠ being obligated to reply; the obligation never existed.
- **Information vs execution**: if you don't know what to do, go get information. Once you know, anything other than doing it is in the way.
- **Minimum effective dose**: allocate limited "growth juju" to the top priority and only maintain everything else.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- No meetings 4:00am–noon; first ~8 hours uninterrupted, on the priority only.
- Same bedtime/wake time 7 days a week; treat weekends like weekdays.
- Workouts ~2×/week (maintenance only).
- Fast 1–2 days/week (caffeine + bone broth) to remove food prep/eating interruptions.
- Until you have team leverage ($0–1M, $1–3M, sometimes $5M), leverage your own time; his current routine (20 people executing) doesn't apply.
- Go to a mastermind, get what you need, then go execute instead of returning for the social payoff.

## Playbook steps
1. Pick the single priority; say no to everything else (for a season, not forever).
2. Change your phone number; give the old one to an EA. You reach out only to the people you choose.
3. Stop attending events/masterminds/coaching that mostly meet social needs.
4. Block the morning (4am–noon) with no meetings.
5. Lock the sleep schedule, minimum-dose workouts, fast 1–2 days/week.
6. Delete social apps from your phone; post from the computer at a scheduled time without consuming.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Copying billionaire routines and adding more things.
- Saying everything is important / being unable to say no.
- Conflating having time with being obliged to reply. Half his day was one-off "can I bend your ear" calls.
- Seeking more information when you need execution.
- 3–5 hrs/day on social media ("the new age of watching television").
- Trying to progress on 100 fronts at once.

## Examples / case details
