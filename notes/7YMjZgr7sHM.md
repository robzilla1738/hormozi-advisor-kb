## Core thesis
For big irreversible decisions, control the environment you decide in, because all decisions are emotional. If your answer flips day to day on unchanged data, that's a red flag. Decide rested, fed, away from your usual space, and from a place of needing nothing.

## Frameworks & models
- **Rest → Eat → Separate → Need nothing** (4-step decision environment).
  1. Exceptional sleep ("woke up like a newborn").
  2. Well fed — food raises stress tolerance (stress = cortisol response, physical or emotional).
  3. Physical separation from day-to-day spaces (anchors from past emotions/decisions).
  4. Operate from abundance = having enough, not needing the outcome.
- **Two biases:** confirmation bias (emotion picks the answer, logic gathers supporting data); conviction bias (the more you want the outcome, the more you blind yourself — doesn't change real odds).
- **Inversion (Munger):** how would I make a terrible decision? Tired, hungry, rushed, anchored in stress, needing the thing — reverse all.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Use this process only for irreversible decisions with downstream consequences (who to marry, where to live, what to work on, big strategic business changes).
- If your decision changes while the information doesn't → don't decide yet.
- "Mistakes love a rushed decision."
