# 4 Perspective Shifts That Keep Me Going

## Core thesis
Longevity is the #1 driver of success because quitting is the only way to fail, and entrepreneurs are judged by the quality of their decisions. So manage your emotional state. Four perspective tools keep stress from distorting decisions.

## Frameworks & models
1. **1,000x negative / repetition:** for minor to medium grievances (bad review, upset client, key employee leaves, competitor copies you), imagine it happening every hour of every day until you're desensitized, then "fast-forward" that immunity to now. Still fix core issues.
2. **Emotional bracing:** before a needed habit (e.g. workouts), expect the worst state (exhausted, slept badly, stressed) and pre-decide to act anyway. Anything better is a bonus. (Same reason you tell someone to sit down before bad news.)
3. **Pain anchor / worst-case visualization:** soak in the worst imaginable loss (death of a spouse, parent, child) so current problems shrink. Most problems never get solved, they shrink into irrelevance. Unhappiness comes from "should" (expectation ≠ reality).
4. **Fine-mist universe:** over a long enough horizon everything disappears, so nothing has inherent meaning and you assign it. Choose only games you find meaningful (his: learning, and teaching to learn) so you won't quit.

## Decision rules
- Stress scales with company size (10x revenue ≈ 10x stress potential, per him).
