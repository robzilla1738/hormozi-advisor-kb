# I spent $2,000,000 and it didn't make me happy..
id: RzswhUHnTHA / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzswhUHnTHA / duration: 763s (~13 min) / tags: 12-mindset-leverage, 10-money-cashflow
Signal: low

## Core thesis
He donated about $2M (250K fitness trackers to kids in underprivileged cities plus $1.2M for staffing at After-School All-Stars) hoping to feel better, and didn't. Demanding that work or life "be meaningful" creates a deficit between where you are and where you think you should be. Giving himself permission not to find meaning, and instead acting in line with chosen values, freed his attention and reduced anxiety. A personal philosophy video, not tactics.

## Frameworks & models
- **Equation of deficiency**: any "must" (happiness, meaning, fulfillment) creates a gap that guarantees dissatisfaction. Earlier mantra: "F*** happiness, just get stuff done," which brought more joy. Later he rebuilt the same trap with "meaning."
- **Dr. John Berardi** (caption reads "Bardi"; sold a majority stake for nine figures): "consume content from people outside the matrix" (those not operating from scarcity). "Why do you think work needs to be meaningful?"
- **Values alignment over meaning-seeking**: judge each action (meeting, decision, piece of work) by whether it fits your values. The channel, books and Acquisition.com are framed that way.
- **Wealth without the skill to acquire it ruins people**: Magic: The Gathering card Burning Wish, "She wished for a weapon, but not for the skill to wield it." Money is raw power.
- **Legacy is a myth (his view)**: genes halve each generation, and heirs won't remember you. He and Leila signed wills leaving the fortune to donation. Even with kids, he says he wouldn't hand it to them.
- Orson Scott Card quote (opening of Gym Launch Secrets): "We question all of our beliefs except for those that we truly believe."

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Don't give unearned wealth to heirs who lack the skill to generate or wield it. [inference: applies to succession and inheritance decisions]
- Seek perspectives from people outside the scarcity game.

## Playbook steps
1. Notice hidden "shoulds" (must be meaningful or happy). 2. Drop the demand. 3. Define your values. 4. Do the work at hand well according to those values.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Expecting philanthropy or success to deliver feelings.
- Turning happiness or meaning into a target you constantly audit.
