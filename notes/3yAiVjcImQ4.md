## Core thesis
Case study: an attorney (music/IP law) lost about $100K/yr on side projects (software, courses) while his services were the cash cow. The fixes: kill the vampire and focus 1-1-1; turn the education into free content that feeds the services; capture traffic he was already getting with CTAs and proactive DMs; switch from hourly billing to outcome pricing ($5,800 + commission, with a $25K anchor and a $2–3K DIY downsell); install a repeatable sales process (VSL, BANT, CLOSER script). Includes ads for the $100M Money Models launch event (Aug 16).

## Frameworks & models
- **1-1-1 rule**: one product, one avatar, one channel until $1M/yr.
- **"Help everyone" problem / kill the vampire**: unprofitable "give back" projects drain time from the cash cow. "You're not going to help anybody if you're broke."
- **Avatar pricing**: A clients may spend 10× C clients. Averaging A/B/C and charging 2× sacrifices margin; there are plenty of A's, so serve them first (the ICP).
- **Lubricate existing behavior** instead of building a new funnel.
- **Value-based vs hourly pricing**: hourly rates get compared and make you a commodity. Price relative to the dream outcome.
- **Flat fee + performance**: a commission signals belief in the outcome. Pure commission is too risky when clients may not succeed.
- **A/B offer** (from Money Models): offer choices such as chocolate or vanilla. Here B = $25K with zero commission, an anchor that makes $5,800 look like a steal.
- **Anchors on both sides**: $25K above, DIY $2–3K below. At ~$3K DIY, paying ~$2,800 more for done-for-you feels obvious.
- **BANT** (IBM, 1960s): Budget, Authority, Need, Timing. Use a timing dropdown (today/tomorrow/few weeks) and sell to "today/tomorrow."
- **+ − − + persuasion**: with me, more good and less bad; without me, more bad and less good.
- **CLOSER**: Clarify, Label, Overview past, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce.
- **Scaling roadmap**: this was Stage 4 "Prioritize." The product constraint came from saying yes to anyone, so specialize and price a v2 for the niche avatar. Marketing: better free stuff, more creative, plus qualification and friction so volume self-selects. "Businesses behave in patterns."

## Decision rules & thresholds
- A very high close rate means you're underpriced.
- Put the CTA at ~30% into each YouTube video, and in the description, pinned comment and profile.
- Don't charge for the first call as a "consultation." Qualify, show a VSL, then sell the whole thing up front.
- Keep the VSL to 5–7 minutes: who/what/where/when/why plus FAQs, using the exact questions prospects ask on calls.
- Use a DIY downsell mainly when there are lots of unqualified leads.

## Playbook steps
1. Cut unprofitable products ("kill the vampire"). 2. Reposition paid education as free content that ascends people into services. 3. Map where leads actually come from and add CTAs along that path. 4. Proactively DM new followers: "Here for free stuff or do you want help with your legal stuff?" 5. Move to outcome pricing with a performance kicker and A/B anchor offer. 6. Qualify with BANT, show the VSL, run a CLOSER script with +−−+. 7. Add a DIY downsell. 8. Record calls and keep improving the VSL and script.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Spreading time across unprofitable projects and feeling justified ("doing the Lord's work").
- Hoping to sell mediocre side software for a 10× multiple.
- Not knowing where leads come from, so you optimize the wrong thing.
- Hourly billing for a result-driven service.
- Ad hoc sales, treating calls like paid consultations.
