## Core thesis
Consistent monthly sales come from five C's: a **CLOSER** conversation framework written only as questions, **Call recordings**, a **Comms** cadence, **Cut** the bottom performers, and **Competition**. Draws on closing 4,000+ people one-on-one in weight loss and on outsourced sales for gyms.

## Frameworks & models
- **CLOSER** (script buckets, *questions only*, never paragraphs, so the rep can pause and needs "no intelligence," which makes it scalable):
  - **C**larify why they're there: what made you get on, your goal, why it's important, what 12 months from now looks like.
  - **L**abel the problem: "what I'm hearing is… the missing link has been X. Is that right?"
  - **O**verview past pain: what have you tried, why didn't it work. Loop through the "pain cycle" until nothing is left, then recap.
  - **S**ell the vacation: three pillars, each with a ~30-second illustrative story (weight loss: fitness, nutrition, accountability, e.g. the brushing-teeth story; leads: exclusive, timely, geographic).
  - **E**xplain away concerns: only three objections exist: price (a value discrepancy), stall, and decision-maker (use implied past agreements: the partner knows you're struggling and wants a fix). Drill with flashcards. "Crazy people buy without obstacles. Normal people have concerns."
  - **R**einforce the decision: a 30-second personalized founder video, a handwritten card, a t-shirt, to prevent backing out.
- **Decision process walk-through**: like me? like the product? think we can get you the outcome? have access to the money or know someone who does? → "What card do you want to use?" If not, "What's your main concern?" → overcome → close.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Record every call (for compliance and accountability). He recommends Gong for Zoom sales.
- Comms cadence: daily huddles to share testimonials ("they get beat up every single day with nos"), weekly 1:1s, monthly reviews.
- In 1:1s the rep tags their best, worst and average call. Review where they clarified, outlined pain, asked for the sale, and overcame objections. "You can never make a sale if you never ask for it."
- Cut the bottom 10% regularly, even when the team is fine. He says each cut gives about a 30% productivity jump.
- Leaderboard published and updated daily, plus a bell thread for every sale.
- Competitions: 6 weeks works best, with three-person teams (sharing best practices, less cutthroat). Prize about 25% of one month's pay per person (e.g. a rep on $70K/yr gets about a $1,500 trip; about $5K for a team).

## Playbook steps
1. Write the CLOSER script as questions. 2. Write 3 pillar stories. 3. Drill the 3 objection types. 4. Record all calls. 5. Daily huddle, weekly 1:1 call review, monthly review. 6. Cut the bottom 10% on a schedule. 7. Leaderboard plus bell. 8. 6-week team competitions.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Statements or paragraphs in scripts, which reps end up reading.
- Not recording calls.
- Never asking for the sale.
- Inconsistent lead-quality, CAC and capacity swings (the problem this system targets).
