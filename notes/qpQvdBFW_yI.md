# 7 Ways To Get Customers for Free

## Core thesis
Referrals close at higher rates, pay more, buy more often and refer more. Across his businesses, asking for referrals systematically at specific moments brings in about 30% more customers at any scale.
## Frameworks & models
- **Referral vs churn = at-rest growth rate:** if 10% of customers refer each month against 10% churn, you hold steady; 20% referral on 10% churn grows 2:1 no matter how big you get.
- **"Who else do you know…" ask:** open-ended, not yes/no. A record-breaking rep got 30–50% more sales from asking after every close; about 1 in 3 gave 1–2 names.
- **The 7 plays:**
  1. *Gift card play:* holiday or made-up occasion. Card value = one base unit of service (e.g. $200, about a month at a gym; $2,000 for an SEO agency). Sell it to existing customers at 90%+ off ($20), limit 2 per customer, only for giving to others, 30-day expiry. Making the card out to a named person captures the lead, so you get paid for leads. Then apply the card to a premium package (a $200 card against a $2,000 package).
  2. *Discount → referral trade:* when asked for a discount, say "sure, give me the name of three friends," with introductions made now. Max discount ≈ 1x CAC (assume 1 of 3 converts). He never discounts without a change in terms.
  3. *Bonus instead of discount:* offer $400 of service (costing about $80) instead of $200 off. Higher perceived value at lower hard cost, and more usage.
  4. *"Want to know how to get this for free?"* right after the sale: $100 cash per referred person who buys (on a $500 product). This drove about 30% of promo sales; one person referred 10. Referral names were listed under the contract.
  5. *Referral at success moment:* at the transformation moment, take a selfie on their phone and text the intro from their phone (needs rapport).
  6. *Handwritten card + event:* at least a quarterly customer appreciation event. A handwritten invite with a PS saying they can text a photo of the card to a friend to get in, plus a three-way text. 30–50% of the room were plus-ones. Set appointments at the event, don't sell there, and book them within days.
  7. *Spouse request:* proactively raise the spouse obstacle and pitch a spouse program with a success stat (e.g. "3x more successful"). Never say "mandatory."
  - Bonus: a friend's online challenge repeated "bring a friend" in every ad and post, and 25–30% of customers came from word of mouth (almost none without it).
## Decision rules & thresholds
- Referral discount ceiling ≈ normal CAC.
- Gift cards: 90%+ discount, 2 per customer, 30-day expiry.
- Events: at least quarterly; book follow-ups fast while interest is high.
## Mistakes he warns against
Reps skip the ask so they don't "scare the sale away." Yes/no asks ("do you know anybody?"). Booking follow-ups too far out. Saying "it won't work for my business" ("the voice of the loser").
## Notable lines
- "Everybody want to get customers but ain't nobody want to ask for referrals."
- "Everybody believes in positive word of mouth, they don't believe in negative word of mouth."
