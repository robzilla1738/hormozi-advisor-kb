## Core thesis
Live teardown of Anie's eyelash-salon chain (Amy Lash & Beauty: 7 locations, $2.3M revenue, ~$644K profit, 28% net, 10 years, 20K+ clients). The biggest constraint isn't tactics but a messy multi-partner structure created by rushing expansion. Fix the partnerships, then capture easy profit: weekend surge pricing paired with a membership, "BAMFAM" rebooking, a default-close membership upsell with prepay, a staff career ladder, check-in attribution, and a dedicated speed-to-lead caller. Guest is Anie (business owner); host is Alex.

## Frameworks & models
- **BAMFAM — book a meeting from a meeting** (credited to Shirro Savata): book the next appointment while they're in front of you. Rebooking "walks and talks like an administrative conversation" but is a sales conversation.
- **Surge pricing via supply & demand**: price up on peak days (Thu–Sat). Frame it as peak prices plus a weekday discount; it smooths demand and boosts utilization.
- **Create a problem, then solve it**: weekend surge pricing + membership benefit "no surge pricing" makes the membership compelling.
- **Front-end promo = lead magnet**: the $99 intro set is pre-closed; the real business is the step-one-to-step-two upsell take rate ($99 → $170).
- **Default/assumed close**: frame the membership as "you've already been coming 6 times in 6 months — you should already be getting these perks, let's formalize it." Opt-out framing (organ-donor study he recalls from a "50 scientific ways to get to yes" book). New-sale upsells ~20–40%; default framing aims for 80–90%.
- **Three-step upsell ladder**: rebook (small win) → free offer (e.g., facial) → membership; then at checkout, prepay: "Do you want today to be free?" (12-month, downsell to 6-month).
- **Why prepay**: lower churn, pulls cash forward, locks out competitors.
- **Bonuses over discounts**: add value instead of cutting price; each bonus ideally worth the price on its own; "magic is in the middle" (not zero, not 100 bonuses). $100M Offers bonus chapter, p.117.
- **Referral sweepstakes**: monthly $200–$500 giveaway; if the winner was referred, the referrer wins too.
- **Career path ladder**: comp/title bump every ~3 months over ~2 years (e.g., 40→42.5→45→47.5→50%), each level unlocking a new chargeable skill; top performer earns a new location with profit share (10% yr1, 20% yr2) — profit share, not equity.
- **Four components of equity**: risk, control, profit, sale proceeds — give profit share (bonus) instead of ownership.
- **Service-business arbitrage**: hire low skill, train on your system, charge what the new skill commands; "you're in the recruiting, hiring, and training business."
- **Waiting-area checklist ("before" process)**: ID → card on file → sign in with "where did you hear about us?" dropdown + referral code → rebook → membership offer → reviews.
- **ICE prioritization** (impact, confidence, ease); he splits ease into speed + effort, mapping to the Value Equation. "Strategy is prioritization of resources."
- **Partnership exit options**: A) pay market rate for the role you do; B) shotgun offer (name a price you'd both buy and sell at, with terms); C) equity swap across co-owned locations (in-kind, clean, no tax per him). He recommends C.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Pay yourself market rate for any operating role; otherwise margins are artificially inflated and you can't backfill to scale. Example: $300K profit/$50K to open looks great, but minus a $200K manager it's $100K.
- "The fastest way to 5 locations isn't the fastest way to 50." Don't open before you have cash and people.
- Don't partner just to fill a role your network can't fill; if the future isn't 100 partners, don't start today.
- Marketing at ~0.5% of revenue ($12K/yr on $2.3M) with half of clients citing Google → probably underspending.
- Sell before the service (once they've got the value they want to leave).
- Price changes: you must be sold on them; he accepts 10% instead of 20%.
- Hire a ~$40K/yr person only to call leads within 1 minute; he calls it a likely 2–3× and ranks it #0.

## Playbook steps
0. Hire a speed-to-lead caller (call within 1 minute, get card on file: "It's how we always do it"). 1. Weekend surge pricing (+10–20%) paired with membership "no surge". 2. Membership offer with bonuses (facials, product discounts, referral bump $10→$50, birthday/anniversary gifts, sweepstakes). 3. Before process at check-in (ID, card, attribution, rebook, membership upsell, reviews). 4. After process at checkout (prepay 12/6 months: "today free"). 5. Career path for staff. 6. Daily 30-minute role-play training with lots of repetition. 7. Resolve partnerships (equity swap; buy out the disengaged partner in exchange for helping replace herself).

## Mistakes he warns against
- Rushing expansion with unvetted partners; leaving partnership issues "in the gray"; avoiding hard conversations.
- Giving ownership (33%) where a profit-share bonus would do.
- Letting customers return "whenever."
- Discount-only memberships.
- Training by handing over a script instead of role-play repetition.
- Tracking 100% close rate on pre-paid promos instead of upsell take rate.
