## Core thesis
Live Q&A hot-seat: businesses from $600K to $10M/yr ask about starting or scaling paid ads. Repeated answers: lead with the prospect's pain, let proof (not promise) do the talking, add global retargeting and own-brand PPC first, tune funnel friction to lead quality, squeeze winners with many variations, raise LTV through bundles, and spend more when ROAS is strong and capacity exists. Group funnels (free community as lead magnet) are flagged as currently cutting call costs sharply.

## Frameworks & models
- **Good vs bad friction**: filters raise lead cost; good filters (e.g. a VSL) raise quality too, bad ones (slow page) only raise cost.
- **Perfect-funnel rule**: too many bad leads → add friction; too few leads → remove friction; almost no friction and still bad leads → targeting is off.
- **Shadow funnel / layer one**: install pixels on every platform and run global retargeting (GDN, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube) on all site visitors; "almost always" positive ROAS. Also bid on your own brand PPC terms — he's never seen that lose money.
- **Proof continuum**: least compelling = faceless, anonymous, old, text testimonial; most = someone who looks like you, live, in person, with the exact result you want. In-person > virtual, live > recorded, like-me > not-like-me, exact result > close result.
- **Promise vs proof**: "your promise is not unique, your proof is." Differentiate on mechanism + believability (proof).
- **ROAS levers**: spend more; lower CAC; raise LTV/average cart value.
- **Creative reuse**: take top 5–10% of past ads, model/splice their first 3 seconds onto other ads (10–50× more creative per winner). ~80% of their resources go to reskinning winners (the "Kale ad": ~100 versions — B&W, sepia, new hooks, headlines, fonts, copy).
- **70/20/10 creative allocation**: 70% variations of the main winner, 20% adjacent ideas, 10% wild ideas; do the 70 first.
- **Group funnel — 4 conversion mechanisms**: (1) free onboarding call for every joiner; (2) 5-video series = broken-up VSL (opportunity/story, 3 belief-breakers — typically vehicle, others, self — then stack/CTA); (3) weekly value webinar with soft CTA, setters DM chatters live; (4) setters DM anyone who likes/comments/posts.
- **Bundle the consumables**: buyer of a device believes they'll use it, so sell a year of refills (printer + year of ink logic).

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Local businesses are the easiest to advertise (high trust, offer-driven, less brand needed). Acupuncture: start with a ~$19–29 front-end offer, call every lead, get card on file, prefer selling the plan before treatment.
- Start on Meta for B2B/older audiences; use a lookalike of your customer list (email list >1,000). Putting "dentist" in copy/video helps the algorithm find dentists.
- ~80% of their ads over the years are about proof; founder needn't be in ads.
- If ROAS is high (7:1, 20:1, 10:1) and you have capacity → spend more; dial spend to fill rate.
- A waitlist only counts if people pay to be on it; keep only ~3–4 paid waitlisters.
- Compliance-heavy ads (health/wealth): hire a compliance-audit firm (~$5–6K, ask for 90%+ post-audit approval rate).
- Call all opt-ins regardless of booking; with a booked call, call them anyway now to confirm/qualify (set, then close on the appointment).
- Group funnel benchmarks: good operators convert ~10% of group joiners; get ~1/3 on calls, close 30–50%. Seen group funnels take call cost from ~$250 to ~$40.
- Bundle example: if 30% take a ~$40 refill add-on, adds ~$12 to average cart (~15% on an $80 item).

## Playbook steps
1. Pixel + global retargeting on all platforms; own brand PPC terms.
2. Ad leads with the avatar's pain; free high-value lead magnet → thank-you page → scheduler (add application if quality is low).
3. Call every lead; two-call close (set, then close).
4. Use case studies/representative examples + FAQ objection handling in the lead magnet.
5. Find winners, make many variations (70/20/10), splice best hooks.
6. Raise LTV with bundled annual consumables and payment plans.
7. Long-term: make content + more proof; consider a group funnel.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Skimping on the free lead magnet.
- Making it about you; "no one cares" — customers want to see people like them.
- Abandoning winners too early instead of "use and abuse it until it doesn't work at all."
- Adding friction that doesn't improve quality.
- Unpaid "waitlists."
- Selling one-off small add-ons instead of annual bundles.

## Examples / case details
- Dental recruiting: already runs job-board ads → same skill on Meta; pains: weekends, retirement, not wanting PE "servitude."
- Vacation rentals B2B: pain = empty second home; send ~5–10 representative home case studies + FAQ (trashing, security, cleaning) + personalized estimate.
- Beauty DTC ($2M, 4.6 ROAS): "you are a marketing company"; to hit $20M need 10–50× more creative via UGC capture/repurposing.
- VA business (70 clients): offer showing ~$25K/yr labor per role replaced for $500/mo (~$6K/yr); guarantee 5× by day 90 or rest of year free. [inference: caption says "$199,000 back"; the stated math gives ~$19K spread.]
- His gym company: Alex not in ads for the last two years; customer proof instead.
- Assisted living (20:1, 8 spots open): spend more, then raise money to open more locations.
- Executive health ($10M, 10:1 on $1K/mo YouTube): blocked by compliance → audit firm.
- Roofing ($30K/mo PPC, 7:1): retargeting first, then Meta (interruption vs intent → lower quality but older audience fine), free audit, two-call close.
