## Core thesis
This is not a business video. It's a personal-nutrition video, billed as a "part two" after commenters asked for one: 12 "zero prep," high-protein, low-carb, low-fat recipes, mostly aiming for about 100 g of protein per meal. Its business-decision value is nil. The tag is kept only as a loose personal-systems / leverage fit [inference]. It's useful only for questions about Alex's personal routines.

## Frameworks & models
- **Protein purity check**: compare any food to a scoop of whey (about 120 cal → about 24 g protein) to judge how "pure" a protein source it is.
- **Protein all day, carbs on purpose**: he eats protein through the day and only eats carbs deliberately.
- **Minimize prep and decisions**: eat the same meal for years (one lunch for about 2 years), prep ground meat once a week, eat from the container to avoid dishes, use a speed toaster ("every minute is precious to me").

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Target about 100 g of protein per meal; he says that's his usual goal.
- Weigh calorie-dense sauces (mayo) on a scale instead of using spoons. He doesn't count calories for hot sauce, salsa or mustard.

## Playbook steps
The recipes, each with the approximate protein figures he states:
1. Fage 0% yogurt + Crystal Light + Fruity Pebbles layered on top (about 94 g protein; a Cinnamon Toast Crunch/vanilla variant).
2. Ezekiel bread + fat-free cream cheese + lox as open-faced sandwiches (3 × about 30 g = about 90 g).
3. Jerky (60 g / 480 cal) + 4 light string cheese (24 g / 200 cal) = 84 g / 680 cal.
4. Precooked shrimp + cocktail sauce.
5. Lean ground beef + riced cauliflower + light mayo/sriracha/stevia sauce (about 140 cal of sauce per pound of meat; he dips the utensil instead of mixing the sauce in).
6. Carton egg whites (100 g, 0 fat/carb) + sugar-free Hershey syrup, drunk. Microwave version with salsa.
7. Premier Protein cereal (3 cups = 60 g) + a Premier shake = about 90 g / 700 cal.
8. Canned tuna in water (66 g) + 3/4 cup Fage + mustard = about 84 g, under 400 cal.
9. His actual daily breakfast: two Fairlife shakes, about 84 g / 460 cal.
10. "Bro nachos": Quest chips + Hormel chili (no beans) + Fage + salsa, built to about 100 g.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Mixing cereal or sauces in early so they go soggy (layer instead).
- Counting a measuring spoon's leftovers twice (weigh instead).

## Examples / case details

- He drinks egg whites rather than cooking them because the cooked volume is too large to eat.
