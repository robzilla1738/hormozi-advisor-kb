## Core thesis
Vlog after the $100M Leads launch, going to podcasts. Nuggets: change your environment to change behavior, operationalize vague words, demonstrate the book's concept with the book's own launch, and "one of one" / "one of zero" content.

## Frameworks & models
- **Environment beats willpower:** B.F. Skinner's "you *can* make a horse drink" (deprive it enough). Starve the negatives (alcohol, friends). Vietnam heroin example (via Atomic Habits): ~90% quit after leaving the environment vs ~90% relapse after rehab and returning home.
- **Operationalize amorphous words:** patience = knowing what to do in the meantime. Love = how much you'll sacrifice to maintain the relationship.
- **Meta-launch:** use the book's methods to market the book.
- **One of one:** say what only you can say, show what only you can show (a big marketing rule). **One of zero:** do what no one else will do or what you're not sure you can do yet.
- **Redefine winning:** punishment ("gun to head") is strongest short-term, reward sustains long-term. His winning = working as hard as possible on a few things, judged by an imagined future self who knows what perfect work looks like.
- **Creator mistake:** chasing one viral topic ("blue trees") you don't care about. Big brands are built over decades.
- **After a win:** "more, better" (do more of what you did, better).
