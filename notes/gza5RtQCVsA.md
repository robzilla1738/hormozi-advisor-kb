# How To Close Everyone Downselling Like A Pro
id: gza5RtQCVsA / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gza5RtQCVsA / duration: 680s (~11 min) / tags: 05-sales-process, 03-pricing
Signal: high

## Core thesis
"Everyone buys something." Lead with the primary program. When the prospect can't afford it, step down ethically through a prepared ladder of exchanges: each lower price asks for more from them. That uses reciprocity instead of begging, keeps your frame, and gives a clean answer to "why is she paying less?" The goal is to get a foot in the door, because after 7–14 days they trust you and buy the full program.

## Frameworks & models
- **Exchange cards** (fitness example) for lowering the price ethically: a longer term/commitment, a testimonial, reviews, or bringing a friend. You can also add bonuses up front. He prefers asking for more from them over removing bonuses.
- **Reciprocity**: people misjudge whether an exchange is fair. A small concession followed by a big ask still gets a yes. The person who sets the terms holds the power.
- **"Fair enough?" close**: it lowers the bar to a yes. Ask it like you want the yes, because tonality carries everything on the phone.
- **Collaborative frame**: "same side of the table… let's figure out a way to make this work." Acknowledge lightly that the offers are getting ridiculous.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- If they still say no after about 6 step-downs, it's not money. You lost them earlier and they are trying to get off the phone. Confront disengagement earlier with "Can I be real with you instead of nice to you?", then rub the pain and add a band-aid.
- A testimonial ask implies they will get results, so you are selling while you downsell.
- Never lower the price without getting something in exchange. Otherwise it sounds like begging, desperation or "commission breath," and feels unethical.

## Playbook steps (his ladder)
1. Primary: $299–300 front end (3 weeks at $100/wk). 2. Half price, $49/wk, with a 12-week commitment. 3. Drop the commitment: "first date," pay as you go at $49. 4. $39/wk if they leave a testimonial at the end. 5. $29/wk pay as you go if they bring a friend in the first 30 days. Close each step with "Fair enough?" After the yes, ask for the card or a micro-commitment ("nutrition appointment morning or afternoon?").

## Mistakes he warns against
- Dropping the price with no exchange.
- A weak "sound good?" call to action.
- Reading the script flat.
- Getting aggressive and losing rapport.
