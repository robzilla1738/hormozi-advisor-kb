# Why losing friends is normal...

Signal: low

## Core thesis
Losing old friends as you grow is normal, a price worth paying. Relationships persist through an exchange of value; their depth is how long you'll wait for the next exchange. Be comfortable with space and hold a high bar for friends who root for you behind your back.

## Frameworks & models
- **Exchange-delay model:** relationship strength = how long you'll wait for the next positive exchange (a parent waits very long). It works by intermittent reinforcement, the same mechanism as addiction and abusive relationships.
- **Past-only test:** if all you can talk about with someone is the past, the friendship will decline.
- **Price you're willing to pay** (from Dean Graziosi): fame brings mean comments and people at your house, and he pays that for impact. That reframe pushed Alex and Leila to build personal brands (~18 months before the video).
- Friends: a season, a reason, or a lifetime. Real friends talk trash to your face and praise you behind your back.

## Examples
- Fell out of touch with his last two high school/college friends. His parents didn't support him until success was obvious. Dr. Cashey's response to his idea of patenting the "Hormozi advertising cycles": "Get over yourself."
- 2–3 good friends fill his cup. Many transactional acquaintances drain him.
