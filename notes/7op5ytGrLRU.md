## Core thesis
Leila chose to take less money now to acquire business skills fast (sales, referrals, client management) rather than a slow credentialed path, then picked the path that gave the most business experience fastest — joining Alex to launch Gym Launch.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Choosing between jobs: pick the one that gets you income fastest when cash is tight (she had ~4x Michigan rent in California); later, pick the path that gives the most business experience fastest.
- [inference] "Leapfrog": working for someone ~3 years ahead can skip years of trial and error (Alex's pitch to her).

## Examples / case details
- Lost ~85 lbs; exercise science degree. PT at job fair: 4 yrs out, 45 hrs/week, ~$85k — she wanted ~$100k immediately.
- Moved to Costa Mesa; accepted at ~5 gyms; took 24 Hour Fitness for fastest income; first ~1.5 years = "boot camp in business" (sales, referrals, client management, scheduling, customer service). Prospected at Whole Foods and B2B; couldn't generate clients on demand, so moved to a private training facility to learn marketing.
- Three options: online-fitness partnership, equity in the gym, own gym. Alex (Bumble date) said work for me. He had ~6 gyms + 2 agencies. Returned from first launch with unprocessed contracts worth >$100k; his commitment convinced her. Quit within two weeks; first Gym Launch in Baltimore.

## Notable lines
- "I think it's going to be harder now, I'm going to make less money now, but I think I'm going to make more money later if I just bite the bullet and acquire the skills."
