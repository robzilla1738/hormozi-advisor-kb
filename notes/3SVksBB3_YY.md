# Give me 20 Minutes and I'll Give You Back 20 Years of Your Life
id: 3SVksBB3_YY / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3SVksBB3_YY / duration: 1171s (~20 min) / tags: 12-mindset-leverage, 10-money-cashflow
Signal: medium

## Core thesis
Buy back time spent "humaning" (feeding, cleaning and moving your body) whenever your hourly earnings beat the cost. Seven purchases, in a set order, cost about $2–3K/month and save roughly 45 hrs/week by his tally. Spend that time on skills and active income; he calls it a better return than the S&P 500. (Title says 20 years; in the video he says "10 years back.")

## Frameworks & models
- **Time arbitrage rule**: outsource a task when your hourly earning (or skill-building value) beats its cost per hour saved.
- **The 7 investments, in his recommended order** (sleep is "#0", do it first even though it doesn't save hours):
  0. **Sleep**: blackout curtains (dark enough that eyes open looks the same as closed), earplugs, electrical tape over LEDs, big-cup sleep mask; optional ~$2K cooling mattress (he says it can lower HVAC bills). Raises productivity per hour; one-time cost, permanent gain.
  1. **Meals**: avg 13.5 hrs/wk on groceries/planning/cooking/cleaning/portioning. Two delivered meals/day + a morning shake ≈ $750/mo (replaces groceries); worth it above ~$16/hr. Eating out (Chipotle twice a day, 500+ transactions) counts as "flexible meal prep."
  2. **Laundry**: ~4 hrs/wk. Drop-off at $1.50–2/lb × 30–40 lb/mo ≈ $60–80/mo. Tip: wear the same outfit daily.
  3. **House cleaning**: ~6–7 hrs/wk (3–4 hr deep clean + ~30 min/day tidying) ≈ 26 hrs/mo for $200–300/mo; worth it above ~$20/hr.
  4. **Landscaping**: mowing 1–2 hrs/wk, trimming, gutters ≈ 8 hrs/mo for ~$300/mo.
  5. **Transport (driver/Uber)**: above ~$50/hr. Commute ~1.5 hr/day ≈ 30–50 hrs/mo for $600–1,000/mo. Use the ride productively (he tweets and checks Slack).
  6. **Semi-private flights (e.g. JSX)**: skip TSA; ~3× a commercial fare (~$300 vs ~$99 Vegas–LA) vs ~$15–20K private; ~$1K/mo extra for frequent flyers.
- **Totals as stated**: ~$3,100/mo all in (~$2K if you don't travel much) for ~45 hrs/wk, "an entire week back."
- **Diminishing $/hour**: each next purchase buys fewer hours per dollar, and each saving can only be bought once.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Meals at >$16/hr, cleaning at >$20/hr, drivers at >$50/hr (his thresholds).
- Skip an item only if you get real meaning from that activity (e.g. you love cooking).
- If your market is pricier, you probably earn more too, so the logic still holds.

## Playbook steps
1. Fix sleep now (cheap, one-time). 2. Outsource meals. 3. Laundry. 4. Cleaning. 5. Lawn. 6. Driving. 7. Semi-private flights if you travel. Reinvest the freed hours into skills and active income.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Frugality dogma ("never eat out") that costs time.
- Phone/TV before bed; ignoring the bedroom despite spending a third of life there.
- Obsessing over passive income instead of increasing active income.

## Examples / case details
- His accountant flagged 500+ Chipotle transactions; his grocery bill was just Red Bull and Coke Zero.
- He and Leila worked on laptops outside laundromats while moving around the country.
- He has bought sleep setups for founders who lacked them.
