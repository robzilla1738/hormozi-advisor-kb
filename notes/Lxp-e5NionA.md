# How I recruit the best employees in the world...

## Core thesis
Scale inside the company the same way you scale customer acquisition. The five external core functions (lead gen → lead nurture → sales → fulfillment → retention/ascension; credited to Alex Tarfin [caption spelling]) have a mirror internal pipeline for talent: application generation → application nurture → interviewing (selling) → training → retaining/ascending employees. "Talent is the way that you scale the business." Older video (company cited at ~$85M/yr revenue).

## Frameworks & models
- **External 5 core functions**: lead generation, lead nurture (get them to show), sales, fulfillment, retention (buy more times) and ascension (buy more expensive things). He says you should never outsource knowing how to do these.
- **Internal mirror pipeline**: (1) application generation; (2) application nurture, so applicants schedule and show; (3) interviewing = selling them on working for you, with a script, indoctrinating values and disqualifying bad fits; (4) training = fulfilling on the job you sold (communication cadences, who they report to, job description, KPIs, compensation, how raises happen); (5) retention (keep them motivated to perform) and ascension (find star players early, fast-track them).
- **Six ways to get customers also get employees**: paid ads, earned media, owned media (lists, CRM, phone, followers), manual outbound (recruit from other companies), affiliates, word of mouth.
- **"You don't need a what, you need a who"**: hire someone who has already done it rather than figuring it out yourself.
- **Employer brand as resume stamp**: make working at your company signal quality (like Google/Facebook/"Harvard"), so people will even take a pay cut for the training.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- He cites a stat that high-quality candidates find a job within ~8 days; if first contact takes ~6 days you lose them. Nurture applications as hard as sales leads.
- Remote work / flexible hours is now table stakes; you compete with Netflix, Apple and Google for talent.
- Spell out titles and pay increases tied to clear milestones. With no visible future, an employee is "a ticking time bomb."
- Know your talent acquisition system's metrics.

## Playbook steps
1. Run recruiting ads and outbound through the same six channels as customer acquisition. 2. Respond to applications fast, with a structured nurture process for scheduling and show rate. 3. Interview with a script: sell, share values, disqualify. 4. Onboard/train with defined reporting, KPIs, comp and raise path. 5. Retain with motivation; identify leaders early and fast-track them with clear milestone-based titles and raises.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Working sales leads hard while ignoring employee applications.
- Assuming remote/flexibility is still a recruiting advantage.
- Not giving employees a visible career path.
- Trying to learn the skill yourself when you could hire someone who has it.

## Examples / case details
- His sales team gets heavily recruited by others, but stays because of high pay, an established business and a clear path to making a lot of money.
