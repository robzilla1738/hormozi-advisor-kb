# How To Win Sales Without Being Skilled
id: iwskrByMIqo / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iwskrByMIqo / duration: 657s (~11 min) / tags: 05-sales-process, 12-mindset-leverage
Signal: medium

## Core thesis
"The person who cares the most about the buyer's interests is the one who will win the sale." A sale is always made: either they sell you on why not, or you sell them. Prospects can smell "commission breath." When they trust that you care more about their outcome than they do, they get real with you, and "the moment you're able to get someone to be real with you in the sale, you've already won." Genuine care lets unskilled, personality-driven reps out-close their skill level. Recorded as a 4:30am vlog while he was building the Gym Launch phone sales training.

## Frameworks & models
- **Care asymmetry**: you have to care more about their best interest than they have shown for themselves. That earns you permission to confront their limiting beliefs.
- **Two winning rep types**: process-oriented reps who follow the script, and personality-driven caring reps who get away with less skill.
- **"Sales is just coaching… with an outcome defined over a period of time."**
- **Advanced = never skipping basics**: people are advanced because of outcomes, not tactics, the same way advanced lifters simply lift more, more consistently.
- **Three breaking points of entrepreneur development (cyclical)**: (1) Skills: getting customers alone is a stack of copy, creative, landing pages, traffic, nurture, choosing the sales event, conversion mechanism, scripting, tonality, objections, payments. (2) Character traits to execute those skills: patience, endurance, discipline. (3) Beliefs: about the market, what's possible, and your tolerance for speed and excellence, set top-down. Each level needs all three while you keep the lower ones.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- A rep who is struggling with the same script → check whose agenda they are selling on before retraining.
- "Can't afford it" is a very small percentage of people. If it's real, sit on the same side of the table and solve it together.

## Playbook steps
1. Before each call, reframe: "this person needs my help." 2. Build rapport until they're real with you. 3. Confront limiting beliefs with ownership questions ("whose responsibility is that?… I'm not saying this to be mean… I want to help"). 4. By the close, the baggage is already handled, so they trust you.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Selling on your agenda instead of theirs.
- Expecting the sale to go as imagined, when people bring shame, guilt and emotional baggage.
- Hunting for advanced tactics instead of executing basics consistently.

## Examples / case details
- A struggling gym owner on the daily coaching call went 0-for-about-25, then 5-for-9 overnight with the same script after the "remember why you care" reframe at rep 25.
