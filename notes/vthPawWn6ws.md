# Watch this if you're feeling burnt out
id: vthPawWn6ws / url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vthPawWn6ws / duration: 596s (~10 min) / tags: 12-mindset-leverage, 13-quit-or-pivot
Signal: medium

## Core thesis
Alex's side of a coaching call with an 8-figure+ CEO considering shutting down because of "burnout." Business tripled in ~6–7 months, yet the CEO feels the same pain as before. So conditions didn't cause the feeling: stress comes from your judgment of externals, and managing it is a learned skill. Don't quit or change a working plan because of feelings; build space between feeling and behavior. Only Alex's side of the call is included.

## Frameworks & models
- **Feelings vs. conditions**: "We changed your conditions… your feelings didn't change, which means the feelings are not valid." (They exist; you don't have to obey them.)
- **Stress-label drill**: notice your physical trigger (his: tightness in chest) → ask "what am I labeling as bad?" → follow the catastrophe chain (sales low today → low forever → fire everyone → alone → die) → recognize "thoughts are not true," feelings are chemicals meant to keep you alive, not help you succeed → ask "do I need to change the external condition? Probably not."
- **Bottom-10 days statistics**: with identical behavior over 365 days, ~10% (~36) will be bottom-10% days, about 3 a month; the worst 10% of those (~3.6/yr) are ~1% days. A bad day isn't a reason to change behavior that trended the business up.
- **Yeti mug**: insulation (space) keeps the inside constant regardless of outside temperature. Bi-directional: external stressor → space; internal feeling off → space before acting. Ask "what if this were good / normal / expected / just a bottom-10 day?"
- **Lungs adjusting to altitude**: each rung of success feels as stressful as the last; you'd now wish for your old $1M-business problems. Discomfort = growing pains.
- **Advice filter**: don't listen to people whose dreams for your life are smaller than yours; valid advice comes from a shared common future. Difference between help and manipulation is intention.
- **The fire gives evidence**: hardship proves traits you already claim.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Don't redefine normal as good; extraordinary outcomes need non-normal behaviors/traits (Andy Grove "only the paranoid survive," Steve Jobs obsessive).
- If the plan is solid, keep commitment to the plan as solid; "when you feel stress you do not need to behave stressfully."
- Delay between feeling and behaving → behavior more aligned with long-term goals.

## Playbook steps
1. Identify your bodily stress cue. 2. Name the label/judgment. 3. Trace the catastrophe chain and test it. 4. Check whether the external condition actually needs changing. 5. Reframe (normal / expected / bottom-10 day). 6. Act only after space.

## Mistakes he warns against
- Quitting a growing business from feelings ("burnout isn't real" framing).
- Believing better conditions will fix how you feel.
- Listening to parents/friends with smaller dreams for you.
- Insecurity and second-guessing; needing to see results before continuing.

## Examples / case details
- CEO's business tripled in ~6–7 months of working with Alex; pain level unchanged.
- COVID: gyms closing in the first 3 weeks had "lost the will to fight" (wanted a socially acceptable reason); gyms closing ~month 9 at the second shutdown truly ran out of cash.
- Viktor Frankl, *Man's Search for Meaning*, as perspective.
