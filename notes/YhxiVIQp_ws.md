## Core thesis
Ignore little tax "hacks"; focus on big rocks: where you live, start compounding early, buy below value, hold long (don't sell → no capital gains), and borrow against appreciating assets to live.

## Frameworks & models
- **Big rocks:**
  1. Location: state (0% vs CA ~15–16% as he says); country — US global taxation; Puerto Rico (6+ months, export services → ~4% federal) or expatriation/golden visa (~$1M investment, <6 months in US).
  2. Net worth comes mostly from growth not contributions → start early, live on little. $500/mo at 20 > $5,000/mo at 45.
  3. Buy below value: buying a $100 thing for $30 = untaxed 3x gain.
  4. Hold, don't sell (Buffett: See's Candies, Coca-Cola, GEICO); buy what will last 30 years.
  5. Borrow against assets: loans aren't income; keep borrowing < asset growth; settle at death.
- Money is only valuable for freedom; don't make the vehicle the objective.

## Decision rules & thresholds
- Small strategies (conservation easements, captive insurance) are "BBs"; he declined a captive-insurance play saving ~$250k/yr; IRS later reversed and back-charged users.
- He chose Texas over Puerto Rico: same lifestyle vs. giving up half his life for more net worth ($100M vs $180M "makes no difference").

## Examples / case details
- ~$750k spent on tax advisors; ~$18M taxes paid in prior 3 years.
