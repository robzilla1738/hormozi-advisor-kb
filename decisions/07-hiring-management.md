# Who should I hire, how do I pay them, and how do I manage them so the business grows without me?

**Bottom line:** Talent is the biggest arbitrage in business. Hire A players (pay up to get them), judge them fast, and cut anyone who isn't excellent. When someone doesn't do what you want, check your communication, training and incentives before you blame the person ([The SIMPLE Managerial Framework that changed my business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMowPVgcWbA) (IMowPVgcWbA), [How to Get Anyone to Do Anything You Want](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUnorufFIq8) (aUnorufFIq8)). Hire in reverse order of value: first buy back your cheapest time, then put the freed hours into higher-leverage work. Past ~$3M you need leaders, not doers. Past ~$30M you need superstars with real authority and upside ([The first 5 hires EVERY entrepreneur should make...or go broke...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NqsFh5ej0w) (7NqsFh5ej0w), [The Talent Grid - How I Recognize Skill in Self & Team](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpKz22P84iM) (gpKz22P84iM), [A BILLIONAIRE told me how to make BILLION DOLLARS...here's my plan...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_x6n-CmZqjo) (_x6n-CmZqjo)).

## Diagnose first

**Collect these before advising:**
1. Revenue, gross profit, headcount, and **revenue/gross profit per employee**. "High headcount, low talent count" is the usual cause of a $1–10M business growing at ~10%/yr ([No BS Business Advice to Get Rich in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnbHgYYHzyE) (cnbHgYYHzyE)).
2. **Founder time study**: log what you did every 15 minutes for about a week, then mark each task Green, Yellow or Red ([If I Wanted to Create a Business That Runs Itself, Here's What I'd Do](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4Pw66_490) (mr4Pw66_490)).
3. For every role: "How does your role make the company money?" Can they name their output metrics? ([13 Years Of Brutally Honest Business Advice in 90 Mins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KhFlD54nQrY) (KhFlD54nQrY), mr4Pw66_490)
4. **Utilization** of the person asking for a hire: hours booked vs available. At 40% utilization, "too busy" is the problem itself ([Simple Operational Framework (ALEX HORMOZI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOY6ZzBMb_4) (JOY6ZzBMb_4), [Recruiting and Hiring Tips - How to Hire The Best Employees](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cemduJKQl5w) (cemduJKQl5w)).
5. Is the recurring problem a **what** (process) or a **who** (person)? The same issue for quarters under the same person is a who issue ([I Doubled A Business in 60 Days to Show It's Not Luck (Part 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c_6BrF7jOGk) (c_6BrF7jOGk), [How to Sell Better than 99% Of People (4 HOUR ULTIMATE GUIDE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE2_7elAcxM) (JE2_7elAcxM)).
6. Comp structure: is the behavior you want in their comp? Pay on cash collected / bottom line? (IMowPVgcWbA, [26 Harsh Lessons I Learned in 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YONIUpk-kAs) (YONIUpk-kAs), [The Ultimate Sales Training for 2026 [Full Course]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StVqS0jD7Ls) (StVqS0jD7Ls))
7. Can you name your weak people? If my whole team is amazing, you're wearing rose-colored glasses ([All Entrepreneurs Have Only 1 Problem...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-is6Z2T2h8o) (-is6Z2T2h8o), gpKz22P84iM).

**How the answer changes by stage:**

| Stage | Main hiring/management move | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue / solo | No partner unless they bring money or skills you lack; learn sell + build yourself; first hire = strong operational second | yJfn_pWzFqg, wtsX7WHQMFM |
| <$1M | First hires extend you: offload cheapest, lowest-value repeat tasks; fractional roles (bookkeeper is fine until ~$100K/mo); hire sales early if you can't sell, a setter if you're a killer closer | 7NqsFh5ej0w |
| $1–3M | Fix management before adding (expectations, accountability, communication, cadence, recognition); pass the away test before a 2nd location; don't step out of the CEO seat yet | 2JLU4R8Zcgg, cnbHgYYHzyE, s3tH9Sz1fEc |
| ~$3M → $10M+ | Level-3 "leaders" (build the machine that builds the machine) are needed; hire people who teach you; operators are leaders, not "systems people" | gpKz22P84iM, mr4Pw66_490, oK2_u-uS_Bo |
| $10–30M+ | People become the only problem; talent was his bottleneck from $30M to $100M; three superstars per business (acquisition, delivery, ops) | -is6Z2T2h8o, _x6n-CmZqjo |

**By model:** Service/local businesses depend on skilled labor, so recruiting spend per tech should match that tech's profit ([Helping 6 Business Owners Scale in 33 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EqJD2o-Mnk) (0EqJD2o-Mnk), YONIUpk-kAs). Sales-led businesses are mainly a sales-team system (see 05-sales-process). AI-first companies get revenue per employee "in the millions." Think in workflows, not roles ([How to Win With AI in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9q5ojtkqsBs) (9q5ojtkqsBs)).

## Core principles

1. **Talent is the best ROI**: A players plus a winning culture compound. Pay 25–50% more for someone who moves you 10 years forward (YONIUpk-kAs, KhFlD54nQrY).
2. **"You get what you tolerate."** The leader's top job is setting and holding the standard. Low standards make people shrink to the bar ([Why Ambitious People Stay Mediocre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A248pGXTSoY) (A248pGXTSoY), c_6BrF7jOGk, [We fired everyone who was late...this is why..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rX5zKeQu7ow) (rX5zKeQu7ow)).
3. **Selection beats training.** "You win the championship in the draft" Also: "Better to find a .400 hitter than teach someone to swing" ([The day when everything changed for me...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_33XVdvO4Gc) (_33XVdvO4Gc), [I emailed myself all of my failures for the last 5 years...this is what I learned..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQGHCG8d1So) (QQGHCG8d1So)).
4. **Non-performance has three or five causes, in order**: didn't know you wanted it, didn't know what, didn't know how, didn't know by when, circumstances. Only then is it motivation. Attack the process, not the person (IMowPVgcWbA, aUnorufFIq8, [How to Articulate Your Thoughts More Clearly Than 99% of People](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6tkRztZwYc) (s6tkRztZwYc)).
5. **Buy back time in reverse order of value**, then redeploy it on higher-leverage work. Otherwise the hire is pure cost (7NqsFh5ej0w, [How to buyback your time as a business owner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awOQDy_o9ww) (awOQDy_o9ww), [I stepped down as CEO...and created a PASSIVE INCOME machine..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3tH9Sz1fEc) (s3tH9Sz1fEc)). "Every new hire is a guaranteed cost but not a guaranteed return" ([How the Top 1% ACTUALLY Make Their Money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wtsX7WHQMFM) (wtsX7WHQMFM)).
6. **Give up control but keep accountability**: "know where the bodies are buried" (awOQDy_o9ww). Each stage means giving up a layer: doing → managing → leading → deciding ([How I Scaled My Sales Team [Compensation, Recruiting & Training]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=okA9Yt2KZuk) (okA9Yt2KZuk)).
7. **Reward over punishment.** Reinforcement unlocks discretionary effort, while punishment gets the minimum. Recency beats intensity ([They lied to you: Why Punishment DOESN'T WORK..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WRW7eY6K4Jo) (WRW7eY6K4Jo), [My 12 Hour Work Day (in 15 Minutes) [Ep 003]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lJF__n_34ew) (lJF__n_34ew), [Watch these 66 mins if you want to build a $10,000,000+ company in 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2JLU4R8Zcgg) (2JLU4R8Zcgg), [Money Lessons I Learned In Marriage.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oK2_u-uS_Bo) (oK2_u-uS_Bo)).
8. **Core values are hire-and-fire filters**: three values, 3/3 or no. Acquisition.com's are unimpeachable character, sincere candor and competitive greatness ([$100M CEO: How I know who to trust...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kW2vDMAmlPI) (kW2vDMAmlPI)).
9. **Pattern recognition in people is execution speed.** Know the "ideal scene" for each seat, and learn it from someone who has built that department many times ([What Millionaires Know That You Don't](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jv3GmZ9Odko) (Jv3GmZ9Odko)).
10. **The best IC is rarely the best manager.** Assess the What / How / Who levels separately (c_6BrF7jOGk, gpKz22P84iM, [How To Build Large Sales Teams (Starting from 0)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lA_A8BGRRs) (2lA_A8BGRRs)).
11. **Equity is the most expensive thing you can give.** Pay for needs. Give performance deals for measurable roles and phantom equity for key, hard-to-measure ones ([10 KEYS to a TERRIBLE Business Partnership [GUARANTEED!]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JShQ8BX08rs) (JShQ8BX08rs), [Here's Why I Always Give Away a % of My Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxpPynt1mis) (PxpPynt1mis), [What I Learned After Countless Failed Business Partnerships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DAm0EOes5to) (DAm0EOes5to)).
12. **Execution (hearts) beats strategy (smarts)** once the strategy is roughly sound. If months of tinkering with offer and pricing changed nothing, look at people and culture (rX5zKeQu7ow, [How To Progress Way Faster Than Anyone Else](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-UzJOk85OZI) (-UzJOk85OZI)).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Who to hire / when
- **First hires**: offload the cheapest, lowest-value repeated tasks first. A fractional bookkeeper is enough until ~$100K/month. Early roles can be fractional (7NqsFh5ej0w).
- If your time is worth $100/hr, don't do $15/hr work (awOQDy_o9ww).
- **Hiring request? Answer "no" first.** Ask for hour-by-hour utilization (cemduJKQl5w). Size roles with activity × time per customer × inflow. Example: a coach running fulfillment, onboarding and check-ins fills ~6 of 8 hrs/day (JOY6ZzBMb_4).
- **Bring in a senior leader only if you'll use the freed capacity for something more valuable** ([Everything I Learned From 60 Days At Acquisition.com | The Operator Pod Ep. 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZklXJzHzW2U) (ZklXJzHzW2U), s3tH9Sz1fEc).
- **Leaders needed around ~$3M** to break through to $10M+/yr (gpKz22P84iM).
- **"Hire one to hire 10."** Hire the sales director first (JE2_7elAcxM).
- **Founder keeps the last revenue-driving role until there's enough team.** Leila still took top sales calls at Gym Launch with >$1M/mo profit and 50 staff ([CEO Reacts to Coldplay Kiss Cam Scandal | The Operator Pod Ep. 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GmJQyCFCz1k) (GmJQyCFCz1k), Leila).
- **Talent trumps timing**: engage exceptional people before the role exists (GmJQyCFCz1k, Leila).
- **AI**: break jobs into tasks and automate them. When a role disappears, raise the company-wide bar rather than inventing work (9q5ojtkqsBs).
- **Partner instead of hire** only when the skill can't be hired or would take a decade to grow ([We Made a BIG Decision...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUI12JmD-lM) (OUI12JmD-lM)). Otherwise a strong operational second is a hire, not a 50% partner ([Top Signs You Have The Wrong Business Partner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJfn_pWzFqg) (yJfn_pWzFqg)).

### How to evaluate candidates
- **Judge by the quality and quantity of metrics** they use to describe the role (mr4Pw66_490, JE2_7elAcxM, c_6BrF7jOGk). Example: a CS candidate who names ~9 metrics (48-hour activation, time to value, back-outs at 24/48/72h, reviews by day 30) beats "make people happy" (KhFlD54nQrY).
- **Leaders should teach you in the interview.** If you aren't learning, it's a waste (mr4Pw66_490). Test: could your head of sales or marketing do it on their own? (YONIUpk-kAs)
- **Higher roles: hire for proven ability. Lower roles: hire for potential**, intelligence and work ethic over experience (-UzJOk85OZI). Degrees don't matter; experience does ([Seriously, watch this before you go to college](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nxL4ZfVqKLI) (nxL4ZfVqKLI)).
- Ask what are you obsessed with in your off time? If it isn't the role's skill, they'll moonlight (-UzJOk85OZI).
- **Interview more than feels reasonable**: "the extra 20 interviews" is worth it for an A player (-is6Z2T2h8o, mr4Pw66_490). Use group interviews for frontline up to manager (mr4Pw66_490).
- **Values: 3/3 or no.** Ambitious and candid but weak character means no (kW2vDMAmlPI). "If you would not do business with someone for a decade, do not do business with them for a day" (kW2vDMAmlPI).
- **No three strikes**: someone with a history (e.g. a fraud indictment) shouldn't be your come-up story. Second chances burned him "the majority of the time" (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Talk to a key hire's spouse**, because neutral support turns negative over time (cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **Hire people who like change.** Tell them the role may change ~50% within 3 months ([Leila Hormozi: Making Money Doesn't Change You with Brooke Castillo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nd22J08b1TA) (nd22J08b1TA), Leila).
- **Undersell the job by ~15%** so reality beats expectations (GmJQyCFCz1k, Leila).
- **Nurture applicants like sales leads.** He cites good candidates being hired within ~8 days, so a 6-day first response loses them ([How I recruit the best employees in the world...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lxp-e5NionA) (Lxp-e5NionA)).
- **Executive assistants**: he was 0 for 12 before his current one. Require replies within 60 seconds, screen for general intelligence, and pay more than you expect (YONIUpk-kAs). Leila hired 3 bad EAs before a good one (ZklXJzHzW2U).
- Don't take hiring advice from people ~6 months ahead of you (Jv3GmZ9Odko).

### Speed of judgment / cutting
- **A players show within days.** "Within the first couple of days" (Jv3GmZ9Odko). Within the first week, and "I need a quarter to get my bearings" is a red flag (OUI12JmD-lM). He says he has always known within 14 days (YONIUpk-kAs). He has never seen a non-A player become one (Jv3GmZ9Odko). *Consistent across videos; the 14-day figure in the 2025 video is the most recent. It applies less to fast-ramping low-wage roles.*
- **Sales reps**: no close within one full sales cycle (_33XVdvO4Gc), or 1–2 weeks / ~14 days ([30 Minutes of sales training that will explode your business in 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oi7bnS8uyJM) (oi7bnS8uyJM), JE2_7elAcxM), means cut. New closers get 10 deals in 2 weeks, and zero of 10 means drop back (okA9Yt2KZuk). "It's easier to take a 6 to a 9 than a 2 to a 5."
- **PIP rule**: below KPI 2 weeks → PIP; no improvement after 2 more weeks → fired. Set this expectation at hire (2lA_A8BGRRs).
- **Cut the bottom ~10% regularly**, even when things are fine. He says each cut gives ~30% more productivity ([How I Built A 20+ Sales Team [The Process]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgmh7p1s7zs) (bgmh7p1s7zs)) [figure as stated]. "If you don't cut the bottom, you're telling the team it's OK to suck" (2lA_A8BGRRs).
- **Fire low performers when things are good, not when they're bad** (cnbHgYYHzyE).
- "Nothing demotivates a high performer like a low performer who's still on the team" (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **Moving an underperformer to another role**: "nine times out of 10" it isn't the right call (IMowPVgcWbA).
- **Behavior problem in a high performer**: give specific observable do/stop instructions before firing (s6tkRztZwYc). But no prima donnas: a 3× rep who skips the process drags the team down (JE2_7elAcxM).
- **"Didn't think that was my job"**: they probably shouldn't do anything else in your company either (aUnorufFIq8).
- **Hard conversations within 24 hours.** Frame them around the employee's own goal ([Everything We Learned From Making $106 Million in 72 Hours](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EuMu6LaxBSk) (EuMu6LaxBSk)).
- **Outgrown early employees**: don't "junk drawer" them. Introduce them to 3–5 better-fit companies (nd22J08b1TA, Leila). Encourage graceful exits, even to competitors (oK2_u-uS_Bo).

### Compensation & incentives
- **Recruiting spend vs. value**: a tech producing $300K/yr profit justifies ~$50K to recruit (0EqJD2o-Mnk). A specialist dentist at $700K/yr gross profit justifies ~$150K per headhunter placement. Four placements cost $600K, cutting the year's profit from $4.6M to $4.0M, and it's "easily worth it" (YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Relocation recruiting**: national ads plus e.g. $25K now and $25K at month 6 (0EqJD2o-Mnk). Or a signing bonus plus a 30-day hotel trial (cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **Only raise comp to fix an attraction or retention problem.** Otherwise "you're paying fine" (cemduJKQl5w).
- **Pay stars to stay**: a star rep often produces 2–3+ normal reps, so a 10–20% raise is cheap (cemduJKQl5w). Paying above market can capture top talent (Henry Ford; aUnorufFIq8).
- **Pay for performance**: existing staff keep their salary with performance pay added on top, and new hires start on the plan. Back-test on last month's numbers. "Buying 3× output at 2× cost is still a deal" (cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **Revenue-line leaders are paid on the bottom line, with uncapped upside**: "If they need you, they don't deserve the incentive" (YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Comp attracts but rarely changes behavior.** The exception is paying on cash collected (StVqS0jD7Ls). If a task isn't in their comp while four other things are, expect it not to get done (IMowPVgcWbA).
- **Sales commission < ~10% of revenue** when the company supplies leads (JE2_7elAcxM).
- **Overcompensated employee**: first hire someone who can do the role at the new pay, then have the talk, then keep the old pay for 2–3 months (cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **Career paths**: titles and raises tied to milestones. Without a visible future, an employee is "a ticking time bomb" (Lxp-e5NionA). Micro-raises like $500 → $525 → $550 per deal at 50/100/150 deals (oi7bnS8uyJM).
- **Equity**: measurable role (marketing) → performance deal. Hard-to-measure key role → phantom equity that vests on a liquidity event (PxpPynt1mis). No planned liquidity event → pay leaders from profit ([Watch These 51 Minutes if You Want to Grow Your Business in 2026 (Dave Ramsey Interview)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HJg4TYBgtI) (-HJg4TYBgtI), Ramsey guest). CEO profit shares of ~5% plus the right to buy in (_x6n-CmZqjo, secondhand billionaire notes).
- **HR's customer is the company.** One HR salary analysis gave out raises without cutting anyone and cost ~$600K/yr in profit for no value (cemduJKQl5w).

### Management cadence
- **Fix management before adding** channels, people or spend. Pick ONE of the five principles to improve (2JLU4R8Zcgg, Leila).
- **If you want it done, have them report it daily or weekly** (IMowPVgcWbA).
- **Always attach a deadline.** End of day is the default (s6tkRztZwYc, aUnorufFIq8). Pulling "end of week" to end of day is ~7× speed (cnbHgYYHzyE, [A DOER's approach to PROCRASTINATION](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oW6JkqL6sdM) (oW6JkqL6sdM)).
- **Fixing a department**: delete all recurring meetings first and replace them with a daily huddle plus ad hoc stand-ups (cnbHgYYHzyE). Audit recurring meetings quarterly ([You’re Wasting 80% of Your Time (here’s how to fix it)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIRkQQHzsxI) (GIRkQQHzsxI)).
- **Protect maker time**: managers batch meetings, and makers keep big blocks. The first 4–6 hours of the day go to high-leverage work (GIRkQQHzsxI, KhFlD54nQrY).
- **Write memos rather than holding meetings to talk.** End each day by asking "What do I have to show for it?" (ZklXJzHzW2U)
- **Sales team cadence**: trainees 1–2×/day for 14 days, weekly 1:1s, daily huddle, competitions in 6-week cycles (2lA_A8BGRRs, bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM).
- **Spending authority box** (e.g. <$500 / $1K / $10K / $100K by company size), checked against monthly financials. If-this-then-that decision trees for common cases (mr4Pw66_490).
- **Don't SOP everything under $10M**: "If you have really dumb people you have to make really strict rules." Hire competent people and give them liberty (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Repeat values about 7 times** and tie every decision to them (2JLU4R8Zcgg, Leila).
- **If someone won't prep for a 1:1, cancel it** (GmJQyCFCz1k, Leila).
- **Give feedback as N do's and N don'ts, never a character label** (s6tkRztZwYc). Mostly positive feedback (WRW7eY6K4Jo).
- **Sleep on emotional reactions**: "if you're still upset tomorrow, chew their head off" (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **When a metric declines, look at where growth first flattened.** The cause is often someone you added (cnbHgYYHzyE).

## Playbooks

### A. Buy back your time (founder doing everything, <$3M)
1. Run a 1-week time study at 15-minute intervals (mr4Pw66_490).
2. Tag each task Project / Process / Person, then Green / Yellow / Red. Hand off greens, then yellows, then reds (mr4Pw66_490).
3. Hire in reverse order of value and replacement cost: admin, then fulfillment, then sales support. If you're a closer, hire a setter. If you can't sell, hire sales early (7NqsFh5ej0w).
4. Hand off with Shadow → Supervise → Support. Accept 80% (even 60%) quality if there's a clear path to improvement. Graduation test: does someone 80% as good as you get 100% of the result? (mr4Pw66_490)
5. Set KPIs by reversing each role's possible mistakes. Set a spending authority box (mr4Pw66_490).
6. Put the freed hours into higher-leverage work. The leverage ladder trades ~200 hrs of doing for ~20 hrs of managing (mr4Pw66_490, [Hard work won't make you rich.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PTgGfV8Tf00) (PTgGfV8Tf00)).
7. Tests: it doesn't burn down in a month away, it gets better, and it grows over 3 months off (mr4Pw66_490).

### B. Someone isn't doing what you asked
1. Did they know you wanted it? Write it down (aUnorufFIq8).
2. Did they know exactly what? Define observable behaviors (s6tkRztZwYc).
3. Did they know how? Document, demonstrate, duplicate (aUnorufFIq8).
4. Did they know by when? Default to end of day (aUnorufFIq8).
5. Circumstances: if the blocker was real and more important, fine. If not, address their agency (aUnorufFIq8).
6. Motivation: is it in their comp or scorecard? Would they do it for $10K by tomorrow? (IMowPVgcWbA)
7. All boxes covered and it still doesn't happen → have the real conversation within 24h, framed around their goals (EuMu6LaxBSk). Same problem for quarters → it's a who problem, so replace them (c_6BrF7jOGk).

### C. Recruit an A player / leader
1. Write the JD as copy, by the manager, from the pain (ZklXJzHzW2U). Undersell it by ~15% (GmJQyCFCz1k).
2. Source leaders by outreach or a recruiter, not ads. Reach 100–1,000 people (JE2_7elAcxM). Frontline roles: national ads, relocation packages (0EqJD2o-Mnk).
3. Respond within days (Lxp-e5NionA). Founders judge culture and values (3/3), and subject-matter experts run tactical interviews (c_6BrF7jOGk, kW2vDMAmlPI).
4. Score them on the metrics they name. Ask whether they teach you (mr4Pw66_490).
5. Talk to their spouse. Agree on comp tied to the bottom line or cash, with uncapped upside (cnbHgYYHzyE, YONIUpk-kAs).
6. Onboard: the manager owns weeks 2–4, and expect value in week 1 (OUI12JmD-lM). Decide by day 14 (YONIUpk-kAs).

### D. Build a sales team (summary; full detail in 05-sales-process)
Hire for coachability and belief. Former customers make great reps ([$100M Salesman Reveals #1 Persuasion Hack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHnVItVGJPA) (bHnVItVGJPA)). Use a question-based script. Train 60 min/day: 25 min reading the script aloud, 5 min objection drills, 30 min call review (oi7bnS8uyJM). Record every call. Keep a daily leaderboard. Run 6-week team competitions with prizes worth ~25% of a month's pay (bgmh7p1s7zs). Cut within 2 weeks (2lA_A8BGRRs). The best rep ≠ manager. Managers are "the rock" (2lA_A8BGRRs). If reps can take 10 calls/day but take 4, you have too many reps (c_6BrF7jOGk).

### E. Fix a stagnant team ($1–10M growing slowly)
1. Diagnose "high headcount, low talent count" (cnbHgYYHzyE). Name the weak people (-is6Z2T2h8o).
2. Delete recurring meetings and install a daily huddle (cnbHgYYHzyE).
3. Pick one management principle: expectations, accountability, communication, cadence or recognition (2JLU4R8Zcgg).
4. Move to pay-for-performance, back-tested (cnbHgYYHzyE).
5. Cut the bottom while things are good, and recruit three superstars for acquisition, delivery and ops (_x6n-CmZqjo).

## Anti-patterns
- **Stepping out of the CEO seat too soon.** This is the #1 mistake. He pushes back on $1–3M founders who want to "outsource my business" (s3tH9Sz1fEc).
- **Stepping away without enough talent** leads to a crash. Alex says he has done it multiple times (_x6n-CmZqjo).
- **Promoting by default**: top salesperson ≠ sales director, and "mom can do some math" ≠ controller (Jv3GmZ9Odko).
- **Adding people, channels or money before fixing management** brings chaos and churn (2JLU4R8Zcgg).
- **Rose-colored glasses**: ~95% of founders say "my team is amazing", then tell horror stories (gpKz22P84iM).
- **Giving equity for a need you could pay for**, or to a partner who says "I don't do agreements" (JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Over-systemizing**: SOPs for everything under $10M. Also, automation with a 10-year payback. A $70K dev build to save ~$7K/yr was rejected (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Ruling by punishment**: before Leila, Alex ran 8 businesses on fear and "never cracked $3 million" (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **"Industry standard" pay and standards**, when the average business barely makes money (A248pGXTSoY, aUnorufFIq8).
- **Inventing work for an automated role** ("Danny") (9q5ojtkqsBs).
- **Ad hoc meetings that wreck makers' blocks** (GIRkQQHzsxI).
- **Raising underpaid people without cutting overpaid ones**, i.e. HR serving employees instead of the company (cemduJKQl5w).
- **Keeping unemployable family employed** (aUnorufFIq8). Couples in business "works until it doesn't" Treat your spouse like any other partner ([Leila Hormozi's Brutally Honest Advice About Relationships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NBhDR8R_YGk) (NBhDR8R_YGk), Leila).
- **Waiting six months for someone to "turn the corner"** (oi7bnS8uyJM).

## Worked examples
- **Recruiting ROI (dentist)**: $700K/yr gross profit per specialist. Four headhunter placements at ~$150K = $600K. Profit drops from $4.6M to $4.0M in year one and is "easily worth it" (YONIUpk-kAs). [inference] Payback is under 3 months per specialist once productive.
- **Raising pay without a reason**: ~90 days and ~$100K of payroll spent on a salary study, then raises for the "underpaid" only. Result: −$600K/yr in profit for no change in attraction or retention (cemduJKQl5w).
- **Fixing the who first**: a portfolio consumer-service company fixed its sales leadership, then show, offer and close rates. Units went from 56 to 93/mo and cash up front from 47% to 82% in 60 days (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **Same profit, different value**: Frustrated Fred vs Wealthy William each have $10M revenue / $2M profit. Fred saves ~$500K/yr. William's team runs the business, so it's worth 6× = $12M, and +$500K of profit adds +$3M of value (mr4Pw66_490).
- **Automation rejected**: 14 dev days × $5K = $70K to save ~200 hrs/yr (~$7K) is a ~10-year payback (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Outbound "farm"**: Gym Launch built BDR → SDS → closer tiers. Cold callers worked 4-hour shifts with 100 dials. BDRs ramped in 2 days with almost no screening. Closers at ≤25% were bumped down (okA9Yt2KZuk).

## Metrics to watch
- **7 days**: new hires' first-week output (A players add value in week 1); time to first contact with applicants; overdue "end of week" tasks; reps' close rate on their first 10 deals.
- **30 days**: hire/no-hire decision on every new hire (day 14 at the latest); utilization by role; recurring meetings deleted; % of KPIs each role reports daily or weekly; the founder's hours on Red vs Green tasks.
- **90 days**: revenue and gross profit per employee; bottom-10% turnover; regretted attrition among A players; the away test (1 month → 3 months); recruiting cost per hire vs. first-year gross profit per hire; whether the founder's freed hours went to higher-leverage work.
