# What content, ads and brand should I make, and how do I judge them?

> Rebuilt from the hand-read notes (notes/<id>.md). Citations are video ids in manifest.json; open notes/<id>.md, then transcripts/<id>.md, to go deeper. **[inference]** marks synthesis or math that isn't Alex's. Guest and Leila Hormozi statements are attributed by name.

**Bottom line:** Make content for *buyers*, not views. Go narrow, deep and educational for your avatar, lead with proof, and judge it by revenue-weighted metrics (opt-ins, applications, book sales, RPM), never raw views. Organic content is the "give" and ads are the "ask." For most businesses organic is *lead nurture*, while paid ads, outbound and affiliates pay the bills. Win ads on hooks and creative volume: "think in hundreds," reskin winners, and "use and abuse" them until they die. Brand is "a deliberate pairing of things through an outcome," and it's how you eventually beat paid's diminishing returns [How to become rich with social media (my exact playbook)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21flGkcZO3A) (21flGkcZO3A), [1.2M Followers in 6 Months… My Content Marketing Strategy REVEALED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MD5-HByRxoA) (MD5-HByRxoA), [Building a brand, but it’s on easy mode instead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGEc9-7X3OQ) (UGEc9-7X3OQ), [$100M CEO Explains How to Build A Brand in 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQM3DrnVTcs) (VQM3DrnVTcs), [Building a $3,000,000 Business for a Stranger in 31 Minutes | Scale or Fail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j2TZMFkj71Q) (j2TZMFkj71Q).

## Core principles

1. **"The algorithm will tell you what the most people like, not the most valuable people like."** His six most-viewed videos of a quarter made 0 sales (21flGkcZO3A). "Likes ain't cash, views ain't cash, cash is cash" (JOfsujnXxCg).
2. **"The content is the targeting."** Topic, setting and clothes decide who gets shown it [My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dMZ-n2KSlxE) (dMZ-n2KSlxE), (CaiLcj6tzBQ).
3. **Organic is lead nurture, not lead generation, early on.** Prospects consume 2–4 pieces to check you're legit [1.2M Followers in 6 Months… My Content Marketing Strategy REVEALED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MD5-HByRxoA) (MD5-HByRxoA), [I Helped Business Owners Overcome Their Fear of Failure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_Gf5v8DEMY) (0_Gf5v8DEMY).
4. **Give in public, sell in private, and "give until they ask."** "The audience is the asset, not the content you make." [Make Money Online (Without Destroying Your Reputation)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5cOwh-8scu8) (5cOwh-8scu8), [I Helped 6 Business Owners Make More Money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBPWCdaJJqA) (aBPWCdaJJqA)
5. **Once you run ads, content becomes all gives and ads do the asking.** "Build all the Goodwill for free and then you use ads to do the CTAs." [More Followers Won't Make You Rich, But This Will](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEIqyLE4iOY) (lEIqyLE4iOY), [Building a brand, but it’s on easy mode instead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGEc9-7X3OQ) (UGEc9-7X3OQ)
6. **Goodwill compounds faster than revenue.** "The longer the runway, the bigger the plane" Direct response is capped by the value in the relationship [Why direct response marketers get rich...not wealthy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ec41cSp_tWc) (Ec41cSp_tWc), [Get Rich in the New Economy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6DCDGSnRDtM) (6DCDGSnRDtM)
7. **"Your promise is not unique, your proof is."** Competitors can copy your offer but not your proof. ~80% of their ads are proof [Learn Paid Ads in 30 Minutes!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI) (fSbqaTlWaYI), [14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDR-R--4HhM) (JDR-R--4HhM), [The New Way of Making Content In The Age of AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XsWSvz-aewA) (XsWSvz-aewA)
8. **Hooks are ~80% of an ad.** Ads are hook → meat → CTA, and "you can never have too many ads." [Business is now on ‘Easy Mode” in 2026 (here’s why)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U) (Ds_Qp2U5I8U), [STEAL Ryan Reynolds' $1.3B Marketing Strategy [NO FAME REQUIRED]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HwPXctiw_rY) (HwPXctiw_rY), (CaiLcj6tzBQ)
9. **Clear beats clever.** Write at a 3rd–5th grade reading level. "A confused mind does not buy." [How to Grow Your Business SO Fast in 2026 It Feels ILLEGAL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HxEQCHpZzHk) (HxEQCHpZzHk), (JDR-R--4HhM), (reisEL_D7xc)
10. **"We need to be reminded more than we need to be taught."** Repeat and repost your winners (HVOubeXUcx0), (oRMG_HpOAN4)
11. **Volume negates luck.** 7 → 80 posts a week grew him 10× faster [How I built a 1.2M follower audience in 6 months](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrCt0R3FBFs) (WrCt0R3FBFs), (JOfsujnXxCg), [17 Life-Changing Conversations I Wish I Had Earlier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULGT0Qpglek) (ULGT0Qpglek)

## Decision rules & thresholds

### What content to make
- **Measure content by attributed revenue, leads and applications.** Use ad revenue (views × RPM) as the north-star *paired* metric. The highest-RPM month also had the most book sales, opt-ins and applications (21flGkcZO3A, HVOubeXUcx0, JOfsujnXxCg).
- **Six shifts that worked for him:** edutainment → education; "for us" → "for you"; wide → narrow; views → ad revenue; plus proof-led intros and more volume. Results after 3–4 weeks: RPM +68%, opt-ins +26%, subscriber conversion +24.6% (Jmkq5RLjm0U, JOfsujnXxCg, HVOubeXUcx0).
- **The 90-day broad-content experiment:** views rose 2–3× and ad revenue halved. A 100K-view video out-earned a 10M-view one (HVOubeXUcx0, Jmkq5RLjm0U, lEIqyLE4iOY).
- **No "funnel" between formats.** Entertainment viewers want more entertainment, and shorts viewers don't become long-form viewers "at any appreciable rate." Long-form drove the most applications and sales. Shorts give face recognition for retargeting, so they can be a bit wider (HVOubeXUcx0, JOfsujnXxCg).
- **Find buyer topics** from your top 20% of customers by spend: their traits, messages and problems (21flGkcZO3A). Ask in-person buyers what content they like best (dMZ-n2KSlxE).
- **Roughly 3:1 or 4:1 niche:wide** in long-form, with one wider piece as top of funnel (UGEc9-7X3OQ).
- **Speak only from experience** ("how I," not "how to"). "If the source of your content is other people's content, don't make it" (Leila Hormozi and Alex, eVY4Zo-hpqU), (WrCt0R3FBFs, ZTSI3DDP_4A).
- **Avoid topics where everyone has an opinion and there's no proof,** like relationships, which are "too many steps" from business-owner buyers (0S5xsICW8qg, lEIqyLE4iOY).
- **Proof ladder** (lEIqyLE4iOY):
  1. "I helped people like you get X" (lEIqyLE4iOY)
  2. "I did the thing you want, and I'm like you" (lEIqyLE4iOY)
  3. "I did work you won't do" (lEIqyLE4iOY) (e.g. "I made 35,000 posts" (JOfsujnXxCg)).
  4. Break down a big verifiable success.
- **The more risk your advice carries, the more proof you need** (and the safer you are from AI). If you have few customers, do free audits publicly and document them (XsWSvz-aewA).
- **Influence = SPCL:** Status, Power (say-do correspondence), Credibility (third-party proof), Likeness (be yourself) (dMZ-n2KSlxE, spXH1kJ6q-Q).
- **Set view expectations by market size.** Only ~9% of Americans own a business, and ~5% of those are over $1M. 100K views from that audience is "crushing it" (Jmkq5RLjm0U, 21flGkcZO3A).

### How to make it
- **Intro: Proof → Promise → Plan** (any order, but educators lead with proof). Obsess over the first 30 seconds (HVOubeXUcx0, UGEc9-7X3OQ, 4GQLJjH9-oA).
- **Packaging should be clear, not vague.** He rejects the curiosity gap for educators (HVOubeXUcx0). There are only three formats: lists, steps and stories. Long-form is strung-together shorts (UGEc9-7X3OQ).
- **Hook → Retain → Reward.** ~80% decide in the first 3 seconds (CaiLcj6tzBQ).
- **Pre > post:** "an ounce of pre is worth a pound of post" Spending a quarter of former post-production time on pre-work removed 90–95% of post work and gave ~4× output (FMzKk73iUhw, Jmkq5RLjm0U). Delete requirements with no evidence (color grading). "The only thing that is more efficient than automation is elimination" (ZTSI3DDP_4A).
- **Capture, don't create:** record Q&As, calls and events and clip them (XsWSvz-aewA, jvXOOddDg_s).
- **No CTAs inside content** ("they kill reach"). Put links in the description and bio. Shorts get zero CTAs. Stories can take 1–2 CTAs a week. Instagram images and carousels act like email to existing followers (UGEc9-7X3OQ, lEIqyLE4iOY).
- **Repost last year's best piece.** He bets it does as well or better (HVOubeXUcx0, Jmkq5RLjm0U).
- **Content must be platform-specific now.** Start YouTube simply: 3 videos a week on a webcam, improving one thing each week (wR8KoE8u1p0).
- **Five phases of content:** post anything → consistent on one platform → reliable on all platforms → max each platform → … (MD5-HByRxoA).
- **A master sheet of the best "convincer" content,** mapped to sales-call FAQs, that reps send before or between calls (MD5-HByRxoA).

### Give:ask
- **Facebook runs ~1 ad per 4 posts (~3:1).** Treat that as the *maximum* ask rate. Use integrated asks for long-form and intermittent asks for short-form (5cOwh-8scu8).
- **Budget and impressions ~70% give, 30% ask.** Expect brand returns in ~18 months (HxEQCHpZzHk). New Balance flipped from 70/30 bottom-funnel to the reverse and saw nothing for 18 months, then a lift in month 19 (as Alex relays the CMO's account, YUXLJauT4eY).
- **"Either ask hard or do not ask at all."** Usually you're better off holding back the ask (ThSFgC_4NVA).
- **Turn down early endorsement deals.** Your audience will be much larger later (5cOwh-8scu8). Monetize audiences in order: affiliates → sponsorships → partnerships (equity) → your own brand. The more cash you have, the more you should lean toward equity (oRqnTOT9ZG8).

### Paid ads and creative
- **"Think in hundreds."** His team makes ~30 new unique ads a week, ~100 with hook permutations. Founders make 5–7 a month (m5ordaa7NN4, j2TZMFkj71Q).
- **Creative quality caps spend** (m5ordaa7NN4):
  - A 5/10 ad caps at ~$100/day.
  - 7/10 → ~$500/day.
  - 8/10 → ~$5K/day.
  - 9–10/10 "converts the world." (m5ordaa7NN4)
- **Spend capping usually means the creative isn't good enough** (MH-IMJxbUY4). If CPA triples as you scale, your hooks are aimed only at product-aware buyers. Move toward pain- and problem-aware hooks (j2TZMFkj71Q).
- **Reskin winners:** take the top 5–10% of past ads and splice their first 3 seconds onto others. ~80% of resources go to remaking winners, e.g. ~100 versions of the "Kale ad" (fSbqaTlWaYI). "Use and abuse it until it doesn't work at all." A portfolio company's new ad director failed with fresh ads, and reusing a 3-year-old hook worked (HxEQCHpZzHk).
- **Organic + CTA is his best current ad type:** run organic winners as ads with a 5-second CTA (Ds_Qp2U5I8U, MH-IMJxbUY4, fr78adfAnuA).
- **UGC engine:** make film yourself using it the first-7-days activation task, so every customer makes an ad (j2TZMFkj71Q). Aim for 20–30 community videos a week (MH-IMJxbUY4).
- **"Good video beats images, but images beat bad videos."** On short-form, use iPhone-selfie style, not polished (MH-IMJxbUY4).
- **Local Meta creative fatigues in ~2–3 weeks,** so refresh about every 21 days (6m6DCQMASEM).
- **Who appears in the ad and the setting** attract the avatar most. Use 2–4 different people to broaden reach (CaiLcj6tzBQ). Call out who it's for and who it's NOT for (JDR-R--4HhM, kGfzLjPNsBU).
- **Awareness levels** (Schwartz) decide the hook. Unaware → curiosity; most aware → just make the offer. Don't open with the offer for non-most-aware audiences (HwPXctiw_rY, 8C_6qojTA78).
- **Use true, specific stats as hooks,** framed to sound biggest ("$15,500" over "30%") and always substantiated (0BZ7-PZR8jE). Pain-based testimonial hooks win: ask "What was your worst moment?" (pLhQOYMGa88).
- **Us vs them** only when you're NOT the leader. The leader does "us vs the alternative" (HwPXctiw_rY).
- **Launch spend cadence** (4 weeks): 5–10% of spend in week 1 to test creative, scale through weeks 2–3, and 60–70% in the final week (j2TZMFkj71Q). At launch peak he spent ~$500K/day with ~2,800 ads banked (0lMn_-EXyhQ).
- **Learning budget:** 10–20% of marketing spend on new ideas, expecting ~4 of 5 to fail (ULGT0Qpglek).
- **Headline formula for local offers:** Duration + Benefit + Power word (e.g. "28-Day Drop Two Belt Notches Transformation") (kGfzLjPNsBU).

### Copy (JDR-R--4HhM, HwPXctiw_rY, HxEQCHpZzHk)
- Headline first. "All ads have headlines. There's just bad ones and good ones." (JDR-R--4HhM)
- **Above the fold dominates.** One headline change tripled throughput (JfcIUxa8Nn0).
- **Damaging admissions:** "statement BUT statement." (JDR-R--4HhM) Whatever comes after "but" is what's believed.
- **Show, don't tell. Be specific on negatives.** Sell the "don't": what keeps happening if they don't buy (HwPXctiw_rY, Mst4hreQYl0).
- **Readability:** below 5th grade, ideally 3rd. Rewriting follow-up emails to a lower grade level gave +50% conversion (HxEQCHpZzHk). Ninth-grade copy loses ~⅔ of readers (JDR-R--4HhM).

### Email
- **Email is cheap follow-up on leads you've already paid for.** Cadence ~3×/week per the best emailers he talked to. Start monthly if you're at zero. Plain text, one tactic, under ~200 words, a PS. Segment by opt-in qualifiers (pLhQOYMGa88).
- **Quarterly list re-engagement:** a challenge or lead magnet with a deadline. Test on ~10–15% of the list first. Once you have 4 tested promos, reuse the calendar yearly (OpeN4O5myIg).
- **Not emailing is "saying no to money"** Skool added email follow-up and got a 40% conversion lift (pLhQOYMGa88).
- **Always have a promotion running on holidays.** A 3-day peak is worth an extra month (mHrAjWni65E, YUXLJauT4eY).

### Brand
- **Pair with what the largest share of your *ideal* audience likes,** and judge it by net money. Pick 75/25 over 25/75 (VQM3DrnVTcs, Jmkq5RLjm0U).
- **Every association trades some audience for others.** Decline pairings that disenfranchise your core (9unucIBuNio).
- **A premium brand needs a dialed product.** Brand earns the first purchase and the product earns the repeat (VQM3DrnVTcs).
- **Measure brand strength by the premium you can charge on a commodity** (VPre_XMgKjs). Pricing-power test (Buffett): if a 10% raise needs a prayer, it's a bad business (4Yz8ggEv0NU, VQM3DrnVTcs).
- **Reviews loop:** surprise gift plus a third-party ask, merch for reviews, a discount at point of sale for a verified review. "You literally get paid to get reviews" (4twK8Yl4iUI). Ask for testimonials at the moment of the result, not months later (7wN8n3qrqnQ).
- **Haters:** ignore (default) → agree → agree and one-up. Never attack back, only reply to people bigger than you, and "drown it out" with more value (UxQJ64BNuco, PZjJEQMgyMM, cGup9yYrOoc).

## Playbooks

### A. Start or fix organic content (service/expert business), from MD5-HByRxoA, UGEc9-7X3OQ, JOfsujnXxCg and eVY4Zo-hpqU
1. Fix your bio and pinned post so they prove who you are (lEIqyLE4iOY).
2. List the top 20% of customers' problems and the sales-call FAQs. These are your topics.
3. Post on 1–2 platforms where your avatar is. Aim for 1 a day minimum, building toward the Rule of 100 (100 minutes a day).
4. Use Proof → Promise → Plan intros, lists/steps/stories, clear packaging, and no CTAs in the content.
5. Track opt-ins, applications and sales per piece. Ignore views. Review weekly and make more of what converts.
6. Clip the winners into ads with a short CTA. Repost last year's best.

### B. Scale paid creative, from j2TZMFkj71Q, fSbqaTlWaYI, m5ordaa7NN4 and MH-IMJxbUY4
1. Bank 50+ hooks from your top organic content and top ads in any industry (Ds_Qp2U5I8U).
2. Produce hundreds of ads: founder winners × settings, community screenshots, UGC, AI statics.
3. Test hooks most, and put ~80% of effort into reskinning winners.
4. When CPA rises, move hooks down the awareness ladder (pain/problem-aware) and widen the people shown in the ads.
5. Optimize on purchases and cost per sale. Raise spend while the back end pays it back within ~30 days.

### C. Build a brand on purpose, from VQM3DrnVTcs, 9unucIBuNio and Jmkq5RLjm0U
1. Define the *who* (ideal customer) and what they already like (outcomes, people, values).
2. Pick the core topic with the strongest positive affinity across the largest share of them.
3. Repeatedly pair yourself with those things in content, ads and partners. Prune random pairings.
4. Keep micro-promises in free content so people believe the macro promise.
5. Check pricing power and ad CTR over 6–18 months, not immediate ROAS.

### D. Monetize an existing list or audience, from OpeN4O5myIg, pLhQOYMGa88, mHrAjWni65E and oRqnTOT9ZG8
1. Segment the list and run a 7-day challenge on 10–15% of it, with a deadline and a big promise.
2. Split-test the subject, name and landing page, then roll it to the full list.
3. Add a holiday promo calendar: a loss leader, discounted gift cards, a scarce high-ticket package (~5% of customers) or free-with-prepay. Name it with MAGIC.
4. For audiences, go affiliate → sponsorship → equity partnership → your own brand. Define deliverables precisely.

### E. Learn a new platform fast, from fd-hi3NqMYo
1. Hire a basic agency for 3–6 months to force cadence.
2. Then hire a premium agency on a 6-month "teach us" contract (e.g. $6K × 6), documenting everything.
3. Build in-house until it matches, downgrade the agency to consulting, then cut it.

## Anti-patterns
- **Chasing views or followers.** "Subscribers don't mean sales." Going broad killed Gym Launch organic sales for ~18 months (0S5xsICW8qg, 21flGkcZO3A, lEIqyLE4iOY).
- **Making content "for us"** (the team's ideas) instead of the avatar. Assuming viewers know you. Inside jokes (Jmkq5RLjm0U, JOfsujnXxCg).
- **Educators copying entertainers' tactics** (curiosity-gap packaging, headline-matching intros) (HVOubeXUcx0, UGEc9-7X3OQ).
- **CTAs and "right hooks" in every piece.** Over-pitching a small audience. Draining the audience early (UGEc9-7X3OQ, MD5-HByRxoA, 5cOwh-8scu8).
- **Teaching what you haven't done.** GPT-generated advice with no proof (eVY4Zo-hpqU, XsWSvz-aewA).
- **"Majoring in the minors"** with production, lighting and backgrounds. Perfecting content instead of posting it (ZTSI3DDP_4A, prj1bnTAM8A).
- **Running a handful of creatives** and capping spend "for no good reason" while profitable (j2TZMFkj71Q).
- **Abandoning winning ads early.** Polished ads on short-form. Concluding video doesn't work from bad video (fSbqaTlWaYI, MH-IMJxbUY4).
- **Ads about the company instead of the customer.** Late or weak brand attribution. CTA only in the audio (npi7UeOE_0o).
- **Incongruence between the ad promise and the landing page** (HwPXctiw_rY, kGfzLjPNsBU).
- **Buying fake followers.** It poisoned his Facebook distribution (wR8KoE8u1p0).
- **Accidental branding** through random pairings. Branding in the negative ("I am not X") (VQM3DrnVTcs, UxQJ64BNuco).
- **Not emailing,** or treating leads as dead after the first follow-up (pLhQOYMGa88, OpeN4O5myIg).
- **Lying or half-truth marketing** (FMzKk73iUhw, ZTSI3DDP_4A).

## Worked examples
- **Registered dietitian.** 5,800 Instagram followers and ~19 likes a post. She takes home just under $1M a year with a $39 code sheet (10+ sales a day) plus a ~$4–5K offer (0S5xsICW8qg, wR8KoE8u1p0).
- **Alex's 40 months.** ~$4M spent, ~35,000 pieces of content, 7.8M followers, 2B impressions, 1M+ books. The return to narrow business content raised RPM 68% and opt-ins 26% within weeks (HVOubeXUcx0, Jmkq5RLjm0U).
- **Email re-engagement.** A 7-day challenge sent to ~15% of a 3.3M-person list produced $2.4M in 5 days. Rerun on another segment the next month: $2.2M (OpeN4O5myIg).
- **Money Models launch.** $105M+ in 72 hours. ~2,800 ads made, editors scaled from 5 to 15, and ad spend reached ~$500K/day at the end (0lMn_-EXyhQ).
- **Sticker-education launch** (Mim Jenkinson). Pitching annual-only with a physical kit took annual uptake from ~10% to 30% and roughly doubled AOV. A UGC activation task makes every customer an ad (j2TZMFkj71Q).
- **Reviews for a new gym location.** $5 overstock shirts in exchange for Yelp, Google and Facebook reviews gave ~15 five-star reviews overnight (4twK8Yl4iUI).
- **Leads book ads.** ~6% CTR at about $1 per signup (4Yz8ggEv0NU).
