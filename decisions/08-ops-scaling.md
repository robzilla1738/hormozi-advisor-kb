# How do I scale the business, and what is the constraint right now?

**Bottom line:** A business grows to its nearest constraint. Find the single biggest one and give it all your attention: More of what works → Better → only then New (["My business has stopped growing..what should I do?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f_ceDZqhKbw) (f_ceDZqhKbw), [4 Steps to Unf*** Your Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kloJJeiysxg) (kloJJeiysxg), [It's Actually Pretty Easy to Get Ahead of 99% of People](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TFxT3G5jwtU) (TFxT3G5jwtU)). Most stuck owners are doing too many things, adding products, locations or businesses when they should fix retention, unit economics and the people. Scale means one business, one priority, a model that works without you, and redundancy against single points of failure ([Entrepreneurs, stop starting new businesses. Fix the one you've got.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s59jbVBprn0) (s59jbVBprn0), [Give Me 47 minutes, And I'll Fix Your Small Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sjt5G3YPjmY) (sjt5G3YPjmY), [If I Wanted to Create a Business That Runs Itself, Here's What I'd Do](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4Pw66_490) (mr4Pw66_490)).

## Diagnose first

**Questions and numbers to collect:**
1. **Supply- or demand-constrained?** It's never both at once, and the constraint swings back and forth like an accordion ([How to Grow Your Business So FAST it Makes Your Accountant Nervous](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sGv2BTUCcCM) (sGv2BTUCcCM)).
2. **The four levers**: traffic, conversion, price, churn. Every activity must "ladder up" to one of them (kloJJeiysxg). Equivalent chain: advertising → schedule/show/close → price → churn ([If you're not unbelievably rich yet, this is why](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6y0nYVZgwE) (h6y0nYVZgwE)).
3. **Hypothetical max** (recurring models): new customers per month ÷ monthly churn % = the customer ceiling. If you don't know your hypothetical max, you are fooling yourself (f_ceDZqhKbw).
4. **LTGP:CAC**, and how manual attraction, conversion and delivery are ([I Just Learned This After 14 Years of Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uYds0zcAFWM) (uYds0zcAFWM)).
5. **Revenue retention** is his "gold standard" metric (uYds0zcAFWM). Logo retention by model ([How To Grow ANY Business FASTER (Masterclass)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qsXxckCbci0) (qsXxckCbci0)).
6. **Unit economics per location/rep/coach**: margin, profit per unit, build-out cost vs. profit ([The Only 4 Ways To Scale A Service Business (PICK ONE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O1As2zxy0es) (O1As2zxy0es), [7 figure CONFIDENTIAL meeting LEAKED - business growth STRATEGY exposed...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8olk4-VFP8) (_8olk4-VFP8)).
7. **Concentration risks**: key man, channel (>50% of leads), customer (≥20% of revenue), vendor (sjt5G3YPjmY).
8. **Founder dependence**: how long can you leave without decline? Use the 1-month / 2-month / 3-month / 6-month tests (mr4Pw66_490, [I STARTED AND SOLD 6 BUSINESSES BY AGE 32...my boring formula..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sEQNnsaeiAs) (sEQNnsaeiAs), [I Reverse Engineered the Perfect Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HKbFUWJwEG0) (HKbFUWJwEG0)).
9. **"What's stopping us from 5x?"** Until you can answer it clearly, nothing else matters (s59jbVBprn0).

**Stage map** ([The 4 Stages of Entrepreneurship](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R7Fin9HFhoM) (R7Fin9HFhoM), uYds0zcAFWM, [The Talent Grid - How I Recognize Skill in Self & Team](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpKz22P84iM) (gpKz22P84iM)):

| Stage | Job | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| $0–1M | One product, one avatar, one channel, until you can do it "in your sleep." (R7Fin9HFhoM) Under ~$100K/mo, you mostly need more people to know you exist (h6y0nYVZgwE) | Shiny objects, branding, rebrands |
| $1–3M "the swamp" | You need help you can't afford. Generate extra (even unscalable) profit to fund 2–3 shots at a hire. Narrow the avatar and pick 1–3 deliverables (uYds0zcAFWM, qsXxckCbci0) | Overextension (2nd location, 2nd business), stepping out too soon |
| $1–10M | A value ladder of cross-sells and upsells to the same customer, not new businesses. Higher LTV unlocks channels. Leaders needed ~$3M (R7Fin9HFhoM, gpKz22P84iM) | Mass-market margin compression. Pause around $1–5M to build customer success ([Biggest mistake that stopped me from getting past $30M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EUW3rMp-Uvg) (EUW3rMp-Uvg)) |
| $10–30M | Professionalize (HR, legal, accounting, IT, systems) without killing innovation. Add a 2nd channel at $10M+ (R7Fin9HFhoM, [How I Scaled My Sales Team [Compensation, Recruiting & Training]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=okA9Yt2KZuk) (okA9Yt2KZuk)) | Channel dependence, one fat customer |
| $30–100M+ | "Souls": intrapreneurs running profit centers around a shared ops core. Team of teams with P&L leaders (R7Fin9HFhoM, [26 Harsh Lessons I Learned in 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YONIUpk-kAs) (YONIUpk-kAs), [A BILLIONAIRE told me how to make BILLION DOLLARS...here's my plan...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_x6n-CmZqjo) (_x6n-CmZqjo)) | Keeping decision control |

**By model (qsXxckCbci0, O1As2zxy0es):**
- **E-commerce**: constrained by free cash flow. Line up lending partners and redundancy at every layer. A strong product has 60–70% of a cohort buying again. Spend ~70/30 brand vs. direct.
- **Service**: design for higher gross margin as a cushion. Reinvest in talent and brand. Go premium unless you choose low-cost from day one. There are four scaling vehicles: private chain, franchise, licensing, software.
- **Education/info**: don't stretch payment plans past the delivered value.
- **Software**: keep >60% of logos by year-end (consumer) and ideally >100% revenue retention. Hire 10–100× engineers. Keep dev in-house.
- **Coaching**: rarely scales past ~$30M on hired coaches. Defer the key-man fix until ~$4M/yr ([If I Wanted To Scale A Service Business In 2026, Here's What I'd Do](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5MExtki_VI) (N5MExtki_VI)).

## Core principles
1. **Grow to the nearest constraint and work on #1 only.** Reassess after solving it, because fixing #1 often makes #2–5 irrelevant (TFxT3G5jwtU, f_ceDZqhKbw).
2. **More → Better → New.** "More" of what works is the best risk-adjusted move. Change has a fixed cost and a variable reward ([If You're in Your 20s or 30s, Here's How to Win (at Anything)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0lMn_-EXyhQ) (0lMn_-EXyhQ), kloJJeiysxg, [How They Keep You POOR Forever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yflKMUffctE) (yflKMUffctE)).
3. **Every change costs ~20% in performance**, so only make changes with big upside. Prefer medium-impact/high-confidence bets (uYds0zcAFWM, [You're Setting Goals Wrong](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XwzU4RikbGs) (XwzU4RikbGs)).
4. **Problems before opportunities.** Solving problems pays off almost for sure. Opportunities grow your problems too ([The REAL Reason You’re Not Making as Much Money As You Want](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNiXk_3C_Io) (zNiXk_3C_Io), [The Biggest Mistake Everyone is Making](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLkFOaxIdxM) (MLkFOaxIdxM)).
5. **Do less to grow.** Attention, not time, is the scarce resource. Run a "season of no" ([Millionaire explains why time is NOT the most precious resource...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ZEZWuVC8-8) (6ZEZWuVC8-8), [The anti-routine...[what billionaires use to win]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRyGIzW9d9k) (lRyGIzW9d9k), [The SEASON OF NO (What it Takes to Win)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ueJg14gQLuc) (ueJg14gQLuc)).
6. **One business, one CEO seat.** "You can't be CEO of two companies" (s59jbVBprn0, [I OWN 6 multimillion dollar companies...this is how I do it...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V7oUcAfof34) (V7oUcAfof34), [I Built My $100M Business WRONG...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKCrYIWPHLc) (sKCrYIWPHLc)). Start a second business only after passing the owner test (MLkFOaxIdxM).
7. **Fix the product and back end (the "sandwich") before pouring on marketing.** LTV funds acquisition ([How to make SO much money it makes you sick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I64gYLBrics) (I64gYLBrics), [Alex Hormozi's Advice on Branding in 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGq0uVspNms) (LGq0uVspNms), f_ceDZqhKbw).
8. **Simple scales, fancy fails.** Complexity is "the child of lazy thinking." Add a feature only when the customer needs it ([Less is More: The Magic of a Simple Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-JLN1d1ZKUU) (-JLN1d1ZKUU), MLkFOaxIdxM, [Big Business Owner Shares 1 YR of Wealth Lessons in 63min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy8XX8EuEnA) (Fy8XX8EuEnA)).
9. **Build to sell, even if you won't**: the company must maintain AND grow without you (V7oUcAfof34, sEQNnsaeiAs, HKbFUWJwEG0).
10. **Redundancy is insurance, not waste.** Remove single points of failure (sjt5G3YPjmY, [Everything We Learned From Making $106 Million in 72 Hours](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EuMu6LaxBSk) (EuMu6LaxBSk)).
11. **Execution, not strategy, is the bottleneck.** "Being advanced means you never don't do the basics" (yflKMUffctE, [We fired everyone who was late...this is why..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rX5zKeQu7ow) (rX5zKeQu7ow)).
12. **Low volatility over the big win.** "Do not risk the empire for a pot of gold" ([#1 Lesson for Young Entrepreneurs from $100M CEO | Alex Hormozi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FTgJ0mQi0uU) (FTgJ0mQi0uU), [Top 10 Fast Business Lessons From My Last 10 Years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WsYgWC7NmO8) (WsYgWC7NmO8)).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Prioritization & change
- **Top-priority test**: "Is there anything else we could do with the resources we have that would give that kind of a return… in the next 30 days?" If not, it becomes the only priority. Remove the normal work rather than stacking the priority on top of it (TFxT3G5jwtU).
- **More than one priority = no priorities** (XwzU4RikbGs). Small businesses (≤10–20 staff): about one big change per year. If one move could double the business, don't do three (0lMn_-EXyhQ).
- **Only take bets with ≥20% upside**, to cover the guaranteed cost of change (uYds0zcAFWM, 0lMn_-EXyhQ). Assume the downside will happen. Example: a new follow-up sequence vs. a proven control at +10% upside / −50% downside means no ([A Hard Learned Lesson (ALEX HORMOZI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Kt-EYieNko) (5Kt-EYieNko)).
- **Leave anything at benchmark alone** (XwzU4RikbGs). Don't judge a volume test after 1–2 days (0lMn_-EXyhQ).
- **Spend 4 hrs/day on the constraint** (6 days a week, before or after the team) until it's solved (h6y0nYVZgwE, [You're Not Behind: My System for Outworking Everyone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gD0X-PLax5I) (gD0X-PLax5I)). Accept 1–5 days with no visible progress (TFxT3G5jwtU).
- **Match cadence to priority**: move from weekly to end of day or twice daily. Ask for estimates in minutes and hours, not days (TFxT3G5jwtU). Pull "end of week" to end of day ([No BS Business Advice to Get Rich in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnbHgYYHzyE) (cnbHgYYHzyE), [A DOER's approach to PROCRASTINATION](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oW6JkqL6sdM) (oW6JkqL6sdM)).
- **Finish the ~25 obvious basics** (email, outreach, content, sales training, call reviews, onboarding) before anything new (yflKMUffctE).
- **New-thing test**: "If we tripled customers in your current business, would you still want the new thing?" If no, fix the current business (s59jbVBprn0).
- **New product lines**: don't launch one until a leader "as smart as you" runs it (R7Fin9HFhoM). Check whether it just splits core sales ([7 Secrets Behind Chick-fil-A’s $50B Empire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIH1w-KuATk) (TIH1w-KuATk)). Survey customers first (WsYgWC7NmO8).
- **Don't bet a $3–5M company on a non-core opportunity** (WsYgWC7NmO8).
- **Goals**: conservative goals you're sure to hit, with room to overachieve (WsYgWC7NmO8). 10–20% per quarter compounds hugely ([I emailed myself all of my failures for the last 5 years...this is what I learned..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQGHCG8d1So) (QQGHCG8d1So)). Planning to double in 5 years as PE does "removes frenetic energy" (Fy8XX8EuEnA). *Tension: A248pGXTSoY and ZklXJzHzW2U push "unreasonable" timelines ("6 months of work in 60 days"). [inference] Reconcile them as conservative outcome goals with unreasonable execution speed.*

### Supply vs. demand
- **Supply-constrained → raise prices 50–100%.** Double the price and lose 30% of clients, and you earn more while serving fewer (sGv2BTUCcCM). If you're full and not profitable, raise the price (h6y0nYVZgwE, cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **An 85–90% close rate → raise the price** ([This Video Should Be Required Viewing for Business Owners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uaLNfijnp-8) (uaLNfijnp-8)).
- **Retention great but growth flat → leave the product alone** and move people and cash to acquisition (sGv2BTUCcCM).
- **Churn high → fix retention before scaling acquisition** (f_ceDZqhKbw). Safe = you'd survive 3–4 months with zero new sales (I64gYLBrics, LGq0uVspNms). Target <3% monthly churn for recurring service ([How I became #1 in a "saturated" industry in my 20s...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y3T4GqoH0Us) (y3T4GqoH0Us)).
- **Before doubling the sales team or ad spend, check every funnel stage for leaks** (sGv2BTUCcCM).

### Unit economics & LTGP:CAC
- **The LTGP:CAC you need depends on how manual the model is**: fully leveraged 3:1 is OK; manual across attraction, conversion and delivery needs ≥20:1 (target 30+, "at the very least" 15:1). At 5–8:1 in a manual model, keep tinkering in one location. Most of his wealth was made at 30:1+ (uYds0zcAFWM). *This supersedes the generic "3:1" rule for service and local businesses.*
- **Mass-market margin compression** (illustrative): $1M→$10M takes margin from 40% to 30%, and $10M→$30M from 20% to 10% (EUW3rMp-Uvg).
- **Scalability needs enough gross margin and volume to pay for top talent** (sEQNnsaeiAs).
- **Brick-and-mortar chains need an operator on a 10–20% profit share** (~$75–100K/yr). The unit must pay that and still profit (h6y0nYVZgwE).

### Locations, expansion & vehicles
- **No second location until the first passes the away test.** Thresholds vary by video: 1 month at minimum, 6 months preferred (cnbHgYYHzyE); 3 months away and growing (mr4Pw66_490); 6 months away and still growing (h6y0nYVZgwE). *The later videos converge on ~6 months; use 6 as the bar and 1 month as the floor.*
- **Don't open another location at ~$30K/yr profit each**: "This shouldn't even be discussion." Corporate units need 30%+ margins before anyone buys in. Get one location to ~$40K/mo profit without you first, even if it takes 1–2 years (_8olk4-VFP8).
- **Close break-even locations that aren't the future.** Bring the best people over ([Building a $3,500,000 Business for a Stranger in 51 Mins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BMF2fWHyDrg) (BMF2fWHyDrg)).
- **National expansion**: can current infrastructure handle ~20 sales/week? ([Helping 4 Educational Business Owners Build a $1M Business in 25 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYpTRiRqS1Y) (BYpTRiRqS1Y))
- **Service vehicle choice** (O1As2zxy0es): private chain if a location costs less than ~6 months of a location's profit; franchise if build-out is very expensive (you need 100+ units to make real money, and 90–95% never get there); licensing if you're cash-poor and the IP is easy to copy; software only with an in-house software co-founder and ≥80% yearly retention. *The 80% licensing-retention threshold is partly garbled in the captions.*
- **Expansion directions**: up market, down market, adjacent, broader, narrower, or get better. Niche down when new. Go broad only with champions in several verticals. Below $10M, don't worry about TAM ([5 Ways to Scale ANY Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M2VP64Tc60w) (M2VP64Tc60w)).

### Concentration & redundancy (sjt5G3YPjmY, HKbFUWJwEG0)
- **Channel**: >50% of leads from one source is a risk. Below ~$10M it's fine; at $10M+ add a second channel (okA9Yt2KZuk). Don't kill the main channel to start a new one, and copy where same-size peers acquire customers (HKbFUWJwEG0).
- **Customer**: no customer ≥20% of revenue (he prefers ≤5%). One whale at 20–30% of revenue can be 100% of profit (HKbFUWJwEG0).
- **Contracts with big customers**: breakup fee ≈10–20% of contract value, notice period 6–12 months (sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Payment processors**: keep several approved, each able to handle full monthly volume. Processing failure is the one true short-term existential crisis (sjt5G3YPjmY, [13 Years Of Brutally Honest Business Advice in 90 Mins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KhFlD54nQrY) (KhFlD54nQrY)).
- **Keep the core economic engine in-house** (marketing, sales, delivery, and dev for software). Accounting, HR and payroll can be outsourced (sjt5G3YPjmY, [Helping 6 Business Owners Scale in 33 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EqJD2o-Mnk) (0EqJD2o-Mnk)).
- **One-shot events**: backups for the backups (EuMu6LaxBSk).

### Systems, time & tools
- **Sizing capacity**: activities per customer × minutes × inflow, mapped onto each role's calendar ([Simple Operational Framework (ALEX HORMOZI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOY6ZzBMb_4) (JOY6ZzBMb_4)). Coaching example: 4 × 90-min sessions/day, 2 days/week ≈ 120 clients ≈ ~$3M (N5MExtki_VI).
- **"It's easier to change a cadence than a ratio"** If you change the ratio, do it once and decisively (N5MExtki_VI). A small group stops being small after ~7 (N5MExtki_VI).
- **Elon's algorithm for any process**: question requirements → delete → simplify → accelerate → automate. Never automate first ([My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2027 - The Algorithm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZTSI3DDP_4A) (ZTSI3DDP_4A)).
- **Automation must pay back**: a 10-year payback was rejected ([Money Lessons I Learned In Marriage.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oK2_u-uS_Bo) (oK2_u-uS_Bo)). AI: automate one end-to-end workflow yourself in a fixed time block ([How to Use AI in Your Business in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fr78adfAnuA) (fr78adfAnuA)). Think in workflows, not roles ([How to Win With AI in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9q5ojtkqsBs) (9q5ojtkqsBs)).
- **Invest early in click→close data.** HubSpot at ~$36K/yr is "worth more than one frontline employee" (Fy8XX8EuEnA). Track only data whose change would change your behavior (cnbHgYYHzyE, zNiXk_3C_Io).
- **Hand off any task eating 10–15 hrs/week**, even if it isn't a full-time role (ueJg14gQLuc).
- **Delegation tools**: spending authority box, if-this-then-that trees, Shadow → Supervise → Support (mr4Pw66_490).
- **Customers don't have your context.** You tire of your business before they notice it (XwzU4RikbGs).

## Playbooks

### A. "My business has stopped growing" (f_ceDZqhKbw)
1. Calculate the hypothetical max from new sales/month and churn. If you're near it, the constraint is retention (f_ceDZqhKbw).
2. Supply or demand? If supply, raise prices 50–100% and build capacity. If demand, work on traffic and conversion (sGv2BTUCcCM).
3. Walk the chain: ads → schedule → show → close → price → churn. Compare each step to a benchmark and pick the worst gap (h6y0nYVZgwE, [I Doubled A Business in 60 Days to Show It's Not Luck (Part 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c_6BrF7jOGk) (c_6BrF7jOGk)).
4. Make it the only priority, cut the normal work, and spend 4 hrs/day on it (TFxT3G5jwtU, h6y0nYVZgwE).
5. Apply More, then Better, then New. Reassess after it's fixed (kloJJeiysxg).
6. If nothing moved after months of tinkering, look at who is doing the work. Same task, same person, 12 weeks undone = the doer is the problem (LGq0uVspNms, rX5zKeQu7ow).

### B. Second location / multi-unit
1. Unit check: margin ≥30%, profit comfortably covers an operator's 10–20% profit share, LTGP:CAC fits how manual the model is (_8olk4-VFP8, h6y0nYVZgwE, uYds0zcAFWM).
2. Away test: leave for 1–6 months. It must hold and grow (cnbHgYYHzyE).
3. Push the current unit's profit up first: price, upfront payment, speed to consult (uaLNfijnp-8).
4. Pick the vehicle (chain / franchise / license / software) from build-out cost vs. profit and IP protectability (O1As2zxy0es).
5. Consolidate losers before expanding (BMF2fWHyDrg, _8olk4-VFP8).

### C. Make the business run without you
1. Time study and R/Y/G, then hand off greens (mr4Pw66_490).
2. Replace each hat: sales, marketing (systemized so it isn't your face), product, decisions, spokesperson. That may mean six hires (sEQNnsaeiAs, mr4Pw66_490).
3. "Two steps forward": delegate what you do now AND hire for what you'd do next, then move to chairman ([I stepped down as CEO...and created a PASSIVE INCOME machine..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3tH9Sz1fEc) (s3tH9Sz1fEc)).
4. Test: 2 months away, no calls; then 6 months of growth without you jumping back in (sEQNnsaeiAs, HKbFUWJwEG0).
5. Don't step out at $1–3M (s3tH9Sz1fEc). Re-entering later can displace your leaders ([the brutal truth behind passive income](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gZvW1Hghv6U) (gZvW1Hghv6U)).

### D. Derisk for scale/sale
Audit the four concentration risks (key man, channel, customer, vendor). Add a second processor, write contracts with breakup fees, add a second channel at $10M+, and run audit-ready financials with automated metrics (sjt5G3YPjmY, HKbFUWJwEG0). See 11-deals-equity-acquisitions for the exit side.

### E. Mass-market business plateauing ($1–5M)
Pause scaling. Hire a CS director tracking time-to-value, NPS and health scores, all aimed at referrals. Then resume (EUW3rMp-Uvg). Keep advertising low while the product is mediocre (KhFlD54nQrY).

## Anti-patterns
- **Starting a new business or product line when the core gets hard.** "Businesses grow to the level of the entrepreneur's incompetence" (s59jbVBprn0, MLkFOaxIdxM, yflKMUffctE).
- **CEO of two companies.** He calls it his "terrible mistake" (V7oUcAfof34, sKCrYIWPHLc).
- **Overexpansion**, one of the top reasons businesses die (mr4Pw66_490, cnbHgYYHzyE).
- **Lots of small tests and CRO hacks** that aren't the constraint between levels (XwzU4RikbGs).
- **Building a second, low-touch product to escape a capacity problem.** The Couplepreneurs' $5K product broke their economics (N5MExtki_VI).
- **Automating before deleting and simplifying** (ZTSI3DDP_4A). SOP-ing everything under $10M (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Rushing.** "Rush is imaginary" except in winner-take-all network businesses (uYds0zcAFWM). Chasing 10× in 90 days is "what kills people" (LGq0uVspNms).
- **Rebranding when nobody knows you yet** (I64gYLBrics).
- **Building every feature customers ask for**: "customers will hang themselves with their own requests" (qsXxckCbci0).
- **Outsourcing software dev to a third party** (O1As2zxy0es, sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Reacting to a competitor's ad or a departing employee.** "Most times no reaction is the best decision" (FTgJ0mQi0uU).
- **Side deals bolted onto the core**, which signal "an undisciplined entrepreneur" to investors (ueJg14gQLuc).

## Worked examples
- **Trophy Outlet**: $1.72M revenue, ~$110K profit (6.4% net), 40% gross margin. The levers were consolidation, pricing and media. At a 6.4% margin, small price moves double profit. The owner had to pick one model: gag-gift media (fast cash) or B2B leagues (exit value) (BMF2fWHyDrg).
- **Smile Magic Dentistry**: 3 locations, 1 doctor, ~$2M revenue, ~47% profit, supply-constrained. The plan: raise the price from $28K to $35K, push upfront payment from 30–50% to 80–90%, and pull consults forward from 15–30 days out, lifting close rate from ~70% to 85–90% (worth ~20% more revenue). No new locations until one runs without the founder doctor (uaLNfijnp-8).
- **Couplepreneurs**: ~$480K/yr, 42 clients. Unlimited Slack ate ~40% of their time. Fix: one $25K offer (1-on-4 sessions, 2 getaways a year). Capacity of ~120 clients ≈ $3M, goal 150 × ~$30K ≈ $4.5M (N5MExtki_VI).
- **Gym with 3 locations** at ~$30K/yr profit each, wanting to franchise. Verdict: consolidate. One focused location could beat all three (_8olk4-VFP8).
- **Gym Launch outbound**: a second channel built over ~14 months (BDR → SDS → closer). Buyers had flagged churn, founder-as-brand and channel dependence (okA9Yt2KZuk).
- **Key-man fix → $46.2M sale**: he credits removing key-man risk for that valuation (sjt5G3YPjmY).

## Metrics to watch
- **7 days**: the single constraint metric (e.g. show rate, close rate, leads/day); hours/day the founder spent on it; open priorities (target: 1).
- **30 days**: hypothetical max vs. current customers; monthly churn (<3% for recurring service); funnel stage conversion vs. benchmark; utilization per role; tasks still requiring the founder.
- **90 days**: revenue retention (gold standard); LTGP:CAC vs. the manual-model bar; % of leads from the top channel (<50%); largest customer % of revenue (<20%, ideally ≤5%); away-test result; margin per unit/location; changes made vs. their ≥20% expected upside.
