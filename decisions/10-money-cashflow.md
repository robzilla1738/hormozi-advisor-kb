# How should I manage cash flow, unit economics and my own money?

**Bottom line:** Rule #1 is don't go out of business, and cash flow is what prevents it ([How I Would Build a Business in 2026 (If I Had to Start Over)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i7bLRKwKSms) (i7bLRKwKSms), [If you're struggling with cash flow, Watch This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SvIcS-Q1Hl4) (SvIcS-Q1Hl4)). Run the business on gross-profit math: LTGP:CAC well above 3:1 (higher the more humans are in the loop), CAC recovered within 30 days from front-loaded cash, and service gross margins ≥80% ([The Mathematics of Business, Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A_tx40lNpf8) (A_tx40lNpf8), [How to Make Money So Fast It Feels ILLEGAL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-3PoOT4vOM) (k-3PoOT4vOM), [If You Don't Understand Margin, You Don't Understand Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xAMe0QBFhU) (9xAMe0QBFhU)). Personally, wealth is the ratio of income to expenses. Spend little and invest early surplus in skills and your own business, not passive investments, until wealth management beats your active income. Then take dividends out monthly, park them in boring index funds, and protect the downside ([The best way to invest your money...nobody will tell you this.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CRMT2yubBxc) (CRMT2yubBxc), [Warren Buffett’s #1 Rule for Business Owners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPEjYEihZ1s) (EPEjYEihZ1s), [How To Retire Early (and be in the top 1%)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnvOwA39dDg) (rnvOwA39dDg)).

## Diagnose first

**Business numbers to collect** ([Introduction To Marketing | Business Marketing 101](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cr0KdqZ954c) (Cr0KdqZ954c), [Million Dollar Equations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QGcjweehrvU) (QGcjweehrvU), A_tx40lNpf8):
1. **Fully loaded CAC**, by channel.
2. **LTGP**: lifetime value as *gross profit*, not revenue. Average churn and LTV over trailing 90 days / 6 / 12 months, not daily (QGcjweehrvU).
3. **30-day cash**: gross profit collected within 30 days of acquisition.
4. **Gross margin** per product and each product's share of sales ([I Reverse Engineered the Perfect Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HKbFUWJwEG0) (HKbFUWJwEG0)).
5. **Payback period** and **cash conversion cycle**.
6. **Hypothetical max**: inflow ÷ churn rate ([How to Scale Your Business Fast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZEjp2mP1U0) (XZEjp2mP1U0), QGcjweehrvU).
7. **Net free cash flow / owner earnings** and what the owner actually takes out monthly (EPEjYEihZ1s, [How gurus lie about how much MONEY they make...I'm calling it out..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=al_WXFDbN8I) (al_WXFDbN8I)).
8. **Runway**: when is payroll due vs. cash on hand? (SvIcS-Q1Hl4)

## Core principles
1. **LTGP:CAC is "the fundamental economic unit of the business."** All profit comes from it. If it's bad, he tosses the business out (QGcjweehrvU, [Business Owners: You NEED to Know This Number](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jzKpAtzKQ54) (jzKpAtzKQ54)).
2. **Get paid first.** Make more in the first 30 days than it costs to acquire the customer, so customers finance your growth ([Making Money is a Game (Here's the Cheat Code)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSQdjim8CsE) (nSQdjim8CsE), k-3PoOT4vOM, [Wealth Principle #1: Get Paid First](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZZZNsDs2G_s) (ZZZNsDs2G_s), [Alex Hormozi Slept on Gym Floors Before He Got Rich](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKvAQIe5vpo) (GKvAQIe5vpo)).
3. **Gross margin creates net margin.** It's the #1 health metric he checks. Service >80% (9xAMe0QBFhU, A_tx40lNpf8).
4. **"It comes from the back, not the front."** If you can't afford ~$100–200 to acquire a service customer, you have a monetization problem, not a marketing one ([4 Steps to Maximizing Revenue Generated Per Customer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-UvmyixFszw) (-UvmyixFszw)).
5. **Whoever makes the most per customer wins the ad auction.** CAC is similar among competent competitors (k-3PoOT4vOM, i7bLRKwKSms).
6. **Owner earnings (net free cash flow) are the real scoreboard.** "99 times out of 100 your business never actually sells", so get paid to grow (EPEjYEihZ1s, al_WXFDbN8I).
7. **Wealth is a ratio, not a number**: income vs. expenses (a0hUVFYzv3k, [I Spent $312,900 Living Like The 1% (Here's What I Learned)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKnAWYqMJ10) (vKnAWYqMJ10), [I lived on $0 income for 3 years growing my business...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x1CtbsEqxW0) (x1CtbsEqxW0)).
8. **Earn before you save; invest in skills first.** Early savings compound to little, and skills compound to a lot ([9 Money Beliefs of the Ultra Wealthy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0dBZbd13jM) (d0dBZbd13jM), [14 Money Mistakes to AVOID in Your 20's](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6SdmgIji30) (q6SdmgIji30), [Dangerously Honest Advice to Create Generational Wealth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m-k0_pQJ1fY) (m-k0_pQJ1fY), [The Ultra Rich Playbook [Legal & Tax-Free]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6x3re5-Ms1E) (6x3re5-Ms1E)).
9. **Attention is the scarcest asset.** Put it into the highest-growth vehicle, usually your business (CRMT2yubBxc, w5g0JiO7OdE).
10. **Protect the downside: "you only have to get rich once."** Multiply estimated downside by 3 (["I'm Broke... What Should I Do?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNl83INePXI) (KNl83INePXI), [No BS Advice to Get Rich in the Next 10 Years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZ-H_TjSzok) (oZ-H_TjSzok), vhOV_Od0A3M).
11. **Don't hire or spend ahead of growth.** Expectations, emotions and expenses feed each other ([“The 3 Evil Es” That Almost Killed My Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=noO8ERpgMr4) (noO8ERpgMr4)).
12. **Pay the taxes and grow equity.** 10× the value instead of saving 10% on taxes (w5g0JiO7OdE, [How the rich avoid paying taxes...MY strategy - Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhxiVIQp_ws) (YhxiVIQp_ws)).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Unit economics
- **LTGP:CAC minimums.** The videos differ; use the most specific one that applies:
  - Generic: >3:1, and 5:1 is great (Cr0KdqZ954c, QGcjweehrvU, XZEjp2mP1U0).
  - By humans in the loop: **3 / 6 / 9 / 12 : 1 for 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 humans** (A_tx40lNpf8).
  - Fully manual model: ≥20:1, target 30+, "at the very least" 15:1. Most of his wealth was made at 30:1+ (uYds0zcAFWM, 2024).
  - *The later videos (A_tx40lNpf8, uYds0zcAFWM) refine the 3:1 rule. Use 3:1 only for fully leveraged, low-touch models.*
- **Optimize the ratio, not the cheapest customer**: LTV $5,000 / CAC $500 (10:1) beats $1,000 / $200 (5:1) (i7bLRKwKSms).
- **At 100:1 with a reliable channel, scale hard.** "Jam as much as you possibly can through that machine" (jzKpAtzKQ54).
- **CAC budget from LTV**: at 3:1 on $5K LTV, spend "less than $2,000" (XZEjp2mP1U0).
- **Churn math**: going from 5% to 10% monthly churn halves lifetime revenue (QGcjweehrvU). $200/mo at 10% churn ≈ $2,000 per continuity customer (-UvmyixFszw).
- **B2B retention target >80% annual, measured on the original cohort.** $100/yr at 50% retention ≈ $200 LTV; at 80%, ≈ $500 (A_tx40lNpf8).

### 30-day cash / payback
- **Recover CAC within 30 days.** Every one of his businesses that got big did (A_tx40lNpf8, i7bLRKwKSms).
- **30-day cash thresholds** (two versions):
  - Minimum: 30-day gross profit : CAC ≥ 1:1, so you break even within the credit-card window and can use other people's money (Cr0KdqZ954c).
  - Stronger target: **30-day gross cash ≥ 2×(CAC + COGS)**, so each customer funds the next one (k-3PoOT4vOM).
  - *[inference] Treat 1:1 as the survival floor and 2× as the scaling target.*
- **Hard mode** example: a $99/mo membership with ~$500 CAC is about 5 months negative. Your options are then savings, debt, investors or going broke, so fix the money model instead (nSQdjim8CsE).
- **The cash-flow quadrant**, stacked on any front end: products, add-on services, prepay/financing, continuity (-UvmyixFszw). A free front end breaks even if you sell $100–200 of product per person (-UvmyixFszw).
- **Prepay**: at least a 10% discount for paying in full. "Buy 10 get 2" → ~15–20% take it; a moderate discount plus a bonus → ~30–40% prepay (A_tx40lNpf8). Instead of net-60, offer quarterly prepay with ~5% baked into the price (k-3PoOT4vOM).
- **Third-party financing** raises overall sales ~35% (per an Affirm contact), but it won't save a bad business (A_tx40lNpf8).
- **Layaway**: they set the payment plan, and work starts once it's paid off ([The 6 Levels of Making Money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMJIOK_FgJk) (rMJIOK_FgJk)).
- **Sell more 2 days after a purchase.** About 20% of customers will buy something significantly more expensive ("fractal 80/20") (nSQdjim8CsE).
- **Payment plans in education**: don't stretch $25K into $2K/mo forever ([How To Grow ANY Business FASTER (Masterclass)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qsXxckCbci0) (qsXxckCbci0)).

### Margin
- **Services >80% gross margin.** He won't get into a business below 80% (9xAMe0QBFhU, A_tx40lNpf8). On $100 of cost: 70% GM means a $333 price, 80% means $500, 90% means $1,000 (A_tx40lNpf8).
- **Fixing a $60 service with $20 cost (66% GM)**: cut the cost to $12 or raise the price to $100 for 80%. For brick-and-mortar netting ~12%, going from 66% to 80% GM takes net to ~26% (9xAMe0QBFhU). *That video has a math slip, corrected in the note [inference].*
- **Price on the value you create**, not your costs. 85% compete on price and 1% win, per the McKinsey figure he cites (k-3PoOT4vOM).
- **Price to delivered service**: when capacity forces 1-on-8 service, $100/mo unlimited needs to become ~$300–400 ([Reopening the Right Way (ALEX HORMOZI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOKl6dodMTg) (mOKl6dodMTg)).

### Reinvest vs. distribute
- **Reinvest only where ROIC beats the S&P.** At ~10% returns, "just put it in the S&P" (EPEjYEihZ1s). Active investments of your time need ≥3:1 and ideally 5:1 annual return on capital (QGcjweehrvU). Example: a $50K store returning $250K in 12 months is worth it ([How to Become Ultra Wealthy (4 Methods)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BO_59sGxztY) (BO_59sGxztY)).
- **Enterprise-value reinvestments can beat the S&P by a wide margin**: $100K of tech that adds ~3 turns of multiple on $1M profit adds +$3M EV, a 30× return ([How the top 1% make their money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fD-sxKiB30M) (fD-sxKiB30M)).
- **Take distributions monthly** above a cash floor and feel the deposit (EPEjYEihZ1s, al_WXFDbN8I, HKbFUWJwEG0). Reinvesting every dollar "gave myself an excuse" not to be profitable (x1CtbsEqxW0).
- **Don't grow faster than needed.** Irresponsible growth adds waste and risk (EPEjYEihZ1s).
- **Don't hire "in anticipation of growth."** 99% of the time growth should pay for the expense; commission-only reps are the exception (noO8ERpgMr4). But "as long as you have lead flow, sales people will always make you more than the price for them" ([Can I Save This Failing Theme Park in 90 Days?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRxVpjeHmeA) (xRxVpjeHmeA), A_tx40lNpf8).
- **If demand outruns capacity, briefly delay** to shore up infrastructure. "It's much easier to solve problems with cash in the bank" (noO8ERpgMr4).
- **Brick and mortar**: overspend on opening to fill capacity fast, e.g. a $40K launch budget instead of $10K (QGcjweehrvU). Plan the schedule on revenue that actually bills, minus another 10% (mOKl6dodMTg).
- **Raising capital**: bootstrap the first business. Raise only if network effects or long capital needs require it. VCs push aggressive growth: "you are their 1% bet" Franchising is the most expensive form of equity (BO_59sGxztY).
- **Debt**: acceptable only if paid off within a year with a plan ([How to Buy Anything You Want Without Going Broke](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=by3ZEoo-Quc) (by3ZEoo-Quc)). Borrowing against a growing business (recaps at ~70% LTV) gives tax-free liquidity, but if it declines you're in deep ([How wealthy people avoid paying taxes..my new plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=52tcB5FopAg) (52tcB5FopAg), 6x3re5-Ms1E). Ramsey: no debt, survive your experiments ([How to Get Rich [Full Interview with Dave Ramsey]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jvXOOddDg_s) (jvXOOddDg_s), guest).
- **Business cash reserves**: keep personal cash to fund the company through a shock. COVID was expected to last 12 weeks and lasted two years (x1CtbsEqxW0). Keep multiple payment processors ([Give Me 47 minutes, And I'll Fix Your Small Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sjt5G3YPjmY) (sjt5G3YPjmY)).

### Cash crisis rules
- **Payroll due within ~30 days**: fix opportunities and threats before strengths and weaknesses ("OTSW," not SWOT). Retention systems take ~12 weeks to pay off, so do them after the cash plays (SvIcS-Q1Hl4).
- **Fastest cash = sell more to existing customers.** A scarce, prepaid, high-touch access offer sold to ~20% of demand can raise $5–50K ([How To Make BUCKETS of Money In SERVICE Businesses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m-7VjocC76M) (m-7VjocC76M), SvIcS-Q1Hl4). Never sell out all your demand (m-7VjocC76M), and limit paid-in-full slots (SvIcS-Q1Hl4).
- **Make ONE front-loaded, urgent bet** to your list, then reinvest the cash into ads (xRxVpjeHmeA).
- **Renegotiate leases.** Ask for 3–6 months of free rent in a crisis. Keep only revenue-generating or revenue-retaining staff ([What I Would Do If I Had My Gyms (Do This in 2020) [ALEX HORMOZI]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ktgTBNlg7M) (3ktgTBNlg7M)).
- **Felt urgency is a red flag.** Extend the gap between feeling and action (noO8ERpgMr4).

## Playbooks

### A. Fix a broken money model (CAC > first-purchase cash)
1. Calculate CAC, 30-day gross profit, LTGP and payback (Cr0KdqZ954c).
2. Add the quadrant to the front end: product, add-on service, prepay/financing, continuity (-UvmyixFszw).
3. Offer a paid-in-full discount (≥10%) plus bonuses, and add third-party financing (A_tx40lNpf8).
4. Upsell within 2 days, and offer the ~20% a much more expensive option (nSQdjim8CsE).
5. Target 30-day cash ≥ CAC (floor), then ≥ 2×(CAC+COGS) (Cr0KdqZ954c, k-3PoOT4vOM).
6. Then scale ads. Gym example: a paid "free if you hit the goal" challenge with the fee credited to an annual membership pre-sold and opened each gym at capacity (GKvAQIe5vpo).

### B. Cash crisis (weeks of runway)
1. List opportunities and threats first (SvIcS-Q1Hl4).
2. Make a prepaid, scarce offer to existing customers and the list (m-7VjocC76M, xRxVpjeHmeA).
3. Renegotiate rent and vendors. Cut non-revenue roles (3ktgTBNlg7M).
4. Collect, then reinvest in ads and the process that caused the problem, e.g. retention (SvIcS-Q1Hl4).
5. Afterward: build a cash reserve and a second processor (x1CtbsEqxW0, sjt5G3YPjmY).

### C. Profitable owner deciding where cash goes
1. Set a cash floor in the business (opex months) [inference]; keep 6 months to 2 years personally (CRMT2yubBxc).
2. Calculate the ROIC of each reinvestment option. Fund it only if it beats ~10% (S&P) by a wide margin. Active bets need 3–5× (EPEjYEihZ1s, QGcjweehrvU).
3. Prefer spends that raise enterprise value: risk reduction, multiple expansion (fD-sxKiB30M).
4. Distribute the rest monthly into index funds (EPEjYEihZ1s, CRMT2yubBxc).
5. Keep lifestyle a small % of income (vKnAWYqMJ10).

### D. Broke individual → first $100K
Cut costs, reclaim time, pick a skill people already pay for (sales), learn it through iterations, spend on tools and attempts, and don't inflate your lifestyle ([If I Wanted to Make My First $100K in 2026, I’d Do This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfW6gL6hKhk) (jfW6gL6hKhk)). Study 4 hrs/day (YFA8AS5Cu2w). Commit to a 36-month season (YFA8AS5Cu2w, [How To Actually Get Rich In Your 20s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aFoMYz_jWcs) (aFoMYz_jWcs)).

## Anti-patterns
- **Calling no profit "reinvesting"** and chasing a mythical exit (EPEjYEihZ1s, x1CtbsEqxW0).
- **Hiring and spending ahead of growth** because of arbitrary expectations (noO8ERpgMr4).
- **Competing on price** (k-3PoOT4vOM).
- **Spending $100 to make $50 up front** with no follow-on purchases (k-3PoOT4vOM).
- **Gurus' inflated "value"**: lifetime revenue or contract value. A high EBITDA multiple usually means near-zero profit (al_WXFDbN8I).
- **Tax shelters**: he declined a captive-insurance play saving ~$250K/yr that the IRS later reversed (YhxiVIQp_ws, w5g0JiO7OdE).
- **Day trading**: he lost ~$200K by selling a position that came back, then paid ordinary-income tax for nothing (c5Lw12PXkgU).
- **Faking wealth**: the Ferrari or house for "networking" ([Why PRETENDING to be RICH will make you POOR..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebrcIErJi10) (ebrcIErJi10)). The Instagram 1% lifestyle costs only ~$300K/yr (vKnAWYqMJ10).
- **Selling your business to escape pain.** Fix the pain instead (52tcB5FopAg).
- **Building 100 hours of $100/mo passive income** when the same time could earn $10K actively (m-k0_pQJ1fY).
- **Startup debt pitched by wealth advisors** (BnvkMpUu-7c).
- **Fixed overhead too big for real MRR**, e.g. $6K rent on $10K MRR (mOKl6dodMTg).

## Worked examples
- **Gym Launch year 1**: ~100:1 LTV:CAC ($100K spend → $10M), later ~30:1. He's had four arbitrage windows above 30:1 (A_tx40lNpf8).
- **Hybrid gym cash play**: 150 members → ~120 trial → ~60 stay × $200/mo = $12K/mo, minus a 20% coach payout = ~$9,600/mo profit (SvIcS-Q1Hl4).
- **Boulder Adventure Park**: $22M build cost, $4.8M revenue, unprofitable, weeks from running out of cash. One front-loaded camp offer produced 3,700 leads and camps up 325% YoY. The plan is a 10:1 return play rerun year-round (xRxVpjeHmeA).
- **Margin fix**: $100 cost at 70/80/90% GM gives $233 / $400 / $900 profit per sale (A_tx40lNpf8).
- **Bridge loan**: $2.5M at 4%/month on a foreclosure building with independent offers of $5.1–5.6M, $300K earnest money and personal guarantees. ~27% after tax for him [inference math in note] (BnvkMpUu-7c).
- **Enterprise-value reinvestment**: $100K of tech that 5–10× delivery capacity takes a 5× multiple to 8× on $1M profit = +$3M (fD-sxKiB30M).

## Metrics to watch
- **7 days**: cash on hand vs. next payroll; cash collected (not booked); % paid in full; speed to lead.
- **30 days**: 30-day gross cash ÷ CAC; payback; gross margin per product; owner distribution taken; personal monthly savings.
- **90 days**: trailing-90-day LTGP:CAC vs. the humans-in-loop bar; churn and the hypothetical max; ROIC of reinvestments vs. S&P; net free cash flow trend; personal spend as % of income; net worth.
