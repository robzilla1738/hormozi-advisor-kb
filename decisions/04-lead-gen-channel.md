# Which lead-gen channel should I use, and how much volume do I need?

> Rebuilt from the hand-read notes (notes/<id>.md). Citations are video ids in manifest.json; open notes/<id>.md, then transcripts/<id>.md, to go deeper. **[inference]** marks synthesis or math that isn't Alex's.

**Bottom line:** There are only four things *you* can do to get customers: warm outreach, posting content, cold outreach and paid ads (the Core Four). There are also four groups of lead getters who do it for you: customers (referrals), employees, agencies and affiliates. Pick **one** channel, put the first 4 hours of every day into it at Rule-of-100 volume, and don't call it broken until your volume matches what practitioners do. A second, different channel comes only once the first one runs without you (around $1M/month). Most lead problems are really volume problems or LTV problems, not channel problems [The reason you are not successful...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2tHlHWgDRdQ) (2tHlHWgDRdQ), [How to Grow Your Business SO Fast in 2026 It Feels ILLEGAL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HxEQCHpZzHk) (HxEQCHpZzHk), [13 Years of No BS Business Advice in 79 Mins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oRMG_HpOAN4) (oRMG_HpOAN4), [How To Get Customers So Fast It Feels ILLEGAL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FMzKk73iUhw) (FMzKk73iUhw).

## Diagnose first

**Collect these numbers before choosing anything:**
- Share of new customers by source, compared with the share of your time spent on each source ("channel % vs time %") (nrounb8NlFQ).
- Primary actions per day: reach-outs, minutes of content and posts, ad dollars per day. Compare them with the Rule of 100 (HsQeQM1jUeg, u4kgZ3pf8cU).
- **Supply- or demand-constrained?** "If you doubled leads, could you handle them?" If you could, it's a lead problem. If you couldn't, build capacity first, because marketing more is pointless (OQf2Ba-Lp_4, jqo0lVveh98, MH-IMJxbUY4).
- Funnel by step: leads, appointments, shows, closes, cost per lead, CAC. Check against benchmarks before blaming the channel (h6y0nYVZgwE).
- LTV (lifetime gross profit) : CAC, and 30-day gross profit compared with CAC+COGS. Together these decide how hard you can push paid (XwZH-lOKG9c, jzKpAtzKQ54).
- Attribution: do you actually know where customers came from? If not, fix that before raising spend (nrounb8NlFQ, jqo0lVveh98).

**Branch by stage:**

| Stage | Channel posture | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue / first 5 customers | Warm outreach to everyone you know (100/day), ask for referrals rather than a sale, give the first ~5 away free as case studies | w7g08dVTwaE, CaiLcj6tzBQ, HsQeQM1jUeg |
| < ~$100K/month | "You mostly just need more people to know you exist." All focus on advertising. First 4 hours a day on ONE Core Four channel | h6y0nYVZgwE, oRMG_HpOAN4, HxEQCHpZzHk |
| < $1M/yr | One avatar, one product, one channel. Exception: small local markets (<~50K people in a 10-mile radius) probably need several channels | YtbHaogHlpY, hnmBXTyMFKI |
| ~$100K/month plateau | Stair-step: fix the leaky bucket (referrals, retention, repeat purchases) before 10×-ing the front end again | oRMG_HpOAN4, prj1bnTAM8A |
| ~$1M/month | Add a second, *different* channel once the first runs without you. Expect 6–12 months before it pays | oRMG_HpOAN4, oDK4g5na4Jw |
| $3–10M+ | Single-channel risk (>50% from one source) now matters. Paid hits diminishing returns, and getting "mega rich" needs decentralized word of mouth (affiliates, referrals) | sjt5G3YPjmY, 4Yz8ggEv0NU |

**Branch by model:**
- **Local service / brick and mortar:** paid ads are easiest here (high trust, offer-driven). Use a cheap front-end ($19–29 assessment, card on file), call within 60 seconds, plus referral partners. Content's job is conversion (do they look legit and nearby?), not acquisition (fSbqaTlWaYI, sBJppqCeFGI, h6y0nYVZgwE, --9kOXNCUdE).
- **High-ticket B2B / service:** outbound plus affiliates/partners, a lead magnet, and BANT qualification before calls. LinkedIn has the highest conversion per follower (Ds_Qp2U5I8U, wR8KoE8u1p0, XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **Info / coaching:** content builds the audience, paid ads ask, and affiliates or super-influencers compress years into weeks (Z2tgZC_XkT4, 60_7PU9JDIw).
- **SMB agency:** the ~$1.5–3K/month retainer is a "dead zone" (4–6 month stick). Go cheap (≤~$400/month, near-zero delivery) or go upmarket (jqo0lVveh98).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Volume and time
- **Rule of 100.** Every day: 100 warm reach-outs, or 100+ cold, or 100 minutes making and posting content, or $100/day on ads. Do it for 100 days and "you will have a business" (HsQeQM1jUeg, u4kgZ3pf8cU, prj1bnTAM8A).
- **Rule of 300** (Skool update). Make 1 post, leave 100 thoughtful comments where your avatar hangs out ("like mini ads"), then run the Rule of 100 on the new "lukewarm" list (HsQeQM1jUeg, prj1bnTAM8A).
- **4 hours a day, first thing,** on the primary acquisition input (6 days a week) while under ~$1–3M/yr (h6y0nYVZgwE, HxEQCHpZzHk, reisEL_D7xc). One talk says ~6 hours (YtbHaogHlpY) and another says 4 hours a day of promotion (0_Gf5v8DEMY). Treat 4 as the floor [inference].
- **Benchmarks for cold work:** ~1% on any cold activity, and a beginner may need 3× that volume (u4kgZ3pf8cU). Cold outbound normally converts 0.5–1% (nrounb8NlFQ). ~1,000 cold calls per deal for a beginner, with the numbers given as illustration only (w9Zvk70uj5Q).
- **Before saying a channel "doesn't work,"** compare your test size with practitioners' volumes. Mailing 300 flyers and quitting is the classic mistake (2tHlHWgDRdQ). Going from 1 post a week to 1 a day (7× effort) grew organic ~40× (2tHlHWgDRdQ).
- **Volatility is a volume symptom.** If you get one customer every 3–5 weeks, compress that advertising volume into a day (prj1bnTAM8A, HxEQCHpZzHk).

### Picking and adding channels
- **One channel, one avatar, one product under ~$1M/yr.** Add a second channel at ~$1M/month once the first runs without you (oRMG_HpOAN4). He waited until $4M/month. Outbound took ~12 months to reach half of revenue (oRMG_HpOAN4, oDK4g5na4Jw).
- **New channels pay "slower than you expect, then faster than you can imagine."** Give one 3–12 months and measure progress (clicks → opt-ins → right people → shows → sales), not dollars (reisEL_D7xc, oDK4g5na4Jw). Acquisition.com's outbound took ~2 months with no ROI, broke even in ~1 quarter, and now runs ~20:1 (XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **Effort split:** 70% proven, 20% adjacent, 10% new (HxEQCHpZzHk). Another talk says ~90% of time on more and better, with new only when nothing works or the channel is truly saturated (reisEL_D7xc). These are consistent.
- **Don't add a channel while the current one still takes more spend profitably** (nrounb8NlFQ, MH-IMJxbUY4, --9kOXNCUdE).
- **Pick ONE of affiliate partners or outbound events, not both.** Each needs a full-time owner (OQf2Ba-Lp_4). Never put a new person on a new channel (sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Single-channel risk** means more than 50% of leads come from one source. Tiny businesses can ignore it, but it bites as you grow (sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Prefer the lowest-risk add first.** Example: add PPC on top of working SEO and write more articles before chasing "whale" B2B outreach (--9kOXNCUdE).
- **A sellable business needs a second acquisition channel very different from the first** (oDK4g5na4Jw).
- **Market expansion comes last.** First pull the four levers: method → platform → media → volume. Then move up-market, down-market, adjacent, broader or narrower. "Who do we provide the most value to by dollars made?" (9gVdCR7W8o8, hnmBXTyMFKI). More than half of the Acquisition.com portfolio went *narrower*, not broader (reisEL_D7xc).
- **Only proper reason to chase a new avatar:** you've run out of the existing one. If you itch for something new, add a channel, not a customer type (QwEb78e5a8Y).

### Paid ads
- **Grow on 30-day cash.** If 30-day gross profit > 2 × (CAC + COGS), you can scale without limit on a credit card's 30-day float (XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 minimum.** Spend at most a third of lifetime gross profit to acquire. A ratio of 20:1 in a warm niche may drop to 5:1 and then 3:1 at scale, which is fine at 100× the spend (reisEL_D7xc). At 100:1 with a reliable channel, scale hard (jzKpAtzKQ54).
- **If $1 returns $24, put in as much as it will take.** Scale down to ~5:1 ROAS, and expect efficiency to drop at scale (sBJppqCeFGI). A 13:1 upfront ROAS on an unoptimized page means you're underspending (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **Order of work:** fix the funnel and ads → then raise spend. That alone can 2–3× leads (OQf2Ba-Lp_4, SmiOK8Yun4s). Raise spend only within sales capacity (nrounb8NlFQ).
- **When ROAS drops,** the fixes are a better lead magnet, better creative, page CRO, sales efficiency, or all four. Change one at a time (QwEb78e5a8Y). Spend usually caps because the creative isn't good enough (MH-IMJxbUY4). If you can't scale past ~$1K/day, it's your ad and awareness skill (hnmBXTyMFKI).
- **Starter:** a $100/day "slush fund" you've accepted losing (XwZH-lOKG9c). "You spend this money before you eat" (h6y0nYVZgwE).
- **First layer:** pixels everywhere, global retargeting of all site visitors, and bidding on your own brand terms. He has "never seen that lose money" (fSbqaTlWaYI, XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **Don't chase your first campaign's CAC.** The first campaign is always the best (FMzKk73iUhw).
- **With agencies,** speak in CPA/CPL targets and give them a stated range below your true max (sBJppqCeFGI). Losing ~$50K on one bad agency is a cost of doing business, not proof that agencies don't work (B-ogfFiQpXg).

### Lead quality and friction
- **Start with the widest net.** "The worst thing in the world is not having any flow to begin with." Add qualifiers only when volume is so high that you'll trade ½–⅓ of throughput for quality (Kk6Vdfdwmxw).
- **Perfect-funnel rule:** too many bad leads → add friction. Too few leads → remove friction. Almost no friction and still bad leads → targeting is off (fSbqaTlWaYI).
- **Five quality levers,** each of which cuts volume: targeting, number of steps, application depth, budget questions and value-first content (Kk6Vdfdwmxw).
- **Each funnel step loses ~50%.** Send ads to a standalone lander, never the home page (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **"It's not that ads don't work for you. It's that you haven't put enough friction in your process"** for high-ticket sales: ad → lead magnet → VSL → BANT → call (Ds_Qp2U5I8U).
- **When leads seem weak,** look first at the monetization system behind them (Kk6Vdfdwmxw). "All leads suck until they become customers" (h6y0nYVZgwE).

### Lead magnets
- **Three types:** reveal a problem, free trial/sample, or one step of a multi-step process (Mst4hreQYl0, Ds_Qp2U5I8U, ovL6Z5z0jxQ).
- **Delivery methods:** software/tools, information, services (only for qualified leads) and physical products (Mst4hreQYl0).
- **Test the name and packaging more than the contents.** The name can change uptake 2–10× (Mst4hreQYl0).
- **A good magnet converts ~3–5× more hand-raisers from the same traffic** (Mst4hreQYl0). A high-intent Google lander should opt in ~30% of visitors (20–50% range) (nrounb8NlFQ).
- **Prefer immediate over more valuable but slow.** "Pre-digest the food" so they do no work (nrounb8NlFQ). Gate costly services behind qualification (the "honey trap") (nrounb8NlFQ, SmiOK8Yun4s).
- **If it's not good enough that people tell others about it, "you have failed"** (7NMH1oAkgLY). "Put 100 in if you've never put more than 10" (7NMH1oAkgLY).

### Referrals and affiliates
- **Ask systematically at specific moments.** It brings ~30% more customers at any scale (qpQvdBFW_yI). Ask at the "wow moment" (e.g. day 90) and at every booking: "who do you want to bring?" (B-ogfFiQpXg).
- **Referral growth vs churn:** 20% of customers referring monthly against 10% churn grows 2:1 indefinitely (qpQvdBFW_yI).
- **Incentives must change behavior.** 20% kickbacks and discounts under ~50% don't (FMzKk73iUhw, --9kOXNCUdE). Size the bounty to the value: a PT client worth ~$350K gross profit/yr justifies a $20K referral fee (QwEb78e5a8Y). Pay partners at least your CAC (YZdE8U5eD_s). For affluent customers, give a real treatment rather than cash-like credit (B-ogfFiQpXg).
- **Referral discount ceiling ≈ your normal CAC,** and never discount without a change in terms (give me three friends) (qpQvdBFW_yI).
- **Ask "which patients this week would be a fit?",** not "do you have any?" (--9kOXNCUdE). "Referrals everyone promises, no one delivers", so don't rely on vague swaps (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **Affiliate machine = Offer → Place (affiliate page) → Promotion → Follow-up/support.** Skip one and "it's not going to work" (DaRXece2ItE).
- **Hand affiliates finished collateral** (emails, posts, ads), prove attribution fast (link plus leaderboard), and remember that friction kills promotion (DaRXece2ItE, 60_7PU9JDIw).
- **Prize design:** the prize should benefit the affiliate's *audience*, so promoting adds goodwill. Use a scarce top tier plus a tiny hurdle anyone can clear (60_7PU9JDIw).
- **Affiliate-sells-your-lead-magnet model** (his favorite): the partner sells your low-ticket offer and keeps 100%, and you upsell the core. CAC = delivery cost ÷ upsell conversion rate (OQf2Ba-Lp_4, --9kOXNCUdE).
- **Keep affiliates active** with 1–3 events a year (nrounb8NlFQ). Name your 10 biggest potential partners and spend real money getting their attention (XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **Super-influencer:** whoever has the bigger audience owns the brand. Send 100 messages a day until you get replies, make their side look better than yours, and "you just need one" (Z2tgZC_XkT4).

## Playbooks

### A. Zero to first 5 customers (warm outreach), from w7g08dVTwaE, CaiLcj6tzBQ and HsQeQM1jUeg
1. Export three lists: email contacts, social DMs and phone contacts.
2. Spend 30 seconds on each profile and open with something real. Use **ACA**: Acknowledge → Compliment → Ask.
3. Ask for referrals, not a sale: "Do you know anybody who is [struggle] looking to [dream outcome] in [time]? I'm taking on five case studies for free because that's all I can handle…" (w7g08dVTwaE) If they say no: "Does anyone you hate come to mind?" (w7g08dVTwaE)
4. Send 100 a day. Take a maximum of 5 free customers, and collect usage, feedback, a testimonial and referrals.
5. Start charging when referrals arrive, and keep stepping the price up. At capacity, free clients must match the full-price rate or leave a review.
6. Rule: "Once you get the yes, shut the [ __ ] up and go." (CaiLcj6tzBQ)

### B. Pick and run your first scalable channel (<$1M), from HxEQCHpZzHk, h6y0nYVZgwE and reisEL_D7xc
1. Compare channel % with time % for your current customers. Double down on the biggest source (nrounb8NlFQ).
2. Choose ONE Core Four channel. Block the first 4 hours a day for it, 6 days a week.
3. Hit Rule-of-100 volume and track inputs daily (an input goal, not an outcome goal) (0_Gf5v8DEMY).
4. Put a lead magnet in front of the "book a call" CTA (Mst4hreQYl0).
5. Respond to leads within 60 seconds and call twice on no answer (pLhQOYMGa88).
6. Each week, ask what would stop me from 10×-ing this? Fix the first thing that would break, then add volume (reisEL_D7xc).

### C. Scale paid ads profitably, from XwZH-lOKG9c, fSbqaTlWaYI, OQf2Ba-Lp_4, sBJppqCeFGI and MH-IMJxbUY4
1. Confirm you're demand-constrained and have attribution in place.
2. Do the math: LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 and ideally 30-day GP > 2×(CAC+COGS).
3. Set up the first layer: pixels, global retargeting and branded PPC.
4. Build a standalone lander → lead magnet → (high ticket: VSL + BANT) → call within 60 seconds.
5. Test hooks most, since ~80% decide in the first 3 seconds (CaiLcj6tzBQ). Reskin winners endlessly: ~80% of their resources go to remaking winners (fSbqaTlWaYI). Run winning organic posts as ads with a short CTA (MH-IMJxbUY4, Ds_Qp2U5I8U).
6. Raise spend month over month while CAC holds, within sales capacity. Accept ROAS sliding toward ~5:1.
7. When ROAS falls, change one thing: magnet, creative, CRO or sales efficiency.

### D. Build an affiliate / referral engine, from DaRXece2ItE, 60_7PU9JDIw, qpQvdBFW_yI and B-ogfFiQpXg
1. Write two offers: one to the affiliate and one to their audience. Aim for win-win-win.
2. Build the affiliate page and tracking (custom link and leaderboard).
3. Recruit through the Core Four. Email your own list about the program only twice.
4. Ship plug-and-play collateral for every format. Hold assets back and release a fresh packet ~3 weeks in.
5. Make the first referral flawless and close the loop with the referrer. Then run a launch, then integrate into their process (--9kOXNCUdE).
6. For customer referrals, build in asks at fixed moments (after the sale, the wow moment, every booking). Use gift cards (90%+ off, 2 per customer, 30-day expiry) and quarterly events (qpQvdBFW_yI).

### E. We've plateaued, so the market must be saturated, from 9gVdCR7W8o8, hnmBXTyMFKI and QwEb78e5a8Y
1. Size the market: add up competitors' spend on one platform. You probably hold <1%.
2. Pull the levers in order: method → platform → media → volume. Use the "should do" list (speed to lead, follow-up, more content, cold calling, more platforms).
3. Ask "why can't we do more?" (QwEb78e5a8Y) The answer is the constraint, so solve it.
4. Only then pick a market direction (up, down, adjacent, broader or narrower) by value per dollar made.

## Anti-patterns
- **Tiny tests, then quitting the channel** (300 flyers), or quitting after one month of ad spend (2tHlHWgDRdQ, reisEL_D7xc, oDK4g5na4Jw).
- **Calling a channel or market "saturated"** at a tiny share of the market (hnmBXTyMFKI, prj1bnTAM8A, 9gVdCR7W8o8).
- **Adding product lines or channels** when one ad channel stops scaling, around $3M (9gVdCR7W8o8). Getting "cute" with many offers and channels too early (YtbHaogHlpY).
- **Sending cold traffic straight to "book a call / get a quote"** or to a form with no value proposition ("goes from zero to let's sleep together") (Mst4hreQYl0, SmiOK8Yun4s).
- **Sending ads to the home page** with navigation and clutter, or designing ads on desktop (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **Fluff lead magnets.** The 99% who never buy judge you on them (7NMH1oAkgLY, SmiOK8Yun4s). Building magnets for the wrong avatar (Ds_Qp2U5I8U).
- **Advertising all 15 services** instead of the 1–3 that convert (--9kOXNCUdE).
- **Weak referral incentives** (generic 20% kickbacks, cash credits to the wealthy). Reps skipping the ask. Yes/no asks like "do you know anybody?" (qpQvdBFW_yI, FMzKk73iUhw, B-ogfFiQpXg).
- **Putting referral or affiliate systems on autopilot** instead of reinvesting in them (4Yz8ggEv0NU). Letting affiliates go inactive (nrounb8NlFQ).
- **Asking affiliates to build their own campaigns,** or making the prize a kickback that forces them to hard-close their audience (DaRXece2ItE).
- **Not calling your own leads,** or calling them slowly. He calls this a huge hidden revenue hole (XwZH-lOKG9c, sBJppqCeFGI).
- **Having a fixed "marketing budget"** instead of client-financed acquisition (XwZH-lOKG9c). Emotional fear of high ad spend when the metrics are known (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **Pausing the service because there are "too many leads"** Inflow never stops (XC_lklN9KmE).
- **Restructuring around a whale** that came from no repeatable channel (sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Expecting content to be the main early lead source** (0_Gf5v8DEMY, MD5-HByRxoA).
- **Solo service owners relying only on referrals:** one new client every 2–3 months while one churns (0_Gf5v8DEMY).

## Worked examples
- **Ashley, AC Styles (luxury stylist).** $309K revenue, 42% margin, LTV $10K, CAC $600 (~16:1). Lead sources split ~⅓ affiliates, ~⅓ paid, ~⅓ referrals. 1,800 high-intent clicks produced only 8 sales, so the fix is a lead magnet, a short VSL and BANT before raising spend. Six affiliates from ~40 manual emails (15%, vs 0.5–1% normal for cold) ≈ ⅓ of revenue, about $2,500 per email sent (nrounb8NlFQ, SmiOK8Yun4s). Thumbtack's "$38 a lead" doesn't match $6K/58 leads ≈ $103 [inference: source mismatch].
- **ProShine (HVAC/duct cleaning).** $1.25M, 38% net. ~60% paid, with 13:1 upfront ROAS on an unoptimized page, so it was underspending. Sequence: landing page (~2×), then ads (2–3×), then retargeting (+10–20%), then reactivation (+20–30%) (OQf2Ba-Lp_4).
- **Raymond's chiro chain (6 locations, $5.2M).** Google returning $24 per $1, so double the spend and scale down to ~5:1. A $19–29 X-ray and assessment with the card taken over the phone pushes the show rate toward ~100% (sBJppqCeFGI).
- **B2B lead-gen agency offer change.** Moving from $1K down + $1.5K/month to $4K once + pay per show, on the same $10K spend: response rate ×2.5, close rate ×2.3, up-front cash $5K → $112K (XwZH-lOKG9c).
- **$100M Leads launch.** ~10,000 affiliates with zero payouts. The prize was an AMA for their audiences plus bonus chapters for anyone bringing 10+. Top affiliates sent ~1,000 registrants each. Changing the banner above the submit form lifted affiliate sign-ups 60% (60_7PU9JDIw).
- **Gym Launch outbound.** First sale at 90 days, and the exec team staged an "intervention" to cut it. At ~12 months it was 50% of revenue (oDK4g5na4Jw).
- **Free-trial math (massage).** A $100 offer to 100 people gets ~1 yes. A $10 trial gets ~20 yes, and ~⅓ buy the $100 package (~6 customers). Spending ~$200 net on the trial acquires ~6 customers worth about $2,000 (7NMH1oAkgLY).
- **Free-service lead magnet math.** $25 hard cost (worth $250+), 1 in 4 buys → $100 CAC (Mst4hreQYl0).
