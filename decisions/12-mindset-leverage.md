# How should I think, focus and spend my time and attention to get the most leverage?

**Bottom line:** Work = output = volume × leverage, not hours or effort. Put your scarcest asset, **attention**, on one thing: the current constraint of one business. Do a "violent, unreasonable" volume of the inputs that make money, judge yourself on those inputs rather than on outcomes, and play a 10-year-plus infinite game. Climb the leverage ladder (labor → capital → code → content/media) by buying skills (paying down "ignorance debt") and talent. Avoid ruin, distraction and emotion-driven decisions, because time and compounding do the rest.

Sources key: `(id)` = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>. Speaker flags are noted where someone other than Alex is speaking.

## Diagnose first

**Questions to answer:**
1. **Where's your attention?** List every business, project, open loop and unconfronted conversation. More than one priority means no priority (6ZEZWuVC8-8, XwzU4RikbGs, uRhArskhqms).
2. **What is your input→output equation?** For example, X reach-outs → Y calls → Z sales. If you don't know it, that's the first problem to solve (YtbHaogHlpY, M4evdxF5G0s).
3. **Hours per day on the core input.** Target 4+ uninterrupted hours, first thing (h6y0nYVZgwE, gD0X-PLax5I, d0dBZbd13jM).
4. **Your hourly value.** Last year's income ÷ 2,000. Outsource anything the market pays less for (vhOV_Od0A3M, awOQDy_o9ww).
5. **Volume vs practitioners.** Is your test size a fraction of what winners do (5,000 flyers/day vs 300)? (2tHlHWgDRdQ, WrCt0R3FBFs)
6. **Skill gap.** Which brick is missing from your eyeballs-to-money pipe? (kULFeI3LRYk)
7. **Reference group.** Whose voices do you hear before big decisions? Are they closer to your goals or just closer to you? (vhOV_Od0A3M, oDK4g5na4Jw)
8. **Horizon.** Are you judging on 90 days or 5–10 years? (VgEvIIpDYG8)

## Core principles

1. **Attention, not time, is the golden resource.** "In order to grow you need to do less." Kill side businesses: "One fat paycheck is better than two" [Millionaire explains why time is NOT the most precious resource](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ZEZWuVC8-8) (6ZEZWuVC8-8). "Attention is my number one most valuable asset" (w5g0JiO7OdE).
2. **Work = output = volume × leverage.** Out-leverage rather than outwork. "Better is better than new" (RdAKXJlMIZM, rhVxX5_8xUw). Throughput = Volume × Skill × Time; beginners must compensate with extraordinary volume (2tHlHWgDRdQ).
3. **Focus is measured by what you say no to.** "The better you get, the better the opportunities you have to say no to" (6ZEZWuVC8-8). "If you don't say no to good opportunities you'll never have the time to pursue the great" (rhVxX5_8xUw). Run a "season of no" lasting 3 months to 3 years (ueJg14gQLuc).
4. **Leverage ladder.** The four forms are capital, media, code and labor, each with its scarce skill (SbZFTPJ9KZU). Media is the compounding one: attention is this century's oil (6DCDGSnRDtM). Climb collaboration → capital → code → content (Nh8Oc7ERdIU). The vehicle sets the ceiling: "it's not how hard you row but what boat you are in" (qxQIcDrre1E).
5. **Pay down ignorance debt first.** Skills are the one asset no crisis can take (KNl83INePXI). Spend savings on education "until you can't even spend the money" (6KqndZuN_Yk, vhOV_Od0A3M). With $1,000 and low income, buy a skill, not a stock (m-k0_pQJ1fY).
6. **Volume negates luck.** Quit only if the whole industry disappeared; otherwise the gap is skill (w9Zvk70uj5Q, YFA8AS5Cu2w). "If results are normal, output is normal" (WrCt0R3FBFs).
7. **Judge inputs, not outcomes.** Score yourself on effort relative to capacity and on completing actions (yes/no). "Your work works on you" (ahslH-8qoFY, MNll1BaskLA, RdAKXJlMIZM, pt50QF6al8g).
8. **Longest time horizon wins.** The master lens of billionaires is time horizon. "I would rather get rich for sure and get rich slowly than never get rich at all" (E732S9fteRc). Business is an infinite game: quitting is the only way to fail (7C-P-ctmhuU, WOZGZK7K-Jo).
9. **Avoid ruin; you only have to get rich once.** Don't bet what you have and need for what you don't have and don't need (vhOV_Od0A3M, VaE4pcf9PI8). "Do not risk the empire for a pot of gold" (WsYgWC7NmO8). He'd "rather have a very sure 20% than a really risky 40" (tdLLGKoBojo).
10. **Emotions are weather.** Ask: is this action meant to make the feeling go away, or is it what the business needs? (138y93ywE_g). Felt urgency is a red flag; extend the gap between feeling and action (noO8ERpgMr4, vthPawWn6ws).
11. **Problems before opportunities.** Solving problems has a near-certain payoff; chasing opportunities also grows problems (zNiXk_3C_Io, TFxT3G5jwtU).
12. **Talent is the biggest arbitrage, and you get what you tolerate.** "Every single person… is three amazing hires away from all the growth you've ever wanted." This is Alex's secondhand note of a billionaire's advice (_x6n-CmZqjo). See also A248pGXTSoY, KhFlD54nQrY.
13. **Simple scales, fancy fails.** Complexity is "the child of lazy thinking" (-JLN1d1ZKUU, MLkFOaxIdxM).
14. **Own, don't earn.** "You get paid on what you do; you get returns on what you own" (s3tH9Sz1fEc). Grow enterprise value rather than shaving taxes: "Instead of saving 10%, think about how can I 10x" (w5g0JiO7OdE).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Time and attention
- **4+ hours a day, 6 days a week, on the primary acquisition input or the biggest problem**, done before the team arrives or after they leave (h6y0nYVZgwE, gD0X-PLax5I). Under $1M, the first 4 hours go to one core-four channel (HxEQCHpZzHk).
- **Protect your freshest hours** (his first ~4–6) for one key input: no meetings, put meetings in the afternoon (M4evdxF5G0s, KhFlD54nQrY). It doesn't have to be morning; some of his billionaire friends are night owls (d0dBZbd13jM).
- **Maker vs manager:** makers need empty calendars with big blocks. Set standing meeting windows. Delete meetings weekly and audit them all quarterly (GIRkQQHzsxI).
- **Meeting cost** = annual pay ÷ 2,000 per attendee-hour. A 10-person meeting at ~$500 should return 4–5× that (4Yz8ggEv0NU).
- **Hourly reality check:** last year's income ÷ 2,000. Do the highest $/hr activity until all your hours go to it, then find a higher one (vhOV_Od0A3M, oZ-H_TjSzok).
- **Outsource chores if you earn more than ~$15/hr.** About 96 hrs/mo can be replaced for ~$1,500/mo (vhOV_Od0A3M). If your work is worth $100/hr, don't do $15/hr work (awOQDy_o9ww).
- **Buy back time when the math works.** An HVAC owner paying $60K/yr to offload customer service goes from $400K → $340K profit for 80% of his time back = yes (spXH1kJ6q-Q). Hand off any task eating 10–15 hrs/week even if it isn't a full role (ueJg14gQLuc).
- **Don't let automation fool you.** 14 dev days × $5K = $70K to save ~$7K/yr is a ~10-year payback, so reject it (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Unmade decisions and unconfronted conversations each take a slice of thinking power.** If simple decisions feel hard, that's the cause (uRhArskhqms). A mediocre person you think about first thing in the morning takes ~25% of your bandwidth (I64gYLBrics, LGq0uVspNms).
- **There are no emergencies except 911 ones.** The real emergency is a third year of no growth (gD0X-PLax5I). Only payment-processing failure is a short-term existential crisis; let other fires burn (KhFlD54nQrY).
- **Deadlines:** default to end of day. Pulling end-of-week to end-of-day is ~7× speed. Ask for estimates in minutes and hours (cnbHgYYHzyE, TFxT3G5jwtU, WrCt0R3FBFs).
- **Sustainable pace (his):** 10–12 hrs/day indefinitely. A "three shifts" sprint 6–8 weeks a year. ~18 hrs/day was too much. The goal is total net output over the longest time, so rest that raises output is required (6KqndZuN_Yk, vhOV_Od0A3M).

### Priorities and change
- **Only the #1 priority.** Test: "Is there anything else we could do with the resources we have that would give that kind of a return…in the next 30 days?" If not, it's the only priority. Remove normal work rather than stacking urgent work on top. Reassess after solving it (TFxT3G5jwtU).
- **Every change costs ~20%.** Only take bets with ≥20% upside; prefer medium-impact/high-confidence (uYds0zcAFWM). Small businesses get about one big change per year (0lMn_-EXyhQ).
- **Three questions "like weapons":** "What problem are you trying to solve?" "What would it take?" "What do you want to have happen?" If a proposal can't name its problem, don't do it (fj5uxdv_j5Y).
- **Data litmus:** if the number going up or down wouldn't change what you do, don't track it ("data driven or data distracted?") (4Yz8ggEv0NU, cnbHgYYHzyE, zNiXk_3C_Io).
- **Season ideas** for 2–4 weeks. Don't pitch a new idea to the team right away (hfwZwPGsbIo, zNiXk_3C_Io).
- **Ask "Will this matter in 10 years?"** and set conservative goals with room to overachieve (WsYgWC7NmO8). 10–20% growth per quarter compounds hugely; don't aim to double every 90 days (QQGHCG8d1So).
- **But think in orders of magnitude when planning.** "What would we do if we were 10× bigger?" Plan so "five different versions of this thing going wrong" still hit the goal (A248pGXTSoY). Big goals are about as hard as small ones and have thinner competition (E732S9fteRc, vhOV_Od0A3M).
- **Elimination before addition.** If you're working a lot without progress, you're doing the wrong things (MNll1BaskLA, gD0X-PLax5I).

### Volume and skill
- **Give it at least ~100 days** (Rule of 100 for 100 straight days, escalating after dry days). Stick with one thing for a year to beat most people (spXH1kJ6q-Q, -UzJOk85OZI, MzAIP_WJ-jE).
- **~20 hours to basic competence** in a skill; 4–6 hrs/day on the right input for 5 years makes you good (6KqndZuN_Yk, M4evdxF5G0s).
- **If behind, do ~2× the top performer's inputs.** Kobe "two-a-days": 100 doors → 200 (Nh8Oc7ERdIU, aFoMYz_jWcs).
- **Plateau over a year → "you're not good enough."** Admit the deficit and increase reps (d0dBZbd13jM). Flat income for years usually means a character-trait gap (YQZK6JVkl4c).
- **Replicate before you iterate.** Don't change a copied system until you perform as well as its source (Tw6kJkVcTL0). Do a program "to a T" the first time and aim to be its #1 success story (IYuiV4YuGB4, vhOV_Od0A3M).
- **Hire experts to "get me on iteration 9 as iteration one,"** then beat them with a faster feedback loop. "If you don't track, you don't care" (q9qBqnhdWKw).
- **Study what you lack, not what you enjoy** (kULFeI3LRYk). Always spend as much as you can on the skill that constrains growth (wtsX7WHQMFM).
- **Learning budget:** 10–20% of marketing spend, expected to be lost (ULGT0Qpglek). Keep a monthly education budget (e.g. cut expenses to free about $1K/mo) (6KqndZuN_Yk).

### Money mindset for leverage
- **Below ~$200–500K/yr of income, spend on skills, not saving** (d0dBZbd13jM). Early savings of $500–2,000/mo are better spent on coaching and books (CRMT2yubBxc).
- **Never spend 100 hours building $100/mo passive income** when the same time could earn $10,000 actively. Don't chase passive income before maxing active income (m-k0_pQJ1fY, uYds0zcAFWM, gZvW1Hghv6U).
- **Cash reserve of 6 months to 2 years** of expenses; everything else goes into truly passive index funds. Switch focus to managing wealth only when ~10%/yr on investable assets exceeds your annual earnings (CRMT2yubBxc).
- **Bet only a small share** of net worth outside your core; treat it as gambling. Max ~10% in any single allocation (tdLLGKoBojo, INm4U2S7Vu8).
- **Multiply your estimated downside by 3** (oZ-H_TjSzok, vhOV_Od0A3M).
- **"If you can't buy it twice (or 3–5×), don't buy it once"** (vhOV_Od0A3M). Track monthly savings, not income (rp1PzCxj3eU).
- **You're compensated in proportion to the (perceived) risk you take.** The best bets look risky to others and aren't risky to you. A 10% chance of a 100× payoff is worth taking, but never risk the farm (rMJIOK_FgJk, VBoRLJimVzc).

### People and environment
- **Razor for every person, activity or belief:** does it make it more or less likely I hit my goals? (vhOV_Od0A3M, aFoMYz_jWcs).
- **Listen to people closest to your goals, not closest to you.** If everyone agrees with you, be suspicious (oDK4g5na4Jw, MzAIP_WJ-jE). "The best way to stay poor is to listen to poor people about how to get rich" (YFA8AS5Cu2w).
- **The highest standard decides** in each function, regardless of seniority or shares (oDK4g5na4Jw, oRMG_HpOAN4).
- **Underperformers:** a 14-day improvement plan. About 1 in 5 turn around; exit the rest (I64gYLBrics, LGq0uVspNms). A players show within days, and he has never seen a non-A player become one (Jv3GmZ9Odko). He reports telling within 14 days (YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Pay 25–50% more for someone who moves you 10 years forward** (KhFlD54nQrY). ~25% more for an A player can yield ~5× output (Nh8Oc7ERdIU).
- **Core values:** three, non-negotiable, hire and fire on them, 3 of 3 or no. "If you would not do business with someone for a decade, do not do business with them for a day" (kW2vDMAmlPI).
- **Work over networking:** "When the work works out, the people will still be there to take your call" (ub1D6RQocRU, oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Hate:** from people behind you is envy; from people ahead is data (ln24y0FPJHo). Before changing anything because of a complaint, count incidents vs the total population (PZjJEQMgyMM).

### Big decisions
- **Decide irreversible calls rested, fed and away from your usual space.** If the answer flips on unchanged data, wait. "Mistakes love a rushed decision" (7YMjZgr7sHM). Sleep on emotional responses (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **By the time you have all the information, the opportunity is closed** (B1-oIkRLh-8). If you've done the research, stop and decide (ULGT0Qpglek). Few decisions are truly irreversible (ub1D6RQocRU).
- **Spell out the trade:** what you get now vs later, and what you pay. "Make more money" is a bad goal because everything makes money (fcyIoN8CUOk, MZPVPCIeUpg).
- **Invert:** "what would guarantee failure here?", then avoid it (C_SgvSvJZdk, 9ySuYdJ0H4s, -Pz0NpcILOY).

## Playbooks

### A. Install a focus system (any stage)
1. List every project, business and open loop. Pick the one with the highest return per unit of attention (rhVxX5_8xUw).
2. Cut, delegate or fire-sell the rest. Make the unmade decisions this week (uRhArskhqms).
3. Write your input→output equation and the daily input target (YtbHaogHlpY).
4. Block the first 4 hours, 6 days a week, for that input. Put meetings in afternoon windows (h6y0nYVZgwE, GIRkQQHzsxI).
5. Split your list into problems vs opportunities. Work problems only; revisit opportunities in two months (zNiXk_3C_Io).
6. End each day with "What do I have to show for it?" (ZklXJzHzW2U).

### B. Climb out of broke (skill → income → leverage)
1. Go fully defensive on spending: everything beyond food, shelter and transport is surplus (YFA8AS5Cu2w, jfW6gL6hKhk).
2. Pick one paid skill (sales recommended) and do 4 hrs/day of focused study and reps (YFA8AS5Cu2w, Nh8Oc7ERdIU).
3. Trade up jobs through volume: message 4 people per company, ~10 companies a day (YFA8AS5Cu2w). Take learning-rich roles over higher pay (DiQ3N8F1Hl8).
4. Put the surplus into the next skill, not investments, until income is high (d0dBZbd13jM, oys_fv25SYM).
5. Commit to a ~36-month season before judging (YFA8AS5Cu2w, aFoMYz_jWcs).

### C. Founder buying back time (≈$1–10M)
1. Run a 15-minute time study for a week and color tasks green, yellow or red (mr4Pw66_490).
2. Hire in reverse order of value: offload the cheapest repeated tasks first (7NqsFh5ej0w).
3. Take "two steps forward": delegate what you do now *and* hire for what you'd do next (s3tH9Sz1fEc). Don't step out of the CEO seat too soon (s3tH9Sz1fEc).
4. Keep accountability while giving up control (awOQDy_o9ww). Refill freed time with higher-value work (s3tH9Sz1fEc, ZklXJzHzW2U).
5. Litmus: 1 month away → 6 months growing without you → 2 months with no calls (unshZobTt6Q, sEQNnsaeiAs).

### D. Making a big decision under stress
1. Name it: "this is what hard feels like" (mNcDKnq6-q8). Separate real burnout (output per hour drops → rest) from an emotional reaction.
2. Change environment; decide only when the answer is stable over days (7YMjZgr7sHM).
3. Write the trade-off explicitly and invert it (fcyIoN8CUOk, C_SgvSvJZdk).
4. Size the downside ×3 and refuse ruin (vhOV_Od0A3M).
5. Choose the regret you prefer. Regret from untaken paths is more certain than from failed execution (_gcqwupsza8). "Pick the more epic story" (pt50QF6al8g).

## Anti-patterns

- **Shiny objects / "woman in the red dress"** (07jC6ooRIHw, 5Kt-EYieNko).
- **Being CEO of more than one thing.** "You can only be CEO of one thing" (ub1D6RQocRU, sKCrYIWPHLc, oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Measuring busyness instead of output.** A day only counts if it produced a clear output (ln24y0FPJHo, 6KqndZuN_Yk).
- **Normal volume expecting abnormal results:** 300 flyers vs 5,000/day (WrCt0R3FBFs, HxEQCHpZzHk).
- **Chasing passive income or tax hacks** before maxing active income and enterprise value (m-k0_pQJ1fY, w5g0JiO7OdE, gZvW1Hghv6U).
- **Pseudo-retirement.** He spent 9–12 months depressed after stepping away (ahslH-8qoFY).
- **Expectations, Emotions, Expenses (the "3 evil Es"):** arbitrary deadlines → missed goals → scarcity emotions → rushed hires (noO8ERpgMr4). Don't hire "in anticipation of growth."
- **Tolerating mediocrity or industry-standard bars.** "Industry standard" means average, and the average business barely makes money (A248pGXTSoY, aUnorufFIq8).
- **Learning the wrong lesson** from a failure or a fast early success (SasEJE4FI-I, FTgJ0mQi0uU).
- **Taking advice from people who don't have what you want** (ln24y0FPJHo, oys_fv25SYM, aRVv5NLVRwE).
- **FOMO bets and "too good to be true" deals** (tdLLGKoBojo, rp1PzCxj3eU).
- **Status spending.** "Impress poor people, outspend them; impress rich people, outwork them" (aFoMYz_jWcs).

## Worked examples

- **Focus payoff:** after the DUI crash he cut from ~9–11 businesses to one → ~$3M profit within ~12 months (uRhArskhqms).
- **Volume:** going from 7 to 80 posts a week grew his audience from 180K to 1.2M in 6 months (WrCt0R3FBFs). Over 40 months: ~35,000 pieces, ~$4M spent, 7.8M followers (HVOubeXUcx0).
- **Stepping back:** stuck around ~$30M for almost 4 years. Growth resumed when he stopped being the center and incentivized other leaders (QQGHCG8d1So).
- **Leila's effect** (as Alex tells it): before her he "never cracked $3 million" running 8 businesses on fear. Within 24 months of working together, $17M personal income (oK2_u-uS_Bo).
- **Talent ROI:** a dentist earning $700K/yr gross profit per specialist was spending ~$2.5K on recruiting. Paying ~$150K × 4 placements is "easily worth it" (YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Passive isn't the goal:** $37.9M in dividends over 36 months, ~$6M/yr at about 8.5/10 passivity, and "boring and empty" (gZvW1Hghv6U).
- **Ruin avoidance in practice:** he says his biggest loss, about $25M, came from reacting to a competitor (ln24y0FPJHo). His *I Lost $100 Million* video traces most losses to straying from core competence (tdLLGKoBojo).

## More principles, rules and quotes (from the medium-signal notes)

### Leverage beats hours
- "Hard work will not make you rich." Once your hours are maxed, the only lever left is *what* you work on. "The CEO needs to have the highest return on time out of anyone in the organization" (PTgGfV8Tf00).
- Newcomers who can't get off the couch should build volume first, then shift to leverage (PTgGfV8Tf00). "Once you have the right strategy, it's volume." Dave Ramsey (guest) has done 3 hrs/day of radio for 30+ years (y7O-iTWwTW8).
- **Talent Grid, What → How → Who:** you do it, then teach it, then systematize it ("Building the machine that builds the machine"). Leaders are usually needed around ~$3M/yr to break through to $10M+. A phone that keeps blowing up means the business isn't systematized (gpKz22P84iM).
- **Buy back "humaning"** (feeding, cleaning, moving your body). His thresholds: meals above $16/hr earned, cleaning above $20/hr, drivers above $50/hr. Skip an item only if the activity gives you real meaning (3SVksBB3_YY). "Go spend this money so you can increase your active income rather than obsessing over passive."
- **AI:** automate your own workflow personally. Set a fixed block to automate one part of your day end to end. "Your business doesn't have to become an AI business, but you do need to use AI in your business" (fr78adfAnuA).
- **Execution speed:** "If you can get things done end of day rather than end of week you were literally speeding up the organization 7x." When someone says "end of week," ask what they're doing in the next two hours that's more important (oW6JkqL6sdM).

### Focus and the "season of no"
- **Anti-routine:** success comes from what you stop doing. His version: no meetings 4am–noon, the same sleep schedule 7 days a week, and maintenance-only workouts. This routine applies until you have team leverage ($0–1M, $1–3M, sometimes $5M). "Do I need more information or more execution?" (lRyGIzW9d9k).
- "Strategy means what do you say no to that still looks like it makes sense on paper" (4vLgOX1Ykjg).
- Don't treat a dichotomy to be managed as a problem to be solved, and don't mis-rank normal operating problems as existential threats (-fV1MZe2vzs).
- If you've tinkered with offer, pages or pricing for months with no change, the problem is likely people and culture, not strategy. "Advanced people never don't do the basics" (rX5zKeQu7ow, iwskrByMIqo).
- "Don't be cute." Until you do as well as the person who taught you, don't change their system. "Replicate before you get fancy" (JfcIUxa8Nn0).
- **Preparation is a lever:** top performers do 100× the prep, not 2–3×. "You are not nervous, you're underprepared." When asked "can you do it for less?", he answers "I can do it for more" (m5ordaa7NN4).

### Time horizon and compounding
- **Think like an investor on a ~5-year horizon** (a mentor who sold for ~$3.4B told him 100 years). Ask: "if I did this consistently for 5 years, would it be unreasonable not to have built a reliable channel?" If yes, invest, and don't measure ROI until next year. "Time stops being a liability and a threat and becomes an asset" (bSYfZdAE8Ks).
- "If you measure on a one-year time horizon then it will never make sense" (qel9bf653Es).
- **Goodwill compounds.** "Goodwill compounds faster than revenue does." Direct response spends goodwill with every ask, while branding delays the ask (Ec41cSp_tWc, cq8GyLrEuAk). "Either ask hard or do not ask at all" (ThSFgC_4NVA).
- **Windfalls:** normalize to your scale and reinvest so the result repeats. "We made $106 million in 72 hours... this was not a 72-hour thing" (EuMu6LaxBSk). That video also says every important initiative needs one owner, and a hard conversation should happen within 24 hours.
- "The point of the game is to keep playing" (DRHnh32OFUs). "Do the uncommonly obvious thing for an inordinate period of time" (YaNX49ygr0I).

### Skills, beliefs and identity
- **Entrepreneur = beliefs + skills + character traits**; diagnose which one is the constraint. Make arguments instead of affirmations ("I am X because…") (4vLgOX1Ykjg). "A trait is a skill, which means it can be learned" (-ouJJs1KaiE).
- **Best-student mindset:** "It is never going to be the thing that works; you are going to be the thing that makes it work." He reports $500K+ spent on education with "I have yet to have a negative ROI on any purchase that I've made." When something "didn't work," first check whether you actually executed it (3Ju1I37jWUM).
- "Allow yourself to be reasonably bad until a point where it would be unreasonable for you to be bad." If you want to earn far more and lack a valuable skill, learn to sell (yAVI0PS-hzU).
- **Sell first, read second.** "My favorite book on sales is to get out there and start selling" (QbDFCbpmI6w).
- Write down who you want to become, not goals: "What would someone 10 times smarter than me do in this situation?" "Committing to the activities is the goal" (YaNX49ygr0I).
- **Confidence from proof:** "The best way to be confident is to have proof." Scale a goal by extrapolating from what you've already done (-ouJJs1KaiE).
- **Hunger, not knowledge, is the bottleneck** once tactics are known. "If you're only trying to make enough to live on, you will always only make enough to live on." Sales availability must match when buyers are free (gN--zZi3Nfc).
- **Rock bottom lowers the bar for action:** "Nothing to lose... makes you a very dangerous person." Name the 1–2 people whose judgment you fear (obmdEvHJlAg).
- **Bias:** assume it won't change and control your own performance. If you're behind, do the best person's work plus another full day. "you have to be better than the bias" (Luvfw14pVx4).

### Emotional control, toughness and haters
- **Mental toughness** = "the chance a bad thing changes how you act in a way that's against your goals." It has four trainable components: tolerance, fortitude, resilience and adaptability. Be more upset about letting something change your behavior than about the thing itself (SE9_1PYsaP0, Avp3xh3Y1Ic).
- **Haters:** never attack back. If a claim is true, lean into it. If it's false and the source is smaller, ignore it. If it's false and the source is bigger, make them seem petty. In every case, get louder. "The only way that you beat hate is you don't beat hate — you drown it out" (UxQJ64BNuco).
- **Copycats:** never acknowledge them. Don't restructure the business in response. "If people are talking smack in the stands, beat them in the field" (cGup9yYrOoc).
- **Crisis communication:** "Bad news doesn't get better with time." Respond first, tell the truth, take responsibility (GmJQyCFCz1k, partly Leila).

### People, trust, partners and power
- **The one problem is people.** "Every single entrepreneur has one problem… it's a people problem." Worth "the extra five interviews… the extra 20 interviews" to find an A player. "Once you've seen an A player you can never unsee them" (-is6Z2T2h8o).
- "You never overexpand, you just undertalent." Past a certain size you partner for unhirable skill. Leila's gut check for a partner: "Do you like having dinner with them?" (OUI12JmD-lM).
- **Reward over punishment:** positive reinforcement directs behavior; "Punishments force people to avoid anything that will give them the punishment" (WRW7eY6K4Jo).
- **Trust** = making yourself punishable and betting they won't punish you. "Does burning you cost them more than protecting you?" One betrayal undoes years (k5-57282taI).
- **Partnerships:** "Equity is the most expensive thing that you give up in a relationship." If you can pay for the need, don't give equity. Litmus: "If you present both of you with the same data would you make the same decision?" (JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Advisors:** judge integrity and efficacy. Interview 10–20, and prefer trusted intent with 8/10 skill over unsure intent with 10/10 skill (zh0bklzwI3g).
- **Spouse:** "The single most important decision that you have in increasing your net worth... is who you marry" (8GRfCTu9Lsg). Leila's relationship advice (NBhDR8R_YGk, speaker Leila): fix the communication skill gap and change yourself first.
- Leila (nd22J08b1TA): "Money doesn't change people; the ability to make money changes people." Hire people who like change, and help outgrown early employees land elsewhere.
- **Retention as a mindset:** treat every relationship as week to week. "The real contract that they have with you is for today" (PArqypiHcyQ).

### Money as a leverage tool
- **"Wealth is a ratio, not a number"** (earned vs spent) (a0hUVFYzv3k, ebrcIErJi10, vKnAWYqMJ10, x1CtbsEqxW0, ymFWgFiKUvM). Spend under 5–10% of income if you can (rnvOwA39dDg, vKnAWYqMJ10). "You want to be rich, not look rich" (ebrcIErJi10).
- **Order of operations** (a0hUVFYzv3k, ymFWgFiKUvM): emergency fund first ($1K–5K, then 3–6 months), then pay off 24% credit cards before chasing 9–10% market returns, and pay cash for cars. "Saving money won't make you money but it will allow you to think further out and make bigger bets."
- **New money for big purchases:** fund splurges from new income generated for that purpose, not savings. Debt only if it's paid off within a year (by3ZEoo-Quc).
- **Inflation:** set goals in future dollars, and prioritize income growth over saving. "The game of entrepreneurship is risk and time" (6BQ3whjWG3M).
- **Hold, don't trade:** keep ~95–97% of net worth in stable assets, with ≤3% for speculation. "Pretend you're a dead person" (c5Lw12PXkgU). Ignore small tax "BBs": "The vast majority of your net worth is going to come from growth, not the contributions" (YhxiVIQp_ws).
- **Take distributions.** Reinvesting every dollar "gave myself an excuse" not to focus on profit (x1CtbsEqxW0). "If you were generating a profit you should be purposely taking some out every month" (al_WXFDbN8I).
- "Never be impressed by money." The "ultra-wealthy" Instagram lifestyle costs ~$300K/yr (vKnAWYqMJ10). Judge gurus by net free cash flow, not "$X million business" claims (al_WXFDbN8I).
- Dave Ramsey (guest, jvXOOddDg_s): "Never risk the empire for a pot of gold." "Make mistakes that are experiments that you survive." He says pour gas on whatever takes off disproportionately.

### Content and the messenger
- "Step one, do stuff and then step two, talk about the stuff you did." If the source of your content is other people's content, don't make it (eVY4Zo-hpqU, part Leila).
- The messenger's X-factor comes from combining rare, even contradictory traits on top of excellence: "You have to be really [f***ing] good, period" (SCi464zfAUM). A good book is "a complete solution to a narrowly defined problem" and "If people aren't sharing it on their own then it's not worth promoting" (-TOYJHax5x8).
