# How should I sell, and how do I build a sales process and team that closes?

> Rebuilt from the hand-read notes (notes/<id>.md). Citations are video ids in manifest.json; open notes/<id>.md, then transcripts/<id>.md, to go deeper. **[inference]** marks synthesis or math that isn't Alex's. Guest speakers are attributed by name.

**Bottom line:** "Selling is the transference of belief over a bridge of trust." Most sales are won or lost *outside* the pitch: in speed to lead, calendar availability, show rate, discovery and rep conviction, not clever closes. Run one simple, question-based script (CLOSER) that every rep follows word for word. Handle obstacles before you name the price, and never drop price without getting something in exchange. Manage the team with recorded calls, daily drills, competitions and fast cuts [How to Sell Better than 99% Of People (4 HOUR ULTIMATE GUIDE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE2_7elAcxM) (JE2_7elAcxM), [The Ultimate Sales Training for 2026 [Full Course]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StVqS0jD7Ls) (StVqS0jD7Ls), [How I Built A 20+ Sales Team [The Process]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgmh7p1s7zs) (bgmh7p1s7zs), [30 Minutes of sales training that will explode your business in 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oi7bnS8uyJM) (oi7bnS8uyJM).

## Diagnose first

**Collect these numbers (team and per rep):**
- Speed to lead, available slots per day, and days per week you sell (StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o).
- The funnel: booking rate → **show rate** (shows ÷ appointments) → **offer rate** (offers ÷ shows, a lead-quality signal) → **close rate** on shows *and* on offers → % cash collected up front → units (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- Calls per rep per day against capacity (utilization), talk-time ratio, and number of calls to close (c_6BrF7jOGk, StVqS0jD7Ls).
- **Cost per sale** by ad set, not cost per lead or per appointment (JE2_7elAcxM, c_6BrF7jOGk).
- Upsell and ascension take rate. Refund and cancel rate in the first 30 days (j1tA4l7R2c0, afbP6sB_Atc).

**Diagnose in this order:**
1. **The Five Ps:** the right Promotion, for the right Prospect, at the right Price, for the right Product, following the right Process. Then check the words (script), then the delivery (tone) (OmDv6-651SM).
2. **Who or what?** If the same problem recurs for quarters under the same person, it's a *who* problem (c_6BrF7jOGk).
3. **Conviction or skill?** A rep who started hot and then slumped has a conviction problem. Training won't fix it (bHnVItVGJPA, 42SOlVhDwWE).
4. **Fix the biggest leak first** (show → booking → close). Leave stages that are already at benchmark alone (4GQLJjH9-oA, sBJppqCeFGI).
5. **"Price" losses are usually offer or process problems.** "Customers will always go to price if you don't give them any other variables" (zzleYxkf39k).

**Branch by sale type:**

**Branch by stage:**
- **Founder selling alone:** sell first, read later. Record yourself from day one; his regret is ~4,000 unrecorded sales (QbDFCbpmI6w, iqAtBbGr3VY). A CEO of a sales-led business should get on the phones until they can teach it (6m6DCQMASEM).
- **Founder converts ~10× better than reps at low volume:** turn the reps into setters and fill the owner's calendar. 4 appointments a week doubles the business and 8 quadruples it at ~40% close (mRlSb0O5QNU).
- **Building a team:** hire a sales director who has built a team before ("hire one to hire 10"). Don't promote your best closer (JE2_7elAcxM, WrCt0R3FBFs).

## Core principles

1. **Sales is a transference of belief or conviction over a bridge of trust.** "How can you transfer a cup that is only one-third full into another empty cup?" 2. **Close rate = Skill × Conviction.** Units = close rate × work ethic, so conviction counts twice (42SOlVhDwWE). Feed conviction with fresh customer results (AGCtZmgJ1JA, OmDv6-651SM).
3. **The person who cares most about the buyer wins.** "The difference between help and manipulation is intention." 6. **Sell the vacation, not the plane flight.** Sell the goal, not the program (q32-l3Yoqg4, 6m6DCQMASEM).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Sales multipliers (outside the call)
- **Respond within 60 seconds.** HBR shows a 391% higher chance of closing. After 5 minutes the odds drop ~80%, and 50% of buyers go with whoever responds first (StVqS0jD7Ls, pLhQOYMGa88).
- **Total available time slots** was the strongest predictor of sales across ~4,000 appointments a day at ALAN. Offer same-day or next-day slots and start one every 15 minutes. "Today appointments always have higher show up rates" (cy2k1GdA-9o, StVqS0jD7Ls, JE2_7elAcxM). One 7:15am slot can't grow a business (gN--zZi3Nfc). Fixing scheduling usually adds 20–40% to ad throughput (0mqqbuM9sAk).
- **Sell 7 days a week.** Weekends add 104 days a year, at least +29% (StVqS0jD7Ls).
- **Pull-ups:** drag later appointments into today's open slots and replace unconfirmed calls. A solar rep who did this sold ~4× the #2 rep (cy2k1GdA-9o).
- **Manual reminders** at 24 hours, the morning of, and 1 hour before, by voice memo or video. Reward the best weekly show rate, e.g. a flat $500 (StVqS0jD7Ls). Ask a preference question before the call (shirt size, gift card) (cy2k1GdA-9o).
- **A +20% schedule or show rate equals a +20% close rate,** and it's easier and automatable (JE2_7elAcxM, 4GQLJjH9-oA).
- **Route the best leads to the best closers** ("feed the killers") (StVqS0jD7Ls).
- **Ask is anyone else needed to decide?** before the appointment, and reschedule to include them (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **The rep with the most hours worked always sells the most** (cy2k1GdA-9o).

### On the call
- **Don't ask for the sale until they've clarified why they need a solution.** Sit in the pain (q32-l3Yoqg4). Pitch only when you believe they'll say yes, or ask permission: "would you like to know how the program works?" (CojS0DwflXc).
- **Scripts are questions, not paragraphs.** Reps memorize only the ~30-second stories and the objection handles. Script the first ~⅔ of the call, up to the ask, in about 12 questions (oi7bnS8uyJM, StVqS0jD7Ls, CojS0DwflXc).
- **Wait up to ~8 seconds after the ask.** Only ~20–30% of sales are picked up after the first "no," and most sales are lost in discovery (sGakuNs9mT4).
- **Once they say yes, stop talking and take payment** (StVqS0jD7Ls, CaiLcj6tzBQ).
- **Never ask again without resolving the concern** (cy2k1GdA-9o). Don't treat "wow, that's expensive" as a real objection (StVqS0jD7Ls).
- **"Do you have any questions?"** is "the worst question ever asked in sales" It hands over control (RVbvhPGFi6E, 2lA_A8BGRRs).
- **"If you didn't get a gasp from a price tag, you didn't go high enough."** Go higher, then walk down from the anchor (NcD2t9qt-fM, iqAtBbGr3VY).
- **Prep ~10% of call length for repeated calls** (60 minutes → 6 minutes). Flip that for rare high-stakes meetings (~6 hours per hour) (cy2k1GdA-9o, JE2_7elAcxM). "You are not nervous, you're underprepared" (m5ordaa7NN4).
- **Needing 3–4 calls to close** means too little education before the first call. Put a VSL before every call, and if they didn't watch it, play it on the call (StVqS0jD7Ls, mRlSb0O5QNU, SmiOK8Yun4s). Two calls to close cut to one doubles sales with the same team (SmiOK8Yun4s).
- **Never present an invoice or proposal by email.** Do it live (mRlSb0O5QNU). Quote fast, because speed raises close rates (mRlSb0O5QNU).
- **Stop selling if they'd genuinely be better off not buying.** Bad selling is selling an unqualified prospect, and if they're qualified you're obliged to keep asking (StVqS0jD7Ls, RVbvhPGFi6E).

### Objections
- **Only three exist:** price (a value gap), stall, and decision-maker (bgmh7p1s7zs). The fuller model is the **onion of blame** (from Albert Ellis): circumstances → others → self, appearing as time, money/value, fit, authority, avoidance. Each layer is closer to the real decision (JE2_7elAcxM, NcD2t9qt-fM, iqAtBbGr3VY).
- **Money:** "Is this a lot to you? Good, you'll go all in." (JE2_7elAcxM) Separate price from belief (worth it if it adds $10K a month?). The Ferrari-for-$5K test: you'd find the money (NcD2t9qt-fM, oi7bnS8uyJM).
- **Authority:** "What if they say no?" About 1 in 3 say they'd do it anyway. Then "what would their objection be?" (JE2_7elAcxM), then past agreements, then forgiveness over permission, or a delayed close with a 3-day no-sweat window (oi7bnS8uyJM, cy2k1GdA-9o, 8VqSFBMMS4M).
- **Stall:** teach them how to decide. "Closer or further from your goal?" Use the options close: do it and succeed, skip it and fail, or do it and fail but guaranteed. Only walking out guarantees no result (NcD2t9qt-fM, JE2_7elAcxM).
- **Reframe with 3A:** Acknowledge → Associate → Ask. "No one can disagree with a question." Use straw men for hard truths (RVbvhPGFi6E).
- **Downsell ladder:** each step down asks for more from them (longer term, testimonial, review, bring a friend). After ~6 step-downs it isn't money, you lost them earlier: "Can I be real with you instead of nice to you?" (gza5RtQCVsA).
- **Prepay:** a 10–20% "prepayment discount" (never say "paid in full"). Present the highest discount first and step down (1UhvBSQFy6A).

### Upsells and the diagnostic sale
- **Upsell ≈ 5× the core price.** If ~20% take it, the average ticket doubles (JE2_7elAcxM, 1UhvBSQFy6A). One-on-one should be ~5× one-to-many, not 2× (8VqSFBMMS4M).
- **Sell mid-process, not only at the end.** End-only gets ~⅓; mid-process gets ~½ to ⅔+ (8VqSFBMMS4M).
- **Menu upsell:** unsell what they don't need → prescribe what they do → A/B choice → card on file (o64cI6tebnU, bx48qPlaGvE). Use low-margin items as "ghost products" to build trust (bx48qPlaGvE).
- **Yes-by-saying-no:** "You don't want anything else, do you?" Only for simple bolt-ons. He claims 90%+ uptake vs 20–30% normal (j1tA4l7R2c0).
- **Diagnostic sale:** questionnaire → card on file → current state → desired state → obstacle → present the vehicle with price tied to the goal → prepay incentive. "We didn't change what we delivered, we only changed how we presented it" (1UhvBSQFy6A). Selling by the week × weeks-to-goal converts "a way higher percentage" (6m6DCQMASEM).
- **Set honest expectations in sales.** Lowering them didn't reduce close rate, and outlier testimonials create disappointment (afbP6sB_Atc, hnfh2jqNVEQ).

### Team building and management
- **14-day onboarding:** ~40 recorded good calls, daily role-play, and a role-play test. Full schedule only after hitting the minimum close rate (2lA_A8BGRRs).
- **Cut fast:** a rep who can't close within 1–2 weeks (one full sales cycle) goes. "It's easier to take a 6 to a 9 than a 2 to a 5." (JE2_7elAcxM) Below KPI for 2 weeks → PIP. No improvement in 2 more weeks → fired (oi7bnS8uyJM, 2lA_A8BGRRs, _33XVdvO4Gc, okA9Yt2KZuk).
- **Cut the bottom ~10% regularly.** He says each cut gives about a 30% productivity jump (bgmh7p1s7zs). "If you're not cutting, you're communicating with the team that it's okay to suck" (2lA_A8BGRRs).
- **Daily training, 60 minutes, 5 days a week:** 25 minutes reading the script aloud with tone, 5 minutes drilling objections, 30 minutes reviewing a call. One correction at a time, drilled 3× correctly in a row (oi7bnS8uyJM, JE2_7elAcxM). Use word-blackout memorization until you "breathe the script" (sGakuNs9mT4, spXH1kJ6q-Q).
- **Cadence:** daily huddles (testimonials, a shared weakness), weekly 1:1s on the rep's tagged best, worst and average calls, monthly reviews, and a weekly sales + CS game-tape review (bgmh7p1s7zs, StVqS0jD7Ls, bHnVItVGJPA).
- **Competitions in 6-week cycles** with mixed small teams (3–4, best paired with worst). Prize ≈ 25% of a month's pay. Career-path micro-raises: $500 → $525 → $550 per deal at 50/100/150 deals (bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM).
- **Utilization:** if reps can take 10 calls a day and take 4, you have too many reps. Starved reps hard-close (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **Don't promote your best closer to manager.** Managers are servant-type and even-keeled (JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, 6m6DCQMASEM). No prima donnas: a cowboy star who ignores the script is a cancer long term (spXH1kJ6q-Q, JE2_7elAcxM).
- **Comp:** compensation attracts talent but rarely changes behavior. The exception is paying on cash collected (StVqS0jD7Ls). Don't pay closers the same whether they close or not (4GQLJjH9-oA).
- **Keep inbound and outbound setters and closers separate** (oi7bnS8uyJM, JE2_7elAcxM). Keep sales reps away from unhappy customers, because it kills conviction (afbP6sB_Atc).
- **Hiring:** judge candidates by the quality and quantity of metrics they track (c_6BrF7jOGk, JE2_7elAcxM). If you can pay well, poach from similar sale types (_33XVdvO4Gc).
- **Where the sources disagree:** RVbvhPGFi6E wants 2+ years of experience, but q32-l3Yoqg4 (Gym Launch era) prefers believers newer to sales. The later material (_33XVdvO4Gc, StVqS0jD7Ls) leans toward proven closers from similar businesses when you can pay [inference: experience matters more as ticket and complexity rise].

## Playbooks

### A. Run a CLOSER call, from oi7bnS8uyJM, q32-l3Yoqg4, bgmh7p1s7zs and StVqS0jD7Ls
1. **C**larify why they're here: "What made you reach out? Goal? Why that number?" (oi7bnS8uyJM)
2. **L**abel them with the problem: So what I'm hearing is X… does that sound right?
3. **O**verview past pain (the pain cycle): what they tried, for how long, and how it went. Repeat until their options are exhausted.
4. **S**ell the vacation with three pillars, in ≤2–3 minutes, using analogies and stories rather than features.
5. **E**xplain away concerns (price, stall, decision-maker). Reframe and ask again.
6. **R**einforce the decision after payment, then hand off live to onboarding (BAMFAM) within the first 48 hours (afbP6sB_Atc, q32-l3Yoqg4).

### B. Sales funnel turnaround in ~60 days, from c_6BrF7jOGk, JE2_7elAcxM and 4GQLJjH9-oA
1. Instrument show → offer → close (on shows and on offers) → cash up front → units, per rep.
2. Compare each stage with the benchmark and fix the biggest gap first, starting with *who* (hire an experienced director).
3. Show rate: SMS, a benefit-driven reminder sequence, behavior-triggered emails and manual reminders.
4. Optimize ads on cost per sale, and fix targeting if cancellations are high.
5. Trim to the reps the volume can support and raise quotas.
6. Rewrite the call frame (cut small talk, qualify for the guarantee) and shorten the script.

### C. Build a sales team from zero, from 2lA_A8BGRRs, oi7bnS8uyJM and okA9Yt2KZuk
1. Record your own calls and write the CLOSER script as questions only.
2. Hire for coachability. For outbound, hire BDRs with almost no screening and let them prove themselves.
3. Run 14-day onboarding, then a half schedule, then a full schedule once they hit the close-rate minimum.
4. Daily drills, weekly 1:1s, 6-week competitions, a daily leaderboard and a bell for each sale.
5. Cut anyone who doesn't close in their first cycle. PIP after 2 weeks below KPI.
6. Keep conviction alive: past clients on calls, testimonial videos at the huddle, and sales + CS weekly reviews.

### D. Outbound "farm" (ticket ≥ ~$15K), from okA9Yt2KZuk
1. Scrape lists → VAs verify → power dialer with local caller ID → 100 dials in a 4-hour shift.
2. BDR (paid $50 per show) books the SDS. The SDS spends 30 minutes giving real value, then 15 minutes switching hats to set the closer.
3. Expect a linear ramp. At Gym Launch it went 1 → 45 sales a month over 14 months, about 60% of volume. Each step takes 2–4 weeks to figure out.
4. Promote up the ladder (BDR → SDS → closer). Closers at ≤25% drop back to SDS.

### E. Add revenue per customer without changing delivery, from 1UhvBSQFy6A, bx48qPlaGvE, o64cI6tebnU and 6m6DCQMASEM
1. Swap transactional "call us when you want it again" (1UhvBSQFy6A) for a diagnostic sale with the price tied to the goal.
2. Take a card on file before the sale (complete your profile).
3. Present an upsell at ~5× the core price and a prepay discount (10–20%).
4. Add a menu or prescriptive close on add-ons attached to existing habits.
5. Make renewal the default: recurring billing, opt-out (8VqSFBMMS4M).

## Anti-patterns
- **Blaming "lead quality"** when the real issue is how leads are worked: slow follow-up, selling 9–5 weekdays only, limited slots (StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o, sBJppqCeFGI).
- **Teaching tactics on the call.** "They're not going to listen to you until they buy" (CojS0DwflXc).
- **Paragraph scripts, superstitious lines** ("the cough"), and reading the script flat (oi7bnS8uyJM, JE2_7elAcxM, sGakuNs9mT4).
- **Asking "any questions"** repeatedly. Talking more than the prospect: "the person who speaks the most in the sale loses" (2lA_A8BGRRs, sGakuNs9mT4).
- **Dropping price with no exchange** ("commission breath"), and price matching (gza5RtQCVsA, zzleYxkf39k, iqAtBbGr3VY).
- **Half-hearted pitches.** "Either ask hard or do not ask at all" (ThSFgC_4NVA).
- **Lazy selling:** leaning on your own story so you only close people like you (iqAtBbGr3VY).
- **Hiring reps and "hoping for the best"** He says ~95% of companies can't train sales (6m6DCQMASEM).
- **Promoting the top closer to manager,** or the CEO acting as sales manager (JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls).
- **Optimizing for the cheapest leads.** That produced 75% cancellations: "about the closest thing to literally burning money" (c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **Setters doing both outbound and nurture.** Quoting price on set calls (JE2_7elAcxM, c_6BrF7jOGk).
- **Mixing delivery and pitching.** Small 8-person Zoom calls instead of a 50–100-person webinar (8VqSFBMMS4M).
- **Ignoring customers right after the sale.** The first 48 hours decide long-term belief (q32-l3Yoqg4).
- **Not recording calls** (oi7bnS8uyJM, bgmh7p1s7zs).

## Worked examples
- **Two-call consumer service doubled in ~60 days.** Show rate 49 → 70%, close 27 → 41%, cash up front 47 → 82%, units 56 → 93 a month. Fixes: experienced sales director, new media buyer and retargeting, trimmed team, higher setter quotas (c_6BrF7jOGk, JE2_7elAcxM).
- **Webinar funnel unstuck.** Revenue ~$570K → ~$860K a month in 4 months with the same offer and team. Show rate 18 → 24%, booked calls 58 → 102 per webinar, close 14 → 35%, CAC $5,763 → $2,868, ROAS 1.62 → 3.96 (4GQLJjH9-oA).
