# Should I take a partner, give equity, do a deal, buy a business, or sell mine?

**Bottom line:** Equity is the most expensive thing you can give away. Take a partner only for money, skills or time you can't buy, and pay for everything else with cash, performance deals or phantom equity ([10 KEYS to a TERRIBLE Business Partnership [GUARANTEED!]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JShQ8BX08rs) (JShQ8BX08rs), [Here's Why I Always Give Away a % of My Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxpPynt1mis) (PxpPynt1mis), [What I Learned After Countless Failed Business Partnerships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DAm0EOes5to) (DAm0EOes5to)). Build the company so it could sell (founder-free, recurring, diversified, $5M+ EBITDA for institutional buyers) even if you never sell. "Rich people sell, wealthy people never sell." Sell only from strength, with a plan for the proceeds, and remember that terms, not price, decide what you keep ([I Reverse Engineered the Perfect Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HKbFUWJwEG0) (HKbFUWJwEG0), [I sold 8 businesses by age 32 [here’s how]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pWbSl7d0tEc) (pWbSl7d0tEc), [I Sold My Company at 31: My Detailed Breakdown of the Decision](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_gcqwupsza8) (_gcqwupsza8), [How wealthy people avoid paying taxes..my new plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=52tcB5FopAg) (52tcB5FopAg)).

## Diagnose first

**Questions to answer:**
1. **What does the other side bring that you can't pay for?** Money, skills or time. If you could hire it, don't give equity (JShQ8BX08rs, [Top Signs You Have The Wrong Business Partner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJfn_pWzFqg) (yJfn_pWzFqg), [No BS Advice to Get Rich in the Next 10 Years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZ-H_TjSzok) (oZ-H_TjSzok)).
2. **Is there a planned liquidity event?** If not, equity is mostly symbolic, so pay from profit or use performance agreements ([Watch These 51 Minutes if You Want to Grow Your Business in 2026 (Dave Ramsey Interview)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HJg4TYBgtI) (-HJg4TYBgtI), DAm0EOes5to).
3. **Is the role measurable?** Measurable → performance deal. Hard to measure, key, long-term → phantom equity (PxpPynt1mis).
4. **Business sellability**: founder dependence, recurring revenue, channel and customer concentration, EBITDA size, audit-ready financials (HKbFUWJwEG0, [Give Me 47 minutes, And I'll Fix Your Small Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sjt5G3YPjmY) (sjt5G3YPjmY)).
5. **Why do you want to sell?** Pain? Proving something? Or a better use of capital and attention? (_gcqwupsza8, 52tcB5FopAg)
6. **Deal risk**: what do they gain by betraying you vs. what do you risk? Has the operator done this 2–3+ years, and will they co-invest? ([How The Top 1% Actually Think About Trust](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k5-57282taI) (k5-57282taI), [I Lost $100 Million... Here's What I Learned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdLLGKoBojo) (tdLLGKoBojo))
7. **BATNA**: what's your alternative if this deal dies? ([How to get what you want every time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jc2UW3nlNBA) (Jc2UW3nlNBA))

**How the answer changes by stage:**

| Stage | Deal posture | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Starting out | Better with no partner. Bootstrap the first business. If you're a promoter, your first hire is a strong operational second, not a 50% partner | yJfn_pWzFqg, BO_59sGxztY, sL16tsGafcQ |
| <$1M profit | Key-man risk is normal. Small businesses sell as asset sales at ~1–3× (often 1–4×) and must roll equity. Focus on making money, not exit quality | fD-sxKiB30M, pWbSl7d0tEc, HKbFUWJwEG0, IFpHasZ7jN4 |
| $1–5M profit | Fix the deal killers (founder face, single channel, churn). Phantom equity for key leaders. Recaps / borrowing against equity become possible | okA9Yt2KZuk, PxpPynt1mis, 52tcB5FopAg |
| $5M+ EBITDA | Institutional buyers appear (he cites a median ~6.7× for institutional deals, source not recalled). $10M+ profit adds a size premium | pWbSl7d0tEc, fD-sxKiB30M, HKbFUWJwEG0 |
| Owner with excess cash | Invest in what you know (OPB), then consider fund management (OPM+OPB) once you have a track record | sL16tsGafcQ, BO_59sGxztY |

## Core principles
1. **Equity is the most expensive currency.** Pay for needs and give equity only to people who will really drive growth, judged 10 years out (JShQ8BX08rs).
2. **Grow the pie with slices.** "I'd rather own 20% of a billion dollar company than 100% of a million dollar company" ([13 Ways to DESTROY Your Competition (Legally)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lc8DNduiwKA) (Lc8DNduiwKA)).
3. **A partner is a marriage.** Mission, values and lifestyle must line up. Litmus test: given the same data, would you both decide the same way? (JShQ8BX08rs, [We Made a BIG Decision...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUI12JmD-lM) (OUI12JmD-lM))
4. **Write it down while you're in the honeymoon phase**: exit, death, money disputes, roles, whether to sell. Contracts for everything in your first ~10 years (DAm0EOes5to, [26 Harsh Lessons I Learned in 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YONIUpk-kAs) (YONIUpk-kAs)).
5. **"You get paid for what you do, you get returns on what you own."** Enterprise value = customers × lifetime gross profit ÷ risk (HKbFUWJwEG0, [I stepped down as CEO...and created a PASSIVE INCOME machine..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3tH9Sz1fEc) (s3tH9Sz1fEc)).
6. **Value can be forced up**: turn risks into "pillars of value" and both EBITDA and the multiple rise. That's why most billionaires come from PE ([How the top 1% make their money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fD-sxKiB30M) (fD-sxKiB30M)).
7. **Price is not proceeds.** Earn-outs, seller financing, rollover, escrow, working capital, indemnities and non-competes decide what you keep (pWbSl7d0tEc, 52tcB5FopAg).
8. **Leverage = willingness to walk.** "You get what you negotiate, not what you deserve" (Jc2UW3nlNBA, pWbSl7d0tEc, [42 Recession Proof Money Hacks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rp1PzCxj3eU) (rp1PzCxj3eU)).
9. **Wealthy people buy and build, and rarely sell.** Borrow against a growing asset instead of selling it (52tcB5FopAg, [The Ultra Rich Playbook [Legal & Tax-Free]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6x3re5-Ms1E) (6x3re5-Ms1E), _gcqwupsza8).
10. **Stay in your circle of competence.** He lost money the further he strayed from marketing, sales and product (tdLLGKoBojo). "Diversification is a hedge against ignorance" (_gcqwupsza8).
11. **Own the economics, not a fee.** Agencies and service providers capturing a fee should move toward partnering, buying or building the operating business ([3 Simple Fixes that Grow Any Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQuyQpFANpA) (KQuyQpFANpA)).
12. **Never trade reputation for money.** Leave meat on the bone so future deals happen ([Dangerously Honest Advice to Create Generational Wealth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m-k0_pQJ1fY) (m-k0_pQJ1fY), [Charlie Munger Changed My Life [8 Lessons]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-NLqtk4F4oY) (-NLqtk4F4oY), [14 Life Lessons I Wish I Knew Earlier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cq8GyLrEuAk) (cq8GyLrEuAk)).

## Decision rules & thresholds

### Partners
- **Only take a partner for money, skills or time you lack** (DAm0EOes5to, yJfn_pWzFqg, oZ-H_TjSzok, [If you want 2026 to be the best year of your life, please watch this video...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vhOV_Od0A3M) (vhOV_Od0A3M)). If you both have the same skills, specialize or split: "Better to recognize an error early than late" (yJfn_pWzFqg).
- **Every business needs three legs**: acquisition, product, operations. Give each person clear, non-overlapping ownership (yJfn_pWzFqg).
- **"I don't do agreements" → walk away** (JShQ8BX08rs). Can't afford two lawyers? Use a free template plus one paid lawyer hour (yJfn_pWzFqg).
- **Also agree on** schedules, vacation, responsiveness, exit "alimony" and whether to sell (yJfn_pWzFqg, DAm0EOes5to, YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Dinner test**: "Do you like having dinner with them?" If not, why give them half your business? (OUI12JmD-lM, Leila). Be *proud* to introduce them (JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Partner instead of hire** only when the skill can't be hired or would take a decade to grow (OUI12JmD-lM).
- **If a deal breaks down on final terms, something was wrong earlier.** Align first (OUI12JmD-lM).
- **Don't squeeze the partner and don't over-give.** Both breed renegotiation (JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Don't partner to fill one role.** "If the future isn't 100 partners, don't start today." A messy multi-partner structure was the lash chain's biggest constraint ([Building a $2,300,000/yr Business for a Stranger in 57 Mins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Koq14DPXC4) (-Koq14DPXC4)).
- **Ending a partnership**: say directly what you'd say behind their back, split equity and debt with simple documents, exit everything and "do right by everyone" (DAm0EOes5to, [Money Lessons I Learned In Marriage.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oK2_u-uS_Bo) (oK2_u-uS_Bo)). A promissory note can be one page (DAm0EOes5to).
- **Couples in business**: treat your spouse exactly like any other partner ([Leila Hormozi's Brutally Honest Advice About Relationships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NBhDR8R_YGk) (NBhDR8R_YGk), Leila).

### Equity & incentives
- **Need ≠ equity.** If you can pay an accountant, pay them (JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Measurable role (e.g. marketing) → performance deal.** Hard-to-measure key role → **phantom equity** that vests on a sale or liquidity event, in proportion to contribution (PxpPynt1mis).
- **No planned liquidity event → pay leaders from profit** rather than stock (-HJg4TYBgtI, Ramsey guest). Don't sell and they don't need control → no equity; use an agreement. A profit share is better for you, a revenue share is better for them (DAm0EOes5to).
- **Milestone equity kickers**, e.g. phantom equity unlocking at revenue milestones (DAm0EOes5to).
- **Benchmark**: full-service e-commerce marketing firms take ~10% of attributed top-line revenue with no equity (DAm0EOes5to).
- **Superstar CEOs**: ~5% profit share plus the right to buy in ([A BILLIONAIRE told me how to make BILLION DOLLARS...here's my plan...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_x6n-CmZqjo) (_x6n-CmZqjo), secondhand notes from a billionaire operator). Revenue-line leaders are paid on the bottom line (YONIUpk-kAs).
- **Unicorn talent you can't delegate**: align incentives heavily, or create liquidity by borrowing against shares instead of selling (sjt5G3YPjmY).

### Influencer / distribution deals
- **Four ways to monetize an audience**: affiliates → sponsorships → equity partnerships → own brand ([If I Wanted to Monetize An Audience in 2026, This is What I Would Do](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oRqnTOT9ZG8) (oRqnTOT9ZG8)). The more money you have, the more you should lean toward equity. As the influencer, get some equity up front, because "you transfer all of that goodwill" (oRqnTOT9ZG8).
- **Define deliverables precisely** (e.g. link in bio 1 week/month, 1 long-form mention, 5 shorts, a monthly whitelisting recording). Never just "I'll promote it" (oRqnTOT9ZG8).
- **Brand side**: target 50K–150K-follower accounts with real engagement and take a small cut (~20%) at first ([54.1% of People Who Try This Make their 1st Dollar Online](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFpHasZ7jN4) (IFpHasZ7jN4)). Make their side look better than yours. Send 100 messages a day until someone replies. Mid-size influencers may want growth more than money ([How to 10x Your Business Overnight with Influencers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z2tgZC_XkT4) (Z2tgZC_XkT4)).
- **Endorse only what "grows on its own"**, because the referrer gets blamed (IFpHasZ7jN4). Acquisition.com promotes only companies where it owns ≥50%, absolutely believes in the product, and the company can handle the inflow ([Leila Hormozi Explains Acquisition.com's Investment Strategy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TlnbPLpZBmQ) (TlnbPLpZBmQ), Leila).
- **Affiliate instead of building software.** Serious traffic can negotiate 20–50% commissions ([Starting a software company is a TERRIBLE F@#$ING IDEA... [TRIGGER WARNING]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pw1PcHfYP2w) (pw1PcHfYP2w)). An affiliate program needs an offer so good affiliates "feel stupid saying no" ([Breaking down Rihanna’s Company (Real Tactics You Can Use)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eX3Ch_HuR70) (eX3Ch_HuR70)).
- **Partnering vs. building**: partnering is faster but slices the pie. A $50M business likely spends ~$5–10M/yr on marketing, which is the size of the partnering opportunity (KQuyQpFANpA).
- **When a key supplier limits you, "buy it or build it"** (Lc8DNduiwKA).

### Selling a business
- **Sell-or-stay is a risk assessment.** Sure you'll grow → don't sell. Unsure → sell. "If what you do every day doesn't change, why sell?" Have a plan for what's next ([58 Mins of Advice That Will Blow Up Your Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rc7cxL7ql7Y) (rc7cxL7ql7Y)).
- **Don't sell to escape pain or to prove something.** He wants to sell most when in pain, so fix the pain by hiring (52tcB5FopAg, _gcqwupsza8). Before selling, decide what you'll buy with the proceeds that beats the business (6x3re5-Ms1E).
- **Don't sell early** if one more year can fix a few things and grow profit (fD-sxKiB30M).
- **Deal killers to fix first**: founder as the brand, single channel, churn. Buyers named all three at Gym Launch ([How I Scaled My Sales Team [Compensation, Recruiting & Training]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=okA9Yt2KZuk) (okA9Yt2KZuk), _gcqwupsza8). Also: no customer ≥20% of revenue (he prefers ≤5%), a leadership team running day-to-day, audit-ready financials (HKbFUWJwEG0, sjt5G3YPjmY).
- **Multiples**: <$10M revenue businesses trade at ~1–4× earnings (HKbFUWJwEG0) or as asset sales at 1–3× (pWbSl7d0tEc). Institutional buyers want $5M+ EBITDA (HKbFUWJwEG0, fD-sxKiB30M). A founder-face business won't get the multiple ([How gurus lie about how much MONEY they make...I'm calling it out..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=al_WXFDbN8I) (al_WXFDbN8I), [How to Build a Valuable Company You Can Sell Someday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VxKwz6hBVZU) (VxKwz6hBVZU)).
- **Model-specific value tests**: software with >~10% annual churn is "just a service business" (pw1PcHfYP2w; the caption is garbled). Software needs ≥80% yearly retention for a tech multiple, and licensing ~80% for a ~6× exit ([The Only 4 Ways To Scale A Service Business (PICK ONE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O1As2zxy0es) (O1As2zxy0es)). Accounting firms and franchise chains trade often; micro-gyms rarely sell (VxKwz6hBVZU).
- **The goose and the eggs**: keep the founder-led engine (goose) and sell the faceless, recurring assets it produces (eggs). If you're bigger than the company, raise a minority stake at the holdco instead of exiting (VxKwz6hBVZU).
- **Process** (pWbSl7d0tEc):
  - Meet ~4–6 bankers who recently sold businesses like yours in both market and size.
  - Contact hundreds of buyers ("got to talk to 500" (pWbSl7d0tEc)).
  - Write down your acceptable number, and why, before you talk to bankers.
  - Pay bankers on cash you receive, not total EV, and negotiate their upfront and breakup fees down.
  - Expect ~12–18 months. A "30-day close" (pWbSl7d0tEc) took 112 days. Your only lever is to keep growing hard.
  - Keep escrow under ~10%, minimize the working capital left in the business, narrow the non-compete, and fight indemnities beyond fundamental reps.
  - Anything you relied on must be in the schedules.
  - Consider taking ~10% less from people you like.
  - His terms: full exit, no earn-out, no seller financing ("if I keep working two years I might as well own it" (pWbSl7d0tEc)). Similar in rc7cxL7ql7Y.
- **Bankers inflate valuations to get you to sign** (_gcqwupsza8).
- **Recap instead of selling**: borrow against the business at ~70% of value (not 80%), keep 100% ownership, diversify the tax-free proceeds and refinance as value grows. This only works if the business keeps growing, and loans carry covenants (52tcB5FopAg, 6x3re5-Ms1E).

### Buying / investing in businesses
- **Opportunity-vehicle test**: (1) how many units could be sold, (2) profit per unit (value vs. cost to deliver), (3) competition and supply/demand. Service businesses need **≥80% gross margin** ([What I look for to invest in companies [no bs]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aOq1Hdz0w3k) (aOq1Hdz0w3k)).
- **LTGP:CAC > 3:1 or he tosses it out** ([Million Dollar Equations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QGcjweehrvU) (QGcjweehrvU)). Acquisition.com looks at $3M+ revenue, mostly $5M+; the average portfolio company was ~$17M ([I Built 4 Businesses In A Row To Show It's Not Luck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mqqbuM9sAk) (0mqqbuM9sAk)). Portfolio investing applies above ~$5M/yr ([I STARTED AND SOLD 6 BUSINESSES BY AGE 32...my boring formula..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sEQNnsaeiAs) (sEQNnsaeiAs)).
- **Only invest where you add outsized value** (Who / What / How: recruit leadership, fix strategy, supply experts). Then 51% isn't "grinding" the founder (TlnbPLpZBmQ, Leila).
- **Reject founders who lack the character to attract top talent** ([Leila Hormozi: Making Money Doesn't Change You with Brooke Castillo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nd22J08b1TA) (nd22J08b1TA), Leila). "If you would not do business with someone for a decade, do not do business with them for a day" ([$100M CEO: How I know who to trust...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kW2vDMAmlPI) (kW2vDMAmlPI)).
- **Buy a small business with seller financing** instead of making a down payment on a house: "agree on price, then agree on terms." Spending six months finding a near-free deal is fine (["I'm Broke... What Should I Do?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNl83INePXI) (KNl83INePXI)).
- **Tech/process reinvestment that raises the multiple**: ~$100K that adds ~3 turns on $1M profit = +$3M EV (fD-sxKiB30M).
- **Outside your core**:
  - Bet only a small share and treat it as gambling. Dip a toe: buy one unit before 5–10.
  - Only co-invest with operators who have done the deal for 2–3+ years and put their own money in.
  - "I'd rather have a very sure 20% than a really risky 40." (tdLLGKoBojo)
  - If a seller offers outsized returns, ask why they don't keep the assets.
  - Believe red flags the first time.
  (tdLLGKoBojo)
- **Don't use debt to fund M&A you don't know how to do.** "Do not risk the empire for a pot of gold" (_gcqwupsza8, [Top 10 Fast Business Lessons From My Last 10 Years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WsYgWC7NmO8) (WsYgWC7NmO8)).
- **Private lending**: you set the terms. Be first position on title. Require 10–20%+ after tax, because lending is taxed as ordinary income ([Hard Money Lending EXPLAINED [I lent $3,000,000 using this]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxKxeie5JFo) (gxKxeie5JFo), [My 4-Part Invesing Model (with real example)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnvkMpUu-7c) (BnvkMpUu-7c)).
- **The four wealth paths**: bootstrap → raise (only for years of planned losses) → invest → fund management (track record, proprietary deal flow, one thesis). In a pitch, never say "guaranteed" ([If You Want To Create Wealth In 2026, Watch This (4 Paths)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sL16tsGafcQ) (sL16tsGafcQ), [How to Become Ultra Wealthy (4 Methods)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BO_59sGxztY) (BO_59sGxztY)).
- **Franchising is the most expensive form of equity** and is usually chosen out of rush (BO_59sGxztY). It needs 100+ units to make real money (O1As2zxy0es).

### Negotiation (Jc2UW3nlNBA, rp1PzCxj3eU)
- **Build your BATNA before you sit down.** Needing the deal is "winning poker only on bluffing." (Jc2UW3nlNBA)
- **Never take the first offer or the standard deal.** Get 4–6 vendor bids. Counterparts often come back 3–4 days after you walk.
- **Anchor high on the opening and low on counters.** Shrink your concessions as you go.
- **Make multiple equivalent simultaneous offers (MESOs).** Trade reciprocity across unbundled variables.
- **Frame as investment vs. cost.**
- **On key points: "if I don't get this, the deal is off"** (pWbSl7d0tEc).
- **Vendors**: don't squeeze the last penny (cq8GyLrEuAk). Demand references "like you" at your scale (tdLLGKoBojo).

## Playbooks

### A. Someone wants equity (employee, marketer, would-be partner)
1. Can you pay for it? If yes, pay cash (JShQ8BX08rs).
2. Is the output measurable? If yes, a revenue or profit share or commission (PxpPynt1mis, DAm0EOes5to).
3. Key long-term leader, no planned sale? Profit share (-HJg4TYBgtI). Planned sale? Phantom equity vesting on liquidity, sized to contribution, with milestone kickers (PxpPynt1mis, DAm0EOes5to).
4. True partner (money, skills or time you lack)? Run the litmus tests: same decision on the same data, dinner, values 3/3 (JShQ8BX08rs, OUI12JmD-lM, kW2vDMAmlPI).
5. Write the agreement first: roles, exit, death, disputes, sale, schedules (DAm0EOes5to, yJfn_pWzFqg).

### B. Prepare a business for sale (2–3 years out)
1. Remove yourself: a leadership team, marketing without your face, 6 months of growth without you (HKbFUWJwEG0, [If I Wanted to Create a Business That Runs Itself, Here's What I'd Do](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4Pw66_490) (mr4Pw66_490)).
2. Add a second channel. No customer >20%. Recurring revenue. Fix churn (okA9Yt2KZuk, sjt5G3YPjmY).
3. Get audit-ready financials and automated metrics, and grow EBITDA toward $5M+ (HKbFUWJwEG0).
4. Write a letter to yourself with your number. Interview 4–6 bankers. Pay them on cash received (pWbSl7d0tEc).
5. Run a wide process to hundreds of buyers, keep growing during diligence, and negotiate terms more than price (pWbSl7d0tEc).
6. Have a plan for the morning after (rc7cxL7ql7Y, 6x3re5-Ms1E).

### C. "Should I sell?" (LVM89ik-7Kw) decision
Work through his seven variables: story, money, energy, team, future vision, what's best for the business, final call (_gcqwupsza8). Rule out bad reasons (pain, "legit," proof, spite). Compare against a recap (52tcB5FopAg) and against holding for one more year of fixes (fD-sxKiB30M). Sure of growth → keep; unsure → sell, with clean terms (rc7cxL7ql7Y).

### D. Agency or service provider wanting bigger outcomes
Find the real constraint. Move from capturing a fee toward owning the economics: partner, buy or build. Take "a half step back so you can take 20 steps forward", and don't try to run the agency while buying the operating business (KQuyQpFANpA).

## Anti-patterns
- **Giving away 50% for a role you could hire** (yJfn_pWzFqg, JShQ8BX08rs).
- **Rushed multi-partner expansion**: "The fastest way to 5 locations isn't the fastest way to 50" (-Koq14DPXC4).
- **Second chances for partners with fraud histories**. He says this burned him "the majority of the time" (oK2_u-uS_Bo, tdLLGKoBojo).
- **Bolting on software for stickiness or a tech multiple.** Buyers check real usage (pw1PcHfYP2w). Dev shops are "about as close to a complete scam as you can come" (0mqqbuM9sAk).
- **Selling to prove something, or because you're in pain** (_gcqwupsza8, 52tcB5FopAg).
- **Believing guru valuations.** Ask: yearly or lifetime? Collected or contracted? What multiple, on what line? (al_WXFDbN8I)
- **FOMO deals and "too good to be true" returns** (tdLLGKoBojo).
- **Inflated margins from not paying yourself market rate.** $300K profit minus a $200K manager is $100K (-Koq14DPXC4).
- **Over-diversifying when you know your space** (_gcqwupsza8).
- **Acting venture-backed while bootstrapped** (BO_59sGxztY).

## Worked examples
- **Gym Launch / Prestige Labs sale**: 2/3 stakes sold at a $46.2M valuation, over $50M total including ALAN, after 18 months to 2.5 years of deliberation. Money "was a wash" either way. He sold to focus on Acquisition.com (_gcqwupsza8, kW2vDMAmlPI). He credits fixing key-man risk for the valuation (sjt5G3YPjmY). Gym Launch had been valued at ~$150M pre-COVID (vhOV_Od0A3M).
- **Banker fee trap**: selling 10% at $1B EV with a 4%-of-EV fee = $40M of your $100M in proceeds. Pay on cash received (pWbSl7d0tEc).
- **Forced value**: a $1M-profit business at 5× gets $100K of tech that 5–10× delivery capacity, the multiple moves to ~8×, and +$3M EV results (fD-sxKiB30M).
- **Recap**: borrow ~70% of the business's value tax-free, keep 100% ownership, refinance as it grows (52tcB5FopAg).
- **Lash chain**: 7 locations, $2.3M revenue, ~$644K profit. Untangling the messy partnerships came before any tactic (-Koq14DPXC4).
- **Bridge loan**: $2.5M at 4%/month on a building with four offers at $5.1–5.6M, $300K earnest money and personal guarantees (BnvkMpUu-7c).

## Metrics to watch
- **7 days**: open deal terms vs. your walk-away list; BATNA status; banker/buyer contact count.
- **30 days**: % of revenue from the top customer and top channel; founder-dependent tasks remaining; partner agreement signed (yes/no); phantom equity plan back-tested [inference].
- **90 days**: EBITDA trend toward $5M+; revenue retention; months the business has grown without you; multiple-relevant risk items closed; ROIC on outside investments vs. a sure 20%.
