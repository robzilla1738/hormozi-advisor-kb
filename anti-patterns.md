# Hormozi Anti-Patterns — what not to do

Deduped and grouped. Each entry gives the mistake, why it hurts, and the videos where he warns against it. Paraphrase unless in quotes.

## Offers & product
- **Fixing marketing when the offer is the problem.** A better offer changed one agency's ROAS from 0.5:1 to 11.2:1 with the same spend. (4GjwtnA76ig, F3NyhOtRpOE, pxVeOkOVr2w)
- **Adding features or programs to fix results.** Overwhelm drives churn, and subtraction often retains more. (-j8_YCWZ05Q, -JLN1d1ZKUU)
- **Filler bonuses.** Each bonus must be worth the price on its own. (5MjjpB8SPMo)
- **Too many products or tiers early.** Sell one core offer and flex the terms. (EZLQzR-_83A, R7Fin9HFhoM, TIH1w-KuATk)
- **Blaming marketing when nobody refers.** No referrals means the product isn't good enough. (TNQyFxVbkBs, Uki3IUkUu7Q)
- **Bolting software onto a service business for a higher multiple.** (pw1PcHfYP2w)

## Pricing
- **Cutting price to fix churn or to match a competitor.** His cut cost about $5M a year and churn didn't move. (BZQtuK-ucDM, tdLLGKoBojo, BYpTRiRqS1Y)
- **Selling out of your own wallet.** Pricing on what *you* would pay. (k-3PoOT4vOM, ZuJryiwxjDw)
- **Pricing in the middle of the market.** (uWdIgftpvBI, spXH1kJ6q-Q)
- **Cost-plus pricing, or hourly pricing for outcome work.** (BMF2fWHyDrg, 3yAiVjcImQ4, 6Fg4VXjRphQ)
- **Running services below 80% gross margin.** (9xAMe0QBFhU, aOq1Hdz0w3k, mRlSb0O5QNU)
- **Treating a payment plan as continuity.** (FiL0r5_dUvM)
- **Next tier only +50%.** Upsells should be 5–10× the core. (ZuJryiwxjDw)

## Lead gen & marketing
- **Under-volume.** Sending 300 flyers and calling the channel dead. (2tHlHWgDRdQ, HsQeQM1jUeg)
- **Chasing new channels before maxing the working one.** (--9kOXNCUdE, oRMG_HpOAN4, SmiOK8Yun4s)
- **Calling the market saturated** when the real problem is skill. (hnmBXTyMFKI, 9gVdCR7W8o8)
- **Treating organic content as lead gen** without funding it like paid. (MD5-HByRxoA)
- **Optimizing for views instead of revenue.** His top-viewed videos made zero sales. (21flGkcZO3A, JOfsujnXxCg)
- **Asking more than giving.** Asks spend goodwill; stay at 3:1 give:ask or better. (5cOwh-8scu8)
- **Clever over clear copy, and promise without proof.** (HxEQCHpZzHk, JDR-R--4HhM, fSbqaTlWaYI)
- **Selling a $30K offer to cold traffic** with no front end. (n6SHLmKcY0E)
- **Spending on brand-building as a local business** before the product is great. (LGq0uVspNms)

## Sales
- **Slow lead response and calendars booked far out.** (StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o, A_tx40lNpf8)
- **Pitching before you expect a yes, or arguing with the prospect.** (CojS0DwflXc, RVbvhPGFi6E)
- **Long pitches.** Keep the pitch under about 3 minutes. (q32-l3Yoqg4, oi7bnS8uyJM)
- **Training mediocre reps instead of selecting great ones.** (_33XVdvO4Gc, bgmh7p1s7zs)
- **Overloading sales calendars** above 85%. (A_tx40lNpf8)
- **Overselling expectations.** It drives escalations and churn. (afbP6sB_Atc, hnfh2jqNVEQ)

## Retention
- **Pouring ad spend into a leaky bucket.** (Uki3IUkUu7Q, U_s0ekwPK5g, XZEjp2mP1U0)
- **Ignoring month one.** Churn is highest early. (-j8_YCWZ05Q, 3fsJFUvA6Ts)
- **Selling to customers who structurally fail.** (thDTmy7VGIw, hnfh2jqNVEQ, CoPs-Bk8M9Y)
- **Stingy refunds and defensive service.** (p39nLmVtOjc)
- **Asking for testimonials long after the win.** (7wN8n3qrqnQ)

## People & management
- **Blaming the person before checking communication and training.** (IMowPVgcWbA, aUnorufFIq8, s6tkRztZwYc)
- **Changing comp for problems comp doesn't solve.** Also, trying to make employees happy rather than serve the company. (cemduJKQl5w)
- **Tolerating non-performers.** (Jv3GmZ9Odko, F84olnKkseM, -UzJOk85OZI)
- **Over-SOP-ing instead of hiring competent people,** and punishing more than rewarding. (oK2_u-uS_Bo)
- **Giving equity to fix a need that a hire or a revenue share would solve.** (DAm0EOes5to, yJfn_pWzFqg)
- **Overhiring support reps.** Two separate incidents, not duplicates. (0mqqbuM9sAk, noO8ERpgMr4)
- **(Leila Hormozi) Adding headcount or channels before management fundamentals are in place.** (2JLU4R8Zcgg)

## Ops & scaling
- **Constant change.** Each change costs about 20% in performance. (hfwZwPGsbIo, XwzU4RikbGs, uYds0zcAFWM)
- **Adding locations or franchising on weak unit economics.** (_8olk4-VFP8, O1As2zxy0es)
- **Adding capacity when you should raise price.** (sGv2BTUCcCM, uaLNfijnp-8)
- **Working on many fixes in parallel** instead of the one constraint. (TFxT3G5jwtU, -JLN1d1ZKUU, s59jbVBprn0)
- **Meetings that shred maker time.** (GIRkQQHzsxI)
- **Breaking a working model** because the slow fix feels "not scalable." (kQFSiEDvXws)
- **Expensive build-outs.** His gym 2 cost $250K and made no more than his $40–50K gym 1. (KNl83INePXI)

## Money & deals
- **Watching revenue instead of LTGP:CAC and cash.** (QGcjweehrvU, Cr0KdqZ954c)
- **Being asset rich and cash poor,** or betting on an exit. (EPEjYEihZ1s, _gcqwupsza8)
- **Key-man, single-channel or single-customer concentration.** (sjt5G3YPjmY, pWbSl7d0tEc, fD-sxKiB30M)
- **FOMO investing, over-borrowing, ruin-level risk** and dealing with bad people. (rp1PzCxj3eU, INm4U2S7Vu8)
- **Tax shelters** that cost more than they save. (w5g0JiO7OdE)
- **Trusting bankers to get the best deal,** expecting headline 20× multiples, or getting greedy after the LOI. (pWbSl7d0tEc)
- **Paying franchise fees** that eat most of the profit. (xZ8d9g6BcKM)
- **Running the business for a hypothetical acquirer.** (_gcqwupsza8)

## Mindset & focus
- **Chasing "the woman in the red dress":** side businesses and shiny opportunities. (MLkFOaxIdxM, 5Kt-EYieNko, rhVxX5_8xUw)
- **Starting a second business at a plateau, or out of boredom.** (s59jbVBprn0, ug5N9qmFVKQ, yflKMUffctE)
- **Switching paths constantly** instead of committing. (-UzJOk85OZI, 0_Gf5v8DEMY)
- **Setting goals on outcomes instead of controllable inputs.** (0_Gf5v8DEMY, noO8ERpgMr4)
- **Making decisions from urgency and emotion.** (noO8ERpgMr4, fcyIoN8CUOk)
- **Accepting industry standards as limits.** (A248pGXTSoY)
- **Chasing opportunities before solving known problems.** (zNiXk_3C_Io)
