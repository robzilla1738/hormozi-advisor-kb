# Hormozi Playbooks — step by step

Deduped, ordered procedures built from the per-video notes. Steps are paraphrased from the cited videos. Sequencing that combines several videos is marked `[inference]`. Pair each playbook with the matching `decisions/` file.

**Diagnostic order (use before picking a playbook)** [inference, built from f_ceDZqhKbw, sGv2BTUCcCM, kloJJeiysxg, QGcjweehrvU]:
1. Unit economics: LTGP:CAC, 30-day cash and gross margin (P13).
2. Constraint type: supply vs demand (P9).
3. Leak location: offer → funnel → sales → retention.
4. Run the playbook for the single biggest leak.

## P1. Build a Grand Slam Offer
Sources: (5MHQr-Z17Hc, _qspvJAq34M, pxVeOkOVr2w, neTSqOAMgao, 5MjjpB8SPMo, s7QA1TJKlbQ)
1. Pick a starving crowd: pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing. (qxQIcDrre1E, nIk3DedjxJM)
2. Write the dream outcome in one sentence in the buyer's words.
3. Raise perceived likelihood with proof: case studies, numbers, guarantees.
4. Cut time delay by designing a day-1 or week-1 win.
5. Cut effort and sacrifice: do it for them where possible.
6. Problem dump: list every obstacle in the journey and turn each into a solution.
7. Trim and stack: keep the high-value, low-cost items. Every bonus must be worth the price on its own. (5MjjpB8SPMo)
8. Add enhancers: scarcity, urgency, a guarantee or pay-per-result, and a name. (4GjwtnA76ig, F3NyhOtRpOE, mHrAjWni65E)
9. Price it high and test the close rate (P5).

**Exit test:** higher close rate or higher price without hero selling. (pxVeOkOVr2w)

## P2. Lead magnet front end
Sources: (7NMH1oAkgLY, Mst4hreQYl0, Ds_Qp2U5I8U, nrounb8NlFQ, SmiOK8Yun4s)
1. Pick a narrow problem whose solution reveals the next, paid problem.
2. Choose the format: reveal the problem, offer a trial, or deliver one step of many. Deliver it as software, info, a service or a physical item.
3. Make it better than competitors' paid stuff. Test the name.
4. Qualify on the opt-in. For high-intent offers, a ~30% opt-in is the benchmark. (nrounb8NlFQ)
5. Follow with a short VSL, an FAQ in buyers' words and a BANT-qualified call.
6. Repeat a clear CTA with a reason to act now.

## P3. Core Four lead gen from zero
Sources: (CaiLcj6tzBQ, HsQeQM1jUeg, w7g08dVTwaE, u4kgZ3pf8cU, nIk3DedjxJM, 2tHlHWgDRdQ)
1. Write three contact lists and send 100 personalized warm messages a day.
2. Offer the first 5–20 customers the service free or cheap in exchange for use, feedback, a review and referrals.
3. Ask every customer, and every non-buyer, for referrals.
4. Add content (hook → retain → reward), cold outreach at volume, then paid ads.
5. Hold the Rule of 100 for 100 days before judging a channel.
6. Once proof exists, raise price about 20% every few sales. (reisEL_D7xc)

## P4. Scale a working channel before adding new ones
Sources: (--9kOXNCUdE, oRMG_HpOAN4, o64cI6tebnU, 9gVdCR7W8o8, ULGT0Qpglek, MH-IMJxbUY4)
1. Fix attribution (UTMs, source tracking). (HGZOxBfnF-E, 21flGkcZO3A)
2. Exhaust the current channel: more volume, more creative (70/20/10), longer runs.
3. Exhaust adjacent methods, platforms and media.
4. Then expand the market: up, down, adjacent, broader, narrower. Under $10M, go narrower. (M2VP64Tc60w)
5. Add leverage: affiliates, agencies, employees, influencer partners. (60_7PU9JDIw, fd-hi3NqMYo, Z2tgZC_XkT4)

## P5. Raise prices
Sources: (k-3PoOT4vOM, A_tx40lNpf8, Gy-RmpRif-I, uWdIgftpvBI, BMF2fWHyDrg, 0EqJD2o-Mnk)
1. Read the close rate. ≥80% means raise hard (typically 3–4× underpriced). Around 30–40% is right. (A_tx40lNpf8)
2. For new customers, raise price on the next batch and watch close rate and churn.
3. For existing customers, send the structured letter: re-sell the current value, preview improvements, give a values-based reason and frame the cost as small. (Gy-RmpRif-I)
4. Pair the increase with a stronger guarantee where possible. (0EqJD2o-Mnk)
5. Add a capped premium tier at 5–10× the core. (uWdIgftpvBI, ZuJryiwxjDw)
6. In thin-margin or retail businesses, test small increases on the top SKUs. (BMF2fWHyDrg, h9UyQiLjSHg)

## P6. Sales system (CLOSER + team)
Sources: (q32-l3Yoqg4, oi7bnS8uyJM, StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o, bgmh7p1s7zs, 2lA_A8BGRRs, 4GQLJjH9-oA)
1. **Outside the call:** respond in under 60 seconds, book same-day or within 7 days, send SMS reminders, keep the calendar about 70–75% booked, and route the best leads to the best closers. (StVqS0jD7Ls, A_tx40lNpf8, cy2k1GdA-9o)
2. **On the call, run CLOSER:**
   - **C**larify why they're there.
   - **L**abel the problem.
   - **O**verview past pain.
   - **S**ell the vacation (under 3 minutes).
   - **E**xplain away concerns.
   - **R**einforce the decision. (q32-l3Yoqg4)
3. Handle objections by peeling them in order (time → money → fit → authority → avoidance) and using the 3A reframe. (NcD2t9qt-fM, RVbvhPGFi6E)
4. If they don't buy, step down the exchange ladder and ask "Fair enough?" at each step. (gza5RtQCVsA)
5. **Team:**
   - Hire for coachability and onboard over 14 days with about 40 calls.
   - Review calls daily or weekly.
   - Run leaderboards and 6-week competitions.
   - Cut the bottom 10% regularly. (2lA_A8BGRRs, bgmh7p1s7zs)
6. Refill rep belief with weekly client wins and testimonial reads. (bHnVItVGJPA)

## P7. Reduce churn
Sources: (-j8_YCWZ05Q, sPkMHh8zTMI, y3T4GqoH0Us, yPDQCfrwh8E, afbP6sB_Atc, hnfh2jqNVEQ, QTZsh3BgOwY)
1. Compute inflow ÷ churn to get your ceiling, and track by cohort. (XZEjp2mP1U0)
2. Profile churned customers against your best ones, then qualify the front end toward the high-LTV group. (hnfh2jqNVEQ)
3. Choreograph the first 24 hours and run 1:1 onboarding to an activation point. (yPDQCfrwh8E, afbP6sB_Atc, QTZsh3BgOwY)
4. Add unlockables past the usual churn point. Track attendance or usage and escalate outreach when it drops. (sPkMHh8zTMI, y3T4GqoH0Us)
5. Run exit interviews that try to save the customer or move them up a tier. (sPkMHh8zTMI)
6. Subtract: survey members on what they would fight to keep, then cut the overwhelm. (-j8_YCWZ05Q)
7. Move people to annual or prepaid terms and add ascension at milestones. (41EvCgwPrDc, QTZsh3BgOwY)
8. Do not cut price. (BZQtuK-ucDM)

## P8. Hiring & delegation
Sources: (7NqsFh5ej0w, awOQDy_o9ww, IMowPVgcWbA, aUnorufFIq8, kW2vDMAmlPI, cemduJKQl5w, ZklXJzHzW2U)
1. Audit your time and buy back the lowest-value hours first: admin, support, then sales. (7NqsFh5ej0w, gD0X-PLax5I)
2. Define 3 core values and hire and fire on them. (kW2vDMAmlPI)
3. (Leila Hormozi) Write the job description pain-first like ad copy, and separate the culture and technical interviews. (ZklXJzHzW2U)
4. Delegate with clear expectations, measurement and feedback cadence. (IMowPVgcWbA, 2JLU4R8Zcgg — Leila)
5. When work isn't done, check in order: did they know what, how and when; was something blocking them; then motivation. (aUnorufFIq8, s6tkRztZwYc)
6. Pay stars to stay and cut low performers quickly. (cemduJKQl5w, Jv3GmZ9Odko)

## P9. Supply vs demand triage
Sources: (sGv2BTUCcCM, OQf2Ba-Lp_4, jqo0lVveh98, N5MExtki_VI, uaLNfijnp-8)
1. Test: if you doubled ad spend tomorrow, could you deliver? If no, you're supply-constrained.
2. **Supply-constrained:**
   - Raise price 50–100%.
   - Offload low-skill steps from the bottleneck.
   - Change the delivery ratio (e.g. 1:1 → 1:4).
   - Productize, then hire.
3. **Demand-constrained:** fix the channel volume and creative, then the offer.

## P10. Customer-financed growth / cash fast
Sources: (XwZH-lOKG9c, GKvAQIe5vpo, nSQdjim8CsE, lXRqOG2yn6E, SvIcS-Q1Hl4, xRxVpjeHmeA)
1. Design the money model: attraction offer → upsell → downsell → continuity. (nSQdjim8CsE)
2. Front-load cash so 30-day gross profit covers CAC and COGS, ideally twice over. (XwZH-lOKG9c)
3. Pre-sell before you open, using a risk-reversed challenge and crediting it toward annual. (lXRqOG2yn6E, unshZobTt6Q)
4. **Cash crisis:** sell to existing customers, matched to speed:
   - a paid-in-full text blast (tomorrow)
   - an internal challenge (about 2 weeks)
   - a giveaway or list reactivation (SvIcS-Q1Hl4, xRxVpjeHmeA, OpeN4O5myIg)

## P11. Make the business sellable / exit
Sources: (pWbSl7d0tEc, sjt5G3YPjmY, fD-sxKiB30M, HKbFUWJwEG0, sEQNnsaeiAs, mr4Pw66_490)
1. Audit concentration risks:
   - key-man
   - one channel above 50% of leads
   - one customer at or above 20% of revenue
   - a single vendor (sjt5G3YPjmY)
2. Remove yourself: run a self-inventory, delegate with scorecards and systemize marketing. (mr4Pw66_490)
3. Show 12–18 months of sustained growth before going to market. (pWbSl7d0tEc)
4. Go to market through a size-matched banker. Reach about 500 buyers and negotiate terms, not just the headline price. (pWbSl7d0tEc)
5. Sell only if the reinvested proceeds plus your next venture beat the stake you'd keep. (_gcqwupsza8)

## P12. Buy a business
Sources: (KNl83INePXI, bgBIO6nZawg, xZ8d9g6BcKM, VxKwz6hBVZU)
1. Build sellable skills and savings.
2. Scan local businesses you know and find motivated, off-market sellers.
3. Agree on price first, then push the terms: seller financing plus SBA. (KNl83INePXI)
4. Apply your operating playbook: price, offer and retention.
5. Roll up the adjacent customers you already serve. (bgBIO6nZawg)

Note: the seller-financing figures in KNl83INePXI don't fully add up in the captions.

## P13. Unit-economics health check
Sources: (A_tx40lNpf8, QGcjweehrvU, Cr0KdqZ954c, 9xAMe0QBFhU, i7bLRKwKSms)
1. Compute fully loaded CAC.
2. Compute LTGP (gross-profit LTV) and the LTGP:CAC ratio. Targets are 3/6/9/12:1 depending on how many humans are in the loop.
3. Check 30-day cash against CAC + COGS.
4. Check gross margin. Services should be at or above 80%.
5. Check retention against your inflow ceiling.
6. Any metric below threshold becomes the fix before you scale spend.

## P14. Break a plateau ($1–10M)
Sources: (s59jbVBprn0, F84olnKkseM, cnbHgYYHzyE, yflKMUffctE, TFxT3G5jwtU, R7Fin9HFhoM)
1. Ask "what stops 5×?" and phrase the single constraint as a how-do-I question.
2. Check the six horsemen of stagnation: underpricing, overcomp, infinite avatars, overextension and the rest. (cnbHgYYHzyE)
3. Audit for your ideal customer, who is often worth 5–10× on the same infrastructure. (F84olnKkseM)
4. Kill side projects (the magic-wand test) and go better → more → new. (yflKMUffctE)
5. Build the back-end value ladder to lift LTV. (R7Fin9HFhoM)
