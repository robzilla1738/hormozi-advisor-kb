# Hormozi Principles — the ranked core

Distilled from 470 hand-written video notes (`notes/<id>.md`). One sharp sentence, the why, and the videos where it is argued most directly. Principles are ranked within each theme (most load-bearing first). Text is paraphrase unless in quotes; quotes are verbatim from note "Notable lines". `[inference]` = cross-video synthesis, not a single claim Alex makes.

Speaker note: Leila Hormozi speaks in 2JLU4R8Zcgg, ZklXJzHzW2U and several other people-management videos; those are attributed to her explicitly below.

## The 12 master principles (start here)

1. **There are only two ways to grow: more customers or make each worth more.** Every activity should ladder to traffic, conversion, price or churn; anything else is noise. (XwZH-lOKG9c, MwhsM7qijy8, f_ceDZqhKbw, kloJJeiysxg)
2. **Fix the offer before the marketing.** A good enough offer removes the need for hard selling, great copy or great creative. Changing only the offer took one agency from 0.5:1 to 11.2:1 ROAS. (pxVeOkOVr2w, 4GjwtnA76ig, F3NyhOtRpOE, neTSqOAMgao)
3. **Price is the single largest profit lever. Never cut price to fight churn or competitors.** His 20–25% cut left churn unchanged and cost about $5M a year. (k-3PoOT4vOM, BZQtuK-ucDM, tdLLGKoBojo, BYpTRiRqS1Y)
4. **Judge any business on LTGP:CAC and payback.** Use lifetime *gross profit*, not revenue, against fully loaded CAC, and aim to recover CAC within 30 days. (QGcjweehrvU, Cr0KdqZ954c, A_tx40lNpf8, i7bLRKwKSms)
5. **A business grows up to its nearest constraint. Find the one and fix only that.** Ask "why can't we do 5× more?" and turn the answer into a single how-do-I question. (f_ceDZqhKbw, s59jbVBprn0, TFxT3G5jwtU, QwEb78e5a8Y)
6. **Better → more → new.** Doing more of what already works is the lowest-risk growth. Every change costs about 20% in performance, so only bet on large upside. (yflKMUffctE, hfwZwPGsbIo, XwzU4RikbGs, 0lMn_-EXyhQ, oRMG_HpOAN4)
7. **Volume solves most acquisition problems.** Most "failed" channels were under-volumed. Output = volume × skill × time. (2tHlHWgDRdQ, HxEQCHpZzHk, WrCt0R3FBFs)
8. **Fix the leaky bucket before pouring in water.** Inflow ÷ churn sets your hypothetical maximum size, so retention moves the ceiling. (XZEjp2mP1U0, f_ceDZqhKbw, Uki3IUkUu7Q)
9. **Product quality is the real marketing.** If customers aren't referring, the product isn't good enough yet. (TNQyFxVbkBs, DiQ3N8F1Hl8, K8MFC9t7snY)
10. **Talent is the biggest arbitrage.** Hire selectively, raise the bar with every hire, and cut fast. (KhFlD54nQrY, _33XVdvO4Gc, Jv3GmZ9Odko)
11. **Build an asset that runs without you.** Key-man, single-channel and concentration risks crush value. (mr4Pw66_490, sjt5G3YPjmY, fD-sxKiB30M, V7oUcAfof34)
12. **Focus is what you say no to.** Attention, not time, is the scarce resource, and side businesses tax the main one. (rhVxX5_8xUw, 6ZEZWuVC8-8, MLkFOaxIdxM, uRhArskhqms)

## 1. Offers & value

1. **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice).** The fortunes are on the bottom of the equation: cut time and effort toward zero. "Fast beats free." (_qspvJAq34M, 5MHQr-Z17Hc, s7QA1TJKlbQ)
   - *Why:* buyers mostly compare you against inaction. Speed and ease are where moats form.
2. **Build one exceptional core offer and flex on terms, not tiers.** Two offers past $1M is the most you need. A bigger upfront payment makes a stickier customer. (EZLQzR-_83A, pxVeOkOVr2w, R7Fin9HFhoM)
3. **Every bonus must be worth the price on its own. Solve every problem the buyer will hit, including the ones they can't foresee yet.** (5MjjpB8SPMo, neTSqOAMgao)
4. **Risk reversal and guarantees raise conversion more than they raise refunds.** Pair them with outcome or pay-per-result terms where you can. (4GjwtnA76ig, F3NyhOtRpOE, unshZobTt6Q, lXRqOG2yn6E)
5. **Win on one vector (speed, risk, cost or ease): the one your customer values most.** (s7QA1TJKlbQ)
6. **Repackage what you already have.** Byproducts ("sawdust") and excess capacity become new or more valuable offers. (t9Vv7rK-4Jc, vZfatNSouDQ)
7. **A lead magnet is a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next, paid problem.** "Your free stuff should be better than their paid stuff." (7NMH1oAkgLY, Mst4hreQYl0, IFpHasZ7jN4)
8. **Learn to monetize free.** Give real value up front and make the money on the back end. (pxVeOkOVr2w, ovL6Z5z0jxQ, nSQdjim8CsE)

## 2. Pricing

1. **Price on value created, not on your wallet or cost-plus.** In his example a 10× price produced 31× the profit. (k-3PoOT4vOM, BMF2fWHyDrg, ZuJryiwxjDw)
   - *Why:* margin funds better talent, delivery, results and reputation. This is the virtuous price cycle.
2. **Close rate tells you your price headroom.** Closing ≥80% typically means you're underpriced by 3–4×; around 30–40% is about right. (A_tx40lNpf8, ZuJryiwxjDw, OQf2Ba-Lp_4)
3. **Never compete on price.** Lowering prices usually makes you less money and leaves churn unchanged. Tripling gym prices lost a third of clients but doubled revenue. (BZQtuK-ucDM, BYpTRiRqS1Y, tdLLGKoBojo)
4. **Go premium or go mass, never the middle.** "The middle is where people die." (uWdIgftpvBI, spXH1kJ6q-Q)
5. **Add a capped tier at 5–10× the core.** About 10% uptake can double revenue. The next tier is 5–10×, not +50%. (uWdIgftpvBI, ZuJryiwxjDw, ovL6Z5z0jxQ, MwhsM7qijy8)
   - *Why:* "customers are fractal." The top 20% will pay multiples.
6. **Services need ≥80% gross margin.** Each halving of delivery cost roughly doubles per-unit profit and lets you outspend rivals. Price services at 5–10× true delivery cost. (9xAMe0QBFhU, aOq1Hdz0w3k, mRlSb0O5QNU, A_tx40lNpf8)
7. **When demand exceeds supply, raise price or cut supply.** Do not add capacity first. (iFhiK5zORGk, sGv2BTUCcCM, uaLNfijnp-8)
8. **At thin margins, tiny price moves are huge.** About +6% can double profit, and $16→$16.99 is roughly +30% profit. (BMF2fWHyDrg, h9UyQiLjSHg)
9. **Raise prices on existing customers with a structured letter.** Re-sell current value, preview improvements and frame the cost as small. (Gy-RmpRif-I)
10. **Serve the rich.** They pay for speed and convenience, and higher prices raise commitment and results. (ONV__y1z7MI)
11. **Charge one-time value up front and ongoing value monthly.** A payment plan is not continuity. (TooAB8Ow6cQ, FiL0r5_dUvM, 41EvCgwPrDc)

## 3. Lead generation & marketing

1. **The Core Four are warm outreach, cold outreach, content and paid ads. Leverage comes from referrals, employees, agencies and affiliates.** Start with warm outreach and free work for reviews and referrals. (CaiLcj6tzBQ, HsQeQM1jUeg, w7g08dVTwaE, u4kgZ3pf8cU)
2. **Rule of 100: 100 qualifying actions a day for 100 days.** Volume compensates for low skill. (2tHlHWgDRdQ, HsQeQM1jUeg, A_tx40lNpf8, prj1bnTAM8A)
3. **Squeeze the working channel before adding new ones.** Most markets are 100–1000× bigger than you think. "Saturated" usually means unskilled. (--9kOXNCUdE, hnmBXTyMFKI, 9gVdCR7W8o8, o64cI6tebnU)
4. **Organic content is lead nurture, not lead gen, for most businesses.** Only treat it as lead gen if you fund it like paid. (MD5-HByRxoA)
5. **Give far more than you ask.** Never go below 3:1 give:ask. Give in public and sell in private. (5cOwh-8scu8, HxEQCHpZzHk, UGEc9-7X3OQ)
6. **Optimize content for revenue (RPM, opt-ins, sales), not views.** Make it for your top-spending buyer. (21flGkcZO3A, JOfsujnXxCg, HVOubeXUcx0, 0S5xsICW8qg)
7. **Proof beats promise. Lead with it, and open with promise → proof → plan.** (HxEQCHpZzHk, fSbqaTlWaYI, UGEc9-7X3OQ, XsWSvz-aewA)
8. **Creative volume is usually the cap on paid ads.** Make many variations of winners (70/20/10) and feed a UGC loop. (MH-IMJxbUY4, j2TZMFkj71Q, fSbqaTlWaYI)
9. **Ask for referrals systematically at peak moments.** Across his businesses this adds about 30% more customers. Referral% ÷ churn% above 1 means passive growth. (qpQvdBFW_yI, IFpHasZ7jN4, B-ogfFiQpXg, 4twK8Yl4iUI)
10. **Treat affiliates as customers.** Give them three-party-win prizes, plug-and-play collateral and a 30-second signup. (60_7PU9JDIw, DaRXece2ItE, FMzKk73iUhw)
11. **Brand is deliberate pairing with what your ideal customer likes.** It pays off in CTR, conversion, price and repurchase. For local businesses, fix the product first. (VQM3DrnVTcs, 9unucIBuNio, 4Yz8ggEv0NU, LGq0uVspNms)

## 4. Sales

1. **Sales is a transference of belief. Conviction beats technique.** (42SOlVhDwWE, OmDv6-651SM, bHnVItVGJPA)
2. **Fix sales outside the call first:** call back in under 60 seconds, book same day, raise show rate, route the best leads to the best closers. Availability is the #1 predictor of sales. (StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o, 4GQLJjH9-oA, A_tx40lNpf8)
3. **Run CLOSER (Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell the vacation, Explain concerns, Reinforce).** Speak only in questions, restatements and stories, and keep the pitch under about 3 minutes. (q32-l3Yoqg4, oi7bnS8uyJM, CojS0DwflXc)
4. **Objections are obstacles to peel.** Work through time → money → fit → authority → avoidance. The goal is a decision, not a sale. (NcD2t9qt-fM, iqAtBbGr3VY, JE2_7elAcxM, RVbvhPGFi6E)
5. **Anchor high and step down through an exchange ladder.** Each step trades something: longer commitment, no commitment, a testimonial, a friend. (gza5RtQCVsA, 1UhvBSQFy6A, JE2_7elAcxM)
6. **Great salespeople are selected, not made.** Recruit proven closers, judge them fast and cut the bottom. "Whenever you cut the bottom, the top moves up." (_33XVdvO4Gc, bgmh7p1s7zs, 2lA_A8BGRRs)
7. **Sell at the point of greatest pain.** Upsell at greatest need, not at satisfaction. (sBJppqCeFGI, 5MjjpB8SPMo, 8VqSFBMMS4M)
8. **Tonality is trainable.** Be loud, articulate, 135–185 wpm, then ask and stay silent. (sGakuNs9mT4)

## 5. Retention & LTV

1. **Hypothetical max = new customers per period ÷ churn rate.** Growth only comes from more inflow, higher price or lower churn. (XZEjp2mP1U0, f_ceDZqhKbw, XC_lklN9KmE)
2. **Churn falls with tenure, so win month one.** Fast time-to-first-value and choreographed onboarding are the levers. (-j8_YCWZ05Q, yPDQCfrwh8E, QTZsh3BgOwY, afbP6sB_Atc)
3. **Fix churn by choosing customers who don't fail.** Profile churned vs best customers and qualify the front end. (hnfh2jqNVEQ, thDTmy7VGIw, CoPs-Bk8M9Y)
4. **Six LTV levers: raise price, cut COGS, increase frequency, upsell, cross-sell, downsell. Price is the biggest.** (Q2VUuUGfpNY, MwhsM7qijy8)
5. **Fix through subtraction.** "Doing less can often retain more." (-j8_YCWZ05Q, -JLN1d1ZKUU)
6. **Sell things people never stop buying.** Recurring revenue is a continuum (the 8 C's). (3P1XjUvo1b4, jOM0m34dxz4, K8MFC9t7snY)
7. **A refund is an apology. Own it fast and over-recover.** (p39nLmVtOjc)
8. **Capture testimonials and asks at the moment of the result.** Perceived value decays. (7wN8n3qrqnQ)

## 6. People & management

1. **Underperformance is one of three boxes: not communicated, not trained, not motivated.** Diagnose in that order and attack the process, not the person. (IMowPVgcWbA, aUnorufFIq8, s6tkRztZwYc)
2. **Hire in reverse order of value to buy back your time.** Start with admin and support, then sales, then leadership. (7NqsFh5ej0w, awOQDy_o9ww, gD0X-PLax5I, m-k0_pQJ1fY)
3. **Pay for top talent and value people by the "disappear tomorrow" test.** Only change comp to fix an attraction or retention problem. (cemduJKQl5w, KhFlD54nQrY, -UzJOk85OZI)
4. **Core values are a few non-negotiables that repel.** Hire and fire on them. (kW2vDMAmlPI)
5. **Lead with reward before punishment. Delegate to competent people instead of SOP-ing everything.** (oK2_u-uS_Bo)
6. **(Leila Hormozi) Fix management fundamentals before adding headcount or channels.** Accountability = expectation × measurement + feedback. (2JLU4R8Zcgg, ZklXJzHzW2U)
7. **Put three superstars per business (acquisition, delivery, ops) on profit share.** (_x6n-CmZqjo)

## 7. Ops & scaling

1. **Triage first: are you supply-constrained or demand-constrained?** Supply → raise price, then add tech, training and headcount. Demand → fix the channel, then the offer. (sGv2BTUCcCM, OQf2Ba-Lp_4, jqo0lVveh98, N5MExtki_VI)
2. **Every change costs about 20% in performance, so only change for ≥20–40% upside.** Make about one big bet a year. (hfwZwPGsbIo, XwzU4RikbGs, uYds0zcAFWM, 0lMn_-EXyhQ)
3. **Never add locations or franchise on weak unit economics.** Make one unit wildly profitable without you, then multiply. (_8olk4-VFP8, O1As2zxy0es)
4. **Stage-appropriate focus.** $0–1M: one product, one channel. $1–10M: a back-end ladder to lift LTV. $10M+: professionalize. (R7Fin9HFhoM, cnbHgYYHzyE, F84olnKkseM)
5. **Do the unscalable things early, then step down the touch ratio.** (H_ZLs1-jOKQ)
6. **Think in workflows, not roles.** Break jobs into tasks and automate them one by one. (9q5ojtkqsBs, JOY6ZzBMb_4)
7. **Protect maker time.** A meeting costs a maker about 10% of their week. (GIRkQQHzsxI)

## 8. Money & cash flow

1. **Get customers to finance acquisition.** 30-day gross profit should cover CAC + COGS, and ideally ≥2× it. (XwZH-lOKG9c, k-3PoOT4vOM, nSQdjim8CsE, GKvAQIe5vpo)
2. **Pull cash forward.** Use prepay discounts, annual billing and paid-in-full offers. "If you want to make more money, ask to get paid sooner." (k-3PoOT4vOM, 41EvCgwPrDc, EZLQzR-_83A)
3. **Need cash fast? Sell to existing customers first,** matching the play to how fast you need it. (SvIcS-Q1Hl4, OpeN4O5myIg, xRxVpjeHmeA)
4. **Track net free cash flow, set a cash floor and sweep the excess.** Reinvest only where returns beat the index. (EPEjYEihZ1s, CRMT2yubBxc)
5. **Invest in skills before markets.** Measure return per hour. (6KqndZuN_Yk, Nh8Oc7ERdIU, oZ-H_TjSzok, vhOV_Od0A3M)
6. **Leverage is not needing the deal.** Protect principal. "FOMO means slow go." (rp1PzCxj3eU, INm4U2S7Vu8, BnvkMpUu-7c)

## 9. Deals, equity & acquisitions

1. **Enterprise value = profit × multiple, and the multiple is risk.** Flip founder, channel and customer concentration into strengths. (fD-sxKiB30M, HKbFUWJwEG0, sjt5G3YPjmY, pWbSl7d0tEc)
2. **Structure the business as if you'll sell it, whether or not you will.** (V7oUcAfof34, sEQNnsaeiAs, s3tH9Sz1fEc)
3. **Only partner for skills, money or time you lack.** Prefer revenue or profit share to equity. (DAm0EOes5to, yJfn_pWzFqg, eX3Ch_HuR70)
4. **Own, don't earn, your way to wealth.** Sell only if reinvested proceeds plus the next venture beat the stake you'd keep. (VBoRLJimVzc, _gcqwupsza8, QQGHCG8d1So, 6x3re5-Ms1E)
5. **Buying a cash-flowing business from a motivated seller can beat starting one.** Agree on price first, then terms. (KNl83INePXI, bgBIO6nZawg)
   - The figures in KNl83INePXI don't fully add up in the captions.
6. **Build your BATNA before the table, anchor high and trade on non-price variables.** (Jc2UW3nlNBA)

## 10. Choosing a business & mindset

1. **Pick a market with pain, purchasing power, easy targeting and growth.** Established demand removes idea risk. (qxQIcDrre1E, A9qHKjFPJ-E, AN2KpRBsmRY, aOq1Hdz0w3k)
2. **The ideal business is unique, expensive, sticky and "air"** (near-zero cost of goods). Retention matters most. (VaE4pcf9PI8, 3fsJFUvA6Ts, Lc8DNduiwKA, -NLqtk4F4oY)
3. **Find the business you're really in.** Gyms are sales, supplements are brand, software is product, services are talent. (07jC6ooRIHw, q9OmYf_IlQ0, qsXxckCbci0)
4. **If others win in your vehicle, you're not good yet. Do the reps for years.** (Tw6kJkVcTL0, VgEvIIpDYG8, kQFSiEDvXws)
5. **Set goals on controllable inputs and commit to one path.** (0_Gf5v8DEMY, -UzJOk85OZI, RdAKXJlMIZM)
6. **Solve problems (sure money) before chasing opportunities (hypothetical money).** (zNiXk_3C_Io, 5Kt-EYieNko)
7. **Plateau doesn't mean start a new business.** Boredom-driven change breaks working companies. (ug5N9qmFVKQ, s59jbVBprn0, yflKMUffctE)
8. **Hard decisions are hard because the easy ones are done. Spell out the trade and pick.** (fcyIoN8CUOk)

[inference] The master pattern across the corpus: diagnose with math (constraint → unit economics → leak), fix offer and price before volume, then scale volume on what already works, while removing yourself and concentration risk.
